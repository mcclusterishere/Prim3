# McCluster Corp Season One quiet-luxury capsule — candidate v01

## Pieces

4. Solid-black slim-straight professional trousers

## Branding hierarchy

### Shirts

- One small ivory-outline M at the left chest
- Optional tonal-black Dual Sight on a sleeve or cuff
- One tiny red `//` at a cuff, collar tip or sleeve hem
- Full monogram permitted only inside the collar stand, lining or back yoke

### Trousers

- Nearly plain black from normal viewing distance
- Sharp pressed crease and professional office cut
- One tiny tonal black-on-black M near a pocket
- One tiny red `//` bartack at a belt loop or pocket edge
- No all-over pattern and no large visible motifs

### Shorts

- Nearly plain tailored black
- One tonal Dual Sight near the lower hem
- One tiny red `//` bartack
- No repeated exterior pattern

## Construction concept

- Cotton-silk poplin shirting
- Tropical wool/silk tailoring
- Black mother-of-pearl buttons
- Ivory and red silk thread for restrained visible marks
- Glossy black thread for tonal identity
- Luxury expressed through cut, hand, drape, hardware and finishing
