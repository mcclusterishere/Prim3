# McCluster Corp Season One private-client capsule — candidate v03

## Owner direction locked in this candidate

- This is wealth, not riches.
- It should feel like the luxury collection that never reaches a public rack.
- Silhouette, fabric, construction, and fit carry the value.
- Identity is exact, sparse, and controlled.
- The trousers and shorts remain visually professional and nearly plain.
- This is not merch, Supreme/drop culture, a loud matching set, a tracksuit, or a monogram costume.
- Presentation uses a faceless black atelier mannequin; no human likeness is part of the brand or story canon.

## Pieces

1. Solid-black long-sleeve tailored button-up
2. Solid-black short-sleeve camp-collar button-up
3. Solid-black tailored city shorts
4. Solid-black slim-straight professional trousers

## Branding hierarchy

### Shirts

- One small exact M at the left chest
- Optional exact tonal-black Dual Sight on a sleeve or cuff
- One tiny exact red `//` at a cuff, collar tip, or sleeve hem
- Full monogram permitted only inside the collar stand, lining, or back yoke

### Trousers

- Nearly plain black from normal viewing distance
- Sharp pressed crease and professional office cut
- One tiny exact tonal black-on-black M near a pocket, if used
- One tiny exact red `//` bartack at a belt loop or pocket edge
- No all-over pattern and no large visible motifs

### Shorts

- Nearly plain tailored black
- One exact tonal Dual Sight near the lower hem, if used
- One tiny exact red `//` bartack
- No repeated exterior pattern

## Construction concept

- Cotton-silk poplin shirting
- Tropical wool/silk tailoring
- Black mother-of-pearl buttons
- Ivory and red silk thread for restrained visible marks
- Glossy black thread for tonal identity
- Luxury expressed through cut, hand, drape, hardware, and finishing

## Identity-production note

The visual establishes wardrobe direction. Its exact detail-row marks were composited from the locked geometry, but the PNG is still not manufacturing artwork. Garment artwork and manufacturing files must place the locked SVG masters from `assets/canon/brands/mccluster-corp/marks/` as separate layers. If an exact mark cannot be placed at the intended scale, omit it.
