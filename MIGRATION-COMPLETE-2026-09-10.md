# 3. Final lifecycle law

Support/main status is dynamic.

Support characters can become mains through repeated usefulness, relationships, responsibility, vacancies and audience familiarity.

Mains may drift, transfer, lose authority, become injured/disabled, resign, become antagonistic or die.

> **If you die, the building changes.**

Death creates organizational as well as emotional residue.

There is no automatic one-for-one replacement.

> **A replacement inherits the vacancy, not the dead person's personality.**

Casualty-state system:

```text
STORY-LOCKED
PROTECTED-UNTIL
CAMPAIGN-VARIABLE
```
