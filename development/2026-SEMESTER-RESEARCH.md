## 2. Verified 2026 event bank

All five items below occurred before the research cutoff. None establishes that PRIM caused, prevented or secretly controlled a real event.

### A. January 17: BBNJ / High Seas Treaty entered into force

**Verified fact:** The BBNJ Agreement entered into force January 17, 2026. This is confirmed by the UN Treaty Collection and the agreement's official information page. It is not a claim that all protective measures were already implemented everywhere.

**Optional use:** A dated public-source item in Open Book's research collection, or a character conversation about governance of shared resources. Do not imply that this treaty itself authorizes a cyber raid, physical access, or equipment seizure. A maritime mission would need separate fictional authorization and a stronger story reason.

Sources: [UN Treaty Collection](https://treaties-prod.un.org/Pages/ViewDetails.aspx?chapter=21&clang=_en&mtdsg_no=XXI-10&src=TREATY), [official BBNJ page](https://www.un.org/bbnjagreement/en). The latter's direct fetch returned 403; its indexed official text was available. The Treaty Collection independently confirms the date. A separate UN delegate summary contains a 2025 typo and was not used to establish the date.

### B. January 23: CISA published PQC product-category guidance

**Verified fact:** CISA's dated official bulletin announced product categories for technologies using post-quantum cryptography standards. The bulletin includes cloud services, web software and endpoint security among examples.

**Optional use:** A believable dated briefing reference or equipment-inventory background item. Teach that knowing the category of a device does not establish its configuration, authorization or safety. This supports Permission Slip without replacing its existing mission.

Source: [CISA official January 23 bulletin](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCISA/bulletins/405c246).

### C. February 6–22: Milano Cortina Winter Olympics

**Verified fact:** The Games' official date range was February 6–22, and the Italian Olympic National Committee published its closing assessment on February 22. This is supported by post-event material, not solely an advance schedule.

**Optional use:** Recurring ensemble life: an old group photograph, an argument about a remembered result, or a personal connection to an event someone watched. No need to invent Olympic cyber incidents, imply access to Olympic networks, or make real athletes part of the conspiracy. Broadcast footage and branding require separate rights review if production uses them.

Sources: [IOC date information](https://support.olympics.com/hc/en-gb/articles/43002165477267-Where-and-when-will-the-2026-Olympic-Winter-Games-take-place), [CONI closing assessment](https://milanocortina2026.coni.it/en/news/milano-cortina-2026-enters-history-president-luciano-buonfiglio-an-olympics-worthy-of-top-marks.html).

### D. August 12: total solar eclipse

**Verified fact:** The total solar eclipse occurred on August 12, 2026. ESA's retrospective describes ground observations and spacecraft imagery, including the Moon's shadow observed from space.

**Recommended light-touch use:** At fall opening, an image or conversation from the summer gives the ensemble shared history before McCluster arrived. In an OSINT exercise, use source date, location and provenance rather than treating an astronomical image as automatic proof of a person's presence. Do not show totality at an unspecified campus location: the path must match geography. The eclipse is not a cause of supernatural access or hacking.

Sources: [ESA retrospective](https://www.esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/A_look_back_at_the_2026_total_solar_eclipse), [NASA imagery collection](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/15075/).

### E. October 27: NIST finalized IR 8547 transition guidance

**Verified fact:** NIST's publication history dates the final *Transition to Post-Quantum Cryptography Standards* report to October 27, 2026. It addresses transition from quantum-vulnerable algorithms to post-quantum signatures and key-establishment schemes.

**Optional late-semester use:** A real document in the background of a new assignment or governance discussion. It cannot appear as a final report in an early-October midterm scene. Do not claim quantum computers have broken everything, that migration is instantaneous, or that the first PQC standards were created in 2026; the first finalized standards predate this report.

Source: [NIST IR 8547 publication record](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ir/8547/final).
