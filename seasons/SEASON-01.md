# Season One — Recognition

> Status remains **PROPOSED**. Rewritten 2026-11-02 to agree with root `CANON.md` after the owner decision to remove the "internship that is secretly an entrance exam" version. What was removed is listed at the end of this file.

## Season promise

A grounded adult workplace-and-college drama. A 33-year-old first-year student joins **Equity Uprise**, a real institution with a real educational and community mission, and finds a living ensemble whose careers, rivalries and histories were already moving before he arrived (`CANON.md` §2, §19; `episodes/S01E03-OSINT.md` "Season-one handoff").

The season must work before the audience understands the PRIM2 mythology. It is the season of **Recognition** (`story/EPISODE-PROGRESSION.md`): the audience learns who these people are, and PRIM2's system quietly recognizes McCluster again.

## Cold open

In the future, McCluster's PRIM-side assault element and Royce's Hitman-side
assault element have penetrated Site 0. PRIM2 performs **High Alert** as a diss
record and psychological weapon against the people who have come to replace him
(`CANON.md` §16).

Seventeen compressed memory flashes point toward global Episodes 02-18. The
opening cuts away before the RAID outcome, the survivor state or the identity
of the second assault element is explained.

Cut to seven years earlier: a 33-year-old first-year college student arrives on campus with no money, no stable life and years of knowledge nobody has formally recognized.

## Emotional spine

The protagonist believes he is returning to school to catch up.

The truth is that his unusual combination of experience, deprivation and persistence makes him legible to a system that already knew him once, long-haired and under another life (`CANON.md` §10: S1E1 is a **reacquisition**, not a first discovery).

## Season movements

### 1. Re-entry (E01)

- First day of college at 33; financial and housing instability remain immediate
- Younger classmates initially mistake age for authority or failure
- Coursework exposes the difference between practical knowledge and formal theory
- Equity Uprise appears to offer legitimacy, and mostly delivers it
- Site 0's identity update reacquires him (`The Blip`)

### 2. Onboarding (E02)

- Equity Uprise presents as prestigious, serious and legitimate, and its public mission is real
- Work begins with believable IT and infrastructure responsibilities
- The onboarding kit includes high-end Dual Sight pieces; an unmanifested device inside it is the human trigger
- For a protagonist entering from poverty and homelessness, the clothing initially feels like dignity, access and proof that he belongs
- THE CALL interrupts onboarding: a lawful task-force operation at Harborline Edge Exchange, owned by Nusrat and Miguel, with McCluster locked out of PLAY

### 3. The world was already moving (E03)

- Relationships form through classes, labs, shifts and financial pressure
- Oksana and Rafael own a case that has nothing to do with him
- Competition exists, but cooperation is necessary
- Nobody is treated as disposable
- PRIM2 remains unseen; his traces are institutional gravity, not a villain's hand

## First death

There is **no** death of a developed recurring character in Season One. Canon places the first recurring-character death slot in E06 (S2E3, *Got Wifi / Dead Air*), victim not yet chosen (`episodes/README.md` "Episode 06 casualty rule").

## Season One wardrobe and brand presence

- McCluster Corp has the visual discipline of an original luxury house, not ordinary promotional merchandise.
- The approved Dual Sight monogram appears on employee outerwear, knitwear, shirts, accessories, bags and interior surfaces as appropriate; the exact initial item list remains a wardrobe-design decision.
- The black, ivory and signal-red pattern should appear repeatedly enough that the audience associates it with aspiration before associating it with control.
- All wardrobe generations must use the supplied seamless master. Do not imitate Gucci, Louis Vuitton or another real house's protected pattern.
- The protagonist's relationship to the swag should evolve: first it is evidence that he has finally arrived; later it becomes the uniform of an architecture he cannot easily leave.
- Do not place an Apex logo on McCluster Corp or Equity Uprise clothing.

## Season-ending note

Season One ends on the ensemble, not on a conspiracy reveal: "The world did not begin when McCluster walked in." PRIM2's E02 "ROOT VIEW" coda already shows the audience that someone above the visible institution is grading people; McCluster does not learn PRIM2's identity or the succession law.

## Season One technology education

Teach through immediate character problems:

- Computer hardware and server anatomy
- Operating-system fundamentals
- Command line and scripting
- Networking models, addressing and routing
- Data-center power, cooling and redundancy
- Monitoring, alerts and incident response
- Identity, permissions and least privilege
- Backups versus resilience
- Documentation and chain of custody
- The human cost of technical decisions

## Tone rules

- College life and poverty remain materially present.
- Technical concepts solve or create dramatic problems.
- Exposition must emerge from conflict, training, failure or investigation.
- The first death must matter beyond shock value.
- PRIM2 should be felt as an intelligence in the architecture before he is seen as a man.
