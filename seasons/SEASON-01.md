# Season One — Recruitment

## Season promise

A grounded adult college drama gradually reveals that the protagonist's technology internship is an entrance examination for a hidden global organization.

The season must work before the audience understands the full Prime mythology.

## Cold open

In the future, Agent Prime Negative One seals the completed helmet inside Site 0 while a recruit he selected raids the facility.

He understands the attack because he once made the same journey.

Cut to seven years earlier: a 33-year-old first-year college student arrives on campus with no money, no stable life and years of knowledge nobody has formally recognized.

## Emotional spine

The protagonist believes he is returning to school to catch up.

The truth is that his unusual combination of experience, deprivation and persistence has placed him ahead of a process he cannot yet see.

## Season movements

### 1. Re-entry

- First day of college at 33
- Financial and housing instability remain immediate
- Younger classmates initially mistake age for authority or failure
- Coursework exposes the difference between practical knowledge and formal theory
- A corporate internship appears to offer legitimacy

### 2. The internship

- The corporation presents as serious but ordinary
- Work begins with believable IT and infrastructure responsibilities
- The protagonist notices permissions, facilities and reporting paths that do not match the stated business
- Members of the same intake display unusually diverse technical and operational ability

### 3. The first cohort

- Relationships form through classes, labs, shifts and financial pressure
- Nobody is treated as disposable
- Competition exists, but cooperation is necessary
- A returning member of an older cohort carries visible mission damage and refuses to explain it
- The presiding Prime remains unseen

### 4. The field threshold

- A technical incident pulls interns into a real operational problem
- The protagonist's experience becomes more valuable than his academic standing
- The team is given instructions that appear complete but contain a dangerous assumption
- Survival requires questioning the assignment, not merely performing it

### 5. The first absence

- The cohort returns changed
- At least one developed character does not return
- The corporation frames the loss in sterile procedural language
- The protagonist recognizes that management anticipated the fatality
- He chooses to remain

## Season-ending revelation

The protagonist discovers evidence that his recruitment was not initiated by the visible internship team.

Someone at the highest level selected him before he applied.

He does not yet learn Prime's identity or the succession law.

## Season One technology education

Teach through immediate character problems:

- Computer hardware and server anatomy
- Operating-system fundamentals
- Command line and scripting
- Networking models, addressing and routing
- Data-center power, cooling and redundancy
- Monitoring, alerts and incident response
- Identity, permissions and least privilege
- Backups versus resilience
- Documentation and chain of custody
- The human cost of technical decisions

## Tone rules

- College life and poverty remain materially present.
- Technical concepts solve or create dramatic problems.
- Exposition must emerge from conflict, training, failure or investigation.
- The first death must matter beyond shock value.
- Prime should be felt as an intelligence in the architecture before he is seen as a man.
