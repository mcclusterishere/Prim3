# What the episode is

`Evil Twin` begins **after Jordan Vale and Royce have already defeated PRIM2 physically during RAID**.

Jordan is the sole surviving PRIM-side attacker.
Royce is the sole surviving Hitman-side attacker.
PRIM2 is critically injured, physically defeated and alive in Site 0's suspension/life-support architecture.

Jordan and Royce initially believe they may be able to finish the conflict by killing him.

PRIM2 reveals that his living identity remains entangled with the deepest trust root of the architecture and that immediate death could leave authority unresolved.

The episode therefore becomes a **confession under threat plus adversarial verification mission**.

---

# Human thesis

PRIM2's last weapon is not force.

It is the fact that he knows more about the board than the two people who beat him.

Jordan and Royce do not have to trust him.
They have to determine which claims are true **before deciding whether killing him is safe**.

The dramatic question is:

> **Can you use information from a source you despise without surrendering judgment to the source?**

---

# Technical thesis

The Evil Twin lesson is about:
- identity;
- provenance;
- common roots;
- duplicated/mirrored systems;
- hidden shared authority;
- conflicting evidence;
- authentication versus appearance;
- corroboration;
- independent validation.

Core model:

```text
LOOKS THE SAME
≠
IS THE SAME

LOOKS SEPARATE
≠
IS INDEPENDENT
```

The player must follow provenance and authority relationships rather than trusting presentation.

# Playable identities

### Jordan
Best at:
- dependency reasoning;
- technical provenance;
- alternate explanations;
- cross-system inference;
- verification discipline.

### Royce
Best at:
- Hitman institutional context;
- recognizing status/obligation relationships;
- identifying claims that contradict how the organization actually behaved;
- pressuring evasive human testimony.

### PRIM2
Not player-controlled.

He is:
- information source;
- antagonist;
- bargaining party;
- unreliable narrator;
- holder of some facts unavailable elsewhere.

The mission works because **none of the three has the complete truth alone**.

---

# Required reveal

The mission must establish with sufficient confidence that:

> **PRIM2's still-living identity is required for the cleanest available succession/revocation path.**

The player does not need to verify every historical confession.

Some claims may remain uncertain deliberately, preserving future Whip Equipped / Hitman prequel reveals.

The reverse-reveal law still applies:

> **Background truth is not disclosure permission.**

---

# Dual-board reveal

PRIM2 can prove enough to establish that visible PRIM operations and Hitman operations were compartmentalized under his wider cross-board visibility.

That does **not** mean:
- he personally scripted every human choice;
- every coincidence was manufactured;
- every bad event was his doing;
- every character existed to serve Jordan's succession.

The stronger reveal is narrower:

> **PRIM2 repeatedly possessed context that people on either board did not know existed.**

He could exploit asymmetry through permission, omission, timing, resource allocation and selective disclosure.

# Safety / scope

Do not reintroduce the superseded procedural infiltration material.

The episode may discuss identity abuse, impersonation, social manipulation and compromised trust at a conceptual/story level, but it should not teach actionable credential theft, SIM hijacking, unauthorized system access or evasion.
