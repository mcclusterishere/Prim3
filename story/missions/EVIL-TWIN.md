# Mission / Episode: Evil Twin

## What it is
Prime has been **caught** (or cornered). He explains **why** he runs missions through both institutions — Prim3-side and **Hitman** — by reconstructing the **origin op** that first embedded him in Hitman.

This is a **season-end** episode: confession + playable/watchable reconstruction, not a random mid-arc flex.

## Thesis
Prime is deeply embedded across systems (including government-facing channels) to steer outcomes on the back end for people who never see the board. Dual control is doctrine. Along the path he surfaces elite rot (trafficking, political money, sealed files — **Epstein-class discoveries as the type of truth**, not gore tourism).

## Two-stage infiltration (the actual mission)

### Stage 1 — Low door
- **Target:** Hitman **lower executive** (below Sponsor)
- **Method:** Planned social engineering
  - Operative (“girl”) bumps target → phone drop
  - **Kiosk SIM swap** (story set **pre-eSIM** so the beat ages)
  - Social / device control
- **Result:** Low privilege inside Hitman; map crumbs; path toward Sponsor layer

### Stage 2 — High door
- **Target:** Hitman **Mission Sponsor** (politics + funds; mirror of Prim3 Authority-03)
- **Method:** Second asset + **evil twin** (jewelry / wrist device + operative dual meaning from lyrics); date/social approach; **Harper-gymnast**-style placement from the bars
- **Result:** Sponsor-level leverage; dual-board control begins
