# HIGH ALERT — THE FUTURE INSIDE THE PILOT

## What it is

`High Alert` is shown in Global Episode 01 / S1E1, but the music-film event occurs chronologically during the final `RAID (HOT SITE)`.

It is **not** an ordinary Season One tutorial operation.

The performer is **PRIM2**, the presiding Mantle holder.

Jordan Vale and Royce are already attacking Site 0 from separate PRIM-side and Hitman-side assault paths.

PRIM2 genuinely resists them.

The song is simultaneously:
- a diss record aimed at the challengers;
- a future battle fragment;
- a seventeen-memory mystery trailer;
- a map of consequences from Episodes 02–18;
- a warning that the person at the top knows more than the audience can explain yet.

---

# Episode-opening function

The audience initially receives an impossible amount of information without the dictionary required to understand it.

They see:
- PRIM2 under High Alert state;
- Jordan much later in his life than the college story that follows;
- Royce as a dangerous parallel challenger whose full relationship to Jordan is withheld;
- Site 0;
- flashes from seventeen events that have not yet been experienced.

Then the sequence cuts away before the RAID resolves.

The story rewinds:

> **SEVEN YEARS EARLIER.**

Jordan is 33 and entering college.

The contrast is the hook.

---

# Memory law

There are **17 core memory anchors**, corresponding to global Episodes 02–18.

Each memory should be approximately 1–4 seconds in the S1E1 presentation.

The audience should not be able to fully explain the memory on first watch.

When the corresponding episode later arrives, the audience learns the human context.

Critically, the flash shown in `High Alert` does **not** prove Jordan owns the later episode.

A memory may feature Jordan because PRIM2 is talking to him while the full episode actually belongs to Aya, Chioma, Marc, Amira, Jonas, Oksana, another ensemble character, or a character cluster.

Canonical rule:

> **The memory belongs to PRIM2's argument. The episode belongs to whoever changed because of it.**

See `story/HIGH-ALERT-MEMORY-ANCHORS.md`.

# Gameplay relationship

The S1E1 future fragment may be WATCH-only or use a tightly bounded playable teaser.

It must **not** teach the player that Jordan is the default campaign pawn.

If PLAY is used in the future fragment:
- it is a scripted endgame glimpse;
- Jordan's presence consumes no Wildcard Authorization;
- Royce is a scripted hostile/parallel principal rather than a random incursion;
- PRIM2 is the active defender;
- the interface may intentionally withhold mechanics/context that will only become understandable later.

After the rewind, ordinary campaign PLAY follows the ensemble/wildcard law in `game/WILDCARD-INTERVENTION-AUTHORITY.md`.

---

# PRIM2's performance

PRIM2 is not performing because he likes spectacle more than survival.

The music film externalizes his psychological and information advantage during an active crisis.

He knows enough about Jordan, Royce and the prior seven years to weaponize memories whose meanings are intimate, institutional or operational.

He does **not** have to have caused each event.

This distinction is mandatory:

> **Knowing the board is not the same as authoring every move on it.**

---

# High Alert state

`HIGH ALERT` is also a literal Site 0 escalation condition.

The final RAID has crossed the threshold at which PRIM2's distributed oversight and normal defensive architecture are no longer sufficient without direct, escalating response.

The repetition of the hook can visually show the state propagating deeper through Site 0.

Keep all facility defense systems fictional/non-actionable.

---

# Pilot handoff

The sequence ends without answering:
- who PRIM2 is;
- why Jordan is attacking him;
- who Royce is;
- why Jordan and Royce are on different sides;
- what PRIM means;
- why the memories matter;
- whether anybody survives.

The rewind then begins the ensemble world whose accumulated lives eventually make that opening intelligible.

---

# Governing line

> **Episode One shows the end before the audience has met the people whose lives make the end matter.**
