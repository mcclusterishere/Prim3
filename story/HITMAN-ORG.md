# Hitman — structure

Hitman is a relationship-heavy kinetic/street-corporate organization, not an IT org with different stickers.

Its exact internal titles may vary by era/region, but the recurring organizational functions include:

| Function | Story job |
|---|---|
| Sovereign / succession seat | highest internal authority; held by Royce in the contemporary PRIM3-era frame |
| Operations / money | keeps obligations, resources and work moving |
| Sponsor / client gate | translates outside demand, money and political/social pressure into work |
| Crew / cell leadership | commands small operational groups |
| Access / social gate | controls introductions, spaces, relationships and proximity |
| Logistics / equipment | keeps transport, inventory and material support available |
| Technical support | solves device/infrastructure problems appropriate to character skill without turning the org into PRIM |
| Overwatch / picture | watches environments, movement and changing risk |
| Active operators | carry field work |
| New blood / trainees | people still proving fit, loyalty and judgment |

The organizational mirror to PRIM is **functional**, not cultural.

Both systems need authority, information, logistics, access, people and money, but they solve those necessities differently.

## Contemporary apex

Jordan/Benji is historical to Hitman by the time PRIM3 begins.

Current succession line relevant to the show:

```text
BIGGA LEX THE JET
    -> offers succession to JORDAN / BENJI
JORDAN REFUSES
    -> ROYCE receives the succession opportunity
```

Royce is therefore the current Hitman-side sovereign/rival figure.
