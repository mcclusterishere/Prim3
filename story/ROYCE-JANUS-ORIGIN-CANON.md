# The Royce Protocol — JANUS Origin

This is the continuity-safe proposed addendum from the owner's October 19, 2026 direction. It is **fictional thriller narrative**, not a claim about any real government's conduct, genetic program, real individual, school, or family. "JANUS" is a working in-world codename, not an owner-approved final title. This addendum becomes governing canon only when merged through the PRIM3 source-of-truth workflow.

## One-sentence premise

The man McCluster knows as his dangerously ambitious younger Hitman rival, Royce, is an engineered, light-skinned/white-presenting biological **derivative of McCluster** created from archived early-life tissue by a covert, government-linked continuity program. The operative knows he has been shaped for a classified purpose; he does **not** know his donor is the man who relinquished the Hitman chair to him.

## Hard continuity locks — do not change

- **Benji = McCluster**, age approximately 19–26; Benji is not another actor, brother or clone.
- **Ghost / Whip Equipped = McCluster**, age approximately 26–33.
- **McCluster returns to college at 33** and enters Equity Uprise; he does not know he is on a PRIM succession path. PRIM3 follows ages 33–40.
- **Royce is exactly seven years younger** in the established narrative: about 19 at McCluster's age-26 Hitman refusal, about 26 at PRIM3 opening, about 33 at the final year.
- **Bigga Lex the Jet** is McCluster's childhood friend and Hitman entry bridge. Lex retires as his children arrive, offers McCluster/Benji the succession, and McCluster refuses. Royce subsequently receives the opportunity. Lex's choices and family loyalty remain real, not secretly fake orders.
- **PRIM2 did not first discover McCluster in childhood** under the existing authority. His *active interest* begins during the Ghost/Whip years after he traces the Hitman succession backward through Royce → Lex → Benji. Preexisting JANUS archives belonged to an older compartmentalized program, not retroactively to PRIM2's personally authored childhood surveillance.
- **Equity Uprise HQ** is the recurring collegiate/workplace setting; **Site 0 / Root HQ** is the hidden succession facility. The two must never merge.
- **Seven seasons × three episodes = 21; one song = one episode = one mission.** This twist adds threads inside existing episodes/prequels, not a 22nd episode. Global Episode 18 remains an unfilled owner-song slot.
- Endgame remains **RAID → Evil Twin → Ghost in the Wires**: McCluster and Royce survive their separate teams, physically defeat PRIM2 together, verify the living-root dependency and complete succession to McCluster as PRIM3. Royce remains a living independent counterweight, not silently absorbed by PRIM.
- McCluster's inheritance is **earned and governance-tested**, not awarded automatically because of ancestry, genetics or race. The lineages are targets of a flawed, dangerous institutional hypothesis, not proof of a superior population.

## Origin: the file existed before the man

1. McCluster's birth creates a match in a hidden historical lineage registry. The index claims to identify candidate people with unusual compatibility for an experimental human-machine command/identity interface. The registry also includes other family lines; it is not a list of one chosen family. The research's actual validity is contested.
2. The program acquires and archives a biological sample when McCluster is an infant. It does **not** create a fully formed adult Royce at McCluster's birth. Its index includes dated specimens and administrative records, but the full chain of custody is withheld from both protagonists.
3. When McCluster is approximately seven, the program begins a second gestation from his biological source and deliberately edits the derivative's externally visible phenotype, including pigmentation, so that he will present as white and will not be visually recognized as a duplicate. A strictly identical DNA clone would not explain the difference: Royce is an *edited, engineered derivative*, "clone" being the characters' shorthand.
4. **Royce is born seven years after McCluster.** He receives a real upbringing and experiences the passage of time normally. There is no accelerated aging, simultaneous adult body or time-travel contradiction.
5. The legacy program raises and trains Royce through proxies, contractors and apparently ordinary social institutions. It gives him real attachment, music, neighborhoods, fear and ambition; the engineering does **not** fully determine his personality or morality.
6. The program's purpose is not merely a spy. It wants a potential *alternate human root candidate* it can influence if the original refuses enrollment or control. This contingency is the reason to engineer an appearance-independent but provenance-linked counterpart. Whether Royce could ever be validated as a Mantle holder is unproven and becomes a core Evil Twin question.

**Who ordered it?** A government-linked legacy procurement/continuity directorate, with JANUS as a working project codename. **Which specific person, agency and PRIM0/PRIM1-era sponsor signed the order remains OPEN.** Do not casually claim PRIM2 personally commissioned Royce's birth; do not assign PRIM0/PRIM1 culpability before an independent reveal. PRIM2 later learns, obtains and weaponizes fragments of the archival arrangement.

## Childhood and school timeline — an interpretable mystery

McCluster's fictional parents repeatedly attempt to preserve his privacy and keep him outside ordinary formal school enrollment during early childhood. Their choices arise from anomalous attention and conflicting institutional paperwork; do not make them omniscient about biological engineering. When McCluster enters formal high school, recruitment, testing or special technology programs create records the hidden legacy network can correlate. Those high-school programs are a **later surveillance and recontact opportunity**, not the event that creates the already seven-year-younger Royce.

The public institution may have legitimate educational aims and innocent staff. A program's existence alone is not evidence that every teacher, counselor or classmate participated. Show the protagonist's limited information, the parents' plausible uncertainty, and independently verified documents before assigning intentional wrongdoing.

## The Hitman collision

At McCluster 19 / Royce 12, McCluster's first college/dean events occur exactly as existing canon records. Do not put Royce into that dorm story or turn Ricky into Royce.

By McCluster's mid-twenties, Royce has arrived in Hitman's orbit as an extremely young, charismatic, volatile and unnervingly disciplined rival. He performs street-cultural confidence with authentic relationships, but certain habits—clean situational recall, unusual tasking, precise debrief language and access he cannot fully explain—feel unlike anyone around him.

**Brandon** is an owner-introduced, **historical Hitman-era** figure whose story job is an early warning, not an automatic 40th immediate-era roster slot. Brandon believes the scene around Royce is feeling "federal" and tells McCluster there are patterns suggesting somebody above the crew is tracking them. He sees operational irregularities, not lab evidence. His exact last name, role and real-world inspiration remain unassigned. Do not write allegations about a real person as verified fact.

Brandon's warning deepens McCluster's preexisting mistrust but does not establish proof of genetic kinship. McCluster starts watching Royce more closely, worries Royce might kill him for the chair, and still chooses to preserve his loyalty to Lex and Lex's family rather than assume control of an organization he no longer believes in.

When Lex retires, **McCluster refuses**. He does not formally appoint a secret federal handler; by refusing and not stopping the institution's succession process, he **allows Royce's ascent**. That is a morally consequential decision which never becomes retroactive approval of Royce's unknown origin. Royce accepts a chair he thinks he earned, carries independent agency and mistakes McCluster's refusal for contempt. This wound motivates his Hitman-side sovereignty long after the legacy program's original assignments.

## Royce as a person, not a racial caricature

Royce's contrast is built on **institutional conditioning versus personal choice**, not on the claim that one race is naturally more criminal or more civilized. He is a ferociously assertive white Hitman figure with an extreme streetwise stage presence and dangerous humor, but his volatility is counterweighted by intelligence, genuine loyalty, fear of disposability and competence in running a real organization. He must have ordinary Tuesdays, relationships, failures and episodes that McCluster does not appear in. He can love someone without faking it; he can disobey his makers; he can choose restraint when it costs him status.

He regards being McCluster's "leftovers" as intolerable. Learning that his body was derived from McCluster threatens a deeper belief: **was any accomplishment ever his own?** The truthful answer must be yes. Biology and manipulation created the conditions, but Royce made costly choices the program did not foresee.

The first watch should read like a tense institutional techno-thriller with a recognizable Hitman rivalry and a credible education/workplace ensemble. The second watch should reveal that the backstory has been visible without spoiling the **choice** both men make after learning it. The story is not "McCluster fights himself."
