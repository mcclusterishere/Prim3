# Hitman character dossiers

These dossiers apply the same human-depth standard used on the Prim3 cast. Hitman people are not job labels, criminal archetypes or lyric props; each needs ordinary routines, money pressure, relationships, humor, fear and something they want that has nothing to do with operations.

## Benji — 28 — initiate → sovereign

**Civilian Tuesday:** late start after a late night, checks on a car or small hustle, family texts he answers selectively, meets somebody who needs a favor, spends more time solving other people's problems than admitting his own.  
**Money:** unstable but improving; obsessed with converting cash flow into ownership.  
**Family:** protective, proud, hates being perceived as the relative who needs saving.  
**Specialty:** people, leverage, mobility, reading hierarchy.  
**Voice:** fast, confident, medium-high profanity, jokes when sizing somebody up, gets very short when angry.  
**Private wound:** years of needing permission from people he did not respect.  
**Desire:** ownership.  
**Fear:** becoming Royce with a younger face.  
**Moral boundary:** believes he will never make his own people permanently dependent on him just to preserve authority.  
**Stress habit:** becomes more controlling while calling it “handling business.”  
**Secret:** part of him likes being needed more than being free.  
**Key ties:** Royce = father/obstacle; Sable = legitimacy; Dez = younger mirror; Mina = field credibility; Jordan = unknown parallel climb.  
**Failure mode:** builds the cage he escaped.

## “Uncle” Royce — 52 — old sovereign

**Civilian Tuesday:** breakfast at the same place, several people asking permission in different language, checks property/business interests, attends family/community events where everybody knows his face, ends the night reviewing who owes whom.  
**Money:** asset-rich, relationship-rich, cash opaque.  
**Family:** large chosen-family network; uses care and obligation interchangeably.  
**Specialty:** legitimacy, patronage, dispute resolution, memory.  
**Voice:** slow, story-heavy, low profanity until offended, almost never answers immediately.  
**Private wound:** built power after a period when nobody protected him.  
**Desire:** continuity.  
**Fear:** irrelevance.  
**Moral boundary:** will not knowingly expose children/family members to the consequences of adult disputes.  
**Stress habit:** tightens dependency networks.  
**Secret:** knows the organization can survive him and hates that fact.  
**Key ties:** Benji = successor threat; Sable = institutional memory; Tone = loyal old guard.  
**Failure mode:** protection becomes possession.

## Sable Quinn — 38 — operations / money

**Civilian Tuesday:** early books, vendor calls, payroll problems, a quiet lunch alone, checks three versions of the numbers because people lie most when they think accounting is boring.  
**Money:** personally stable, institutionally responsible for unstable flows.  
**Family:** distant relationship with siblings who think she “works in finance.”  
**Specialty:** budgets, obligations, routing resources, detecting false confidence.  
**Voice:** short, dry, low profanity, devastating when sarcastic.  
**Private wound:** once trusted a charismatic leader whose numbers were fiction and had to clean up the collapse.  
**Desire:** a system stronger than ego.  
**Fear:** backing the wrong sovereign.  
**Moral boundary:** will not steal from crew payroll or family support funds.  
**Stress habit:** stops eating and starts reciting numbers from memory.  
**Secret:** has modeled Hitman's finances under Benji before openly supporting him.  
**Key ties:** Benji = emerging bet; Royce = old obligation; Rack = trusted infrastructure.  
**Failure mode:** treats people like balance-sheet risk.

## Grant Voss — 47 — sponsor / money-board mark

**Civilian Tuesday:** polished office, donor/client calls, gym or club meeting, expensive lunch, asks other people to “handle” inconvenient consequences.  
**Money:** wealthy enough to confuse money with insulation.  
**Family:** curated public family image; emotional life heavily managed.  
**Specialty:** influence, introductions, converting private desire into respectable requests.  
**Voice:** polished, euphemistic, low profanity in public, dismissive in private.  
**Private wound:** humiliation terrifies him more than guilt.  
**Desire:** influence without liability.  
**Fear:** traceability.  
**Moral boundary:** claims family is off-limits, but his practical boundaries move when reputation is threatened.  
**Stress habit:** delegates more aggressively.  
**Secret:** keeps records as insurance against people he believes he controls.  
**Key ties:** Prime = invisible apex he misunderstands; Sable = transactional contact; Sora/Rae = pieces in a design he cannot see.  
**Failure mode:** believes distance equals innocence.

## Tone Rivera — 36 — block control

**Civilian Tuesday:** gym, neighborhood errands, helps an older relative with something physical, checks on younger crew, spends late evening restoring an old motorcycle.  
**Money:** steady but not wealthy; spends on family and machines.  
**Family:** known as the person who shows up.  
**Specialty:** de-escalation, physical presence, crowd reading.  
**Voice:** medium-short, medium profanity, humor through understatement.  
**Private wound:** once escalated a conflict because everybody expected him to be the tough one.  
**Desire:** respect that does not require fear.  
**Fear:** discovering fear was all he had.  
**Moral boundary:** will not start violence to perform status.  
**Stress habit:** becomes physically still and emotionally closed.  
**Secret:** sees Royce's decline earlier than he admits.  
**Key ties:** Royce = loyalty; Dez = mentorship; Perch = trusted warning voice.  
**Failure mode:** loyalty outlives judgment.

## Kira “Keys” Moon — 29 — access / social gate

**Civilian Tuesday:** answers invitations, coordinates rides, knows which venue changed staff, checks on friends who only call when they need entry somewhere, spends night at an event she is technically working without looking like she is working.  
**Money:** irregular but network-rich; hates being cash-poor around wealthy people.  
**Family:** keeps family separate from the people she connects professionally.  
**Specialty:** access, introductions, social topology.  
**Voice:** quick, teasing, high context, medium profanity; avoids direct answers when she is protecting information.  
**Private wound:** being treated as useful only because of who she knows.  
**Desire:** access that belongs to her, not borrowed status.  
**Fear:** nobody values her without the network.  
**Moral boundary:** will not intentionally feed a vulnerable friend into a situation they do not understand.  
**Stress habit:** starts managing everybody's movements.  
**Secret:** has an exit network Royce does not know about.  
**Key ties:** Rae = protégée/friend; Jules = technical dependency; Sable = skeptical boss.  
**Failure mode:** confuses control of access with control of outcomes.

## Omar “Rack” Dillon — 34 — gear / logistics

**Civilian Tuesday:** warehouse/storage checks, invoices, repairs, pickup/drop-off coordination, then home to a partner who is tired of hearing “five more minutes.”  
**Money:** cash-conscious; wants equity, not another envelope.  
**Family:** committed relationship under strain because the organization owns his schedule.  
**Specialty:** logistics, inventory, reliability.  
**Voice:** constant complaining, medium profanity, funny through specificity.  
**Private wound:** has repeatedly watched flashy people get credit for systems he kept alive.  
**Desire:** ownership in infrastructure.  
**Fear:** being blamed for leadership failures.  
**Moral boundary:** will not knowingly issue unsafe or unverified gear just because somebody is rushing.  
**Stress habit:** works alone rather than delegate.  
**Secret:** has enough inventory knowledge to cripple operations if he ever walked.  
**Key ties:** Sable = mutual trust; Jules = younger technical counterpart; Benji = future owner he might support if ownership becomes real for workers too.  
**Failure mode:** resentment accumulates silently.

## Jules “Kiosk” Mendez — 23 — deniable tech

**Civilian Tuesday:** repair counter, cheap food, online tutorials, side fixes, family asks why he has not gotten a “real tech job,” late-night experiments on discarded hardware.  
**Money:** broke-looking because he reinvests in tools and keeps income fragmented.  
**Family:** loving but skeptical of his career path.  
**Specialty:** repair, device troubleshooting, technical improvisation in ordinary environments.  
**Voice:** fast, playful, medium-high profanity, overexplains when excited.  
**Private wound:** being dismissed as “phone repair” by people who know less than he does.  
**Desire:** respect and real money.  
**Fear:** recognition destroying deniability.  
**Moral boundary:** will not deliberately expose a client's private personal material for entertainment or status.  
**Stress habit:** takes on problems to prove he can solve them.  
**Secret:** keeps a portfolio of legitimate engineering work because he still imagines a normal career.  
**Key ties:** Rae = closest friend; Benji = first leader who treats his technical skill seriously; Rack = logistics mentor.  
**Failure mode:** ego turns technical curiosity into bad judgment.

## Rae “Twin” Colton — 27 — street social-entry asset

**Civilian Tuesday:** beauty/gym/social routine, several accounts and conversations, helps friends stage photos or solve relationship drama, spends more time observing other people than being observed accurately herself.  
**Money:** better than she looks when she wants to look harmless; hates financial dependence.  
**Family:** one younger relative she protects fiercely and keeps away from the organization.  
**Specialty:** social engineering as intuition: attention, trust, vanity, embarrassment, proximity.  
**Voice:** playful, adaptive, medium profanity, changes rhythm depending on audience; becomes unusually plain with Jules.  
**Private wound:** never knowing whether affection is genuine when influencing people comes naturally.  
**Desire:** autonomy.  
**Fear:** being loved only as a version she manufactured.  
**Moral boundary:** will not use a protected family vulnerability against somebody uninvolved in the work.  
**Stress habit:** becomes more charming instead of more honest.  
**Secret:** she wants one relationship where she does not have to be “on.”  
**Key ties:** Jules = genuine trust; Kira = mentor; Sora = unknown twin; Benji = increasingly values her as strategy, not decoration.  
**Failure mode:** manipulation becomes reflex.

## Chris “Perch” Alvarez — 32 — overwatch

**Civilian Tuesday:** delivery/field work or photography-adjacent hustle, watches traffic patterns without meaning to, family dinner, rooftop or quiet place when he needs space.  
**Money:** modest, steady enough, avoids flashy spending.  
**Family:** present but he keeps operational life vague.  
**Specialty:** environmental awareness, pattern change, early warning.  
**Voice:** sparse, medium profanity, quiet humor, hates repeating warnings.  
**Private wound:** once warned people too softly and somebody interpreted uncertainty as permission to ignore him.  
**Desire:** be listened to before danger is obvious.  
**Fear:** late recognition.  
**Moral boundary:** will not fabricate certainty to make people obey him.  
**Stress habit:** withdraws and watches more.  
**Secret:** has considered leaving because the job is training him to see threats in ordinary life.  
**Key ties:** Tone = trusts his warnings; Mina = best leader at hearing uncertainty; Rafael = eventual cross-system mirror.  
**Failure mode:** detachment.

## Dez “Switch” Calhoun — 24 — active crew

**Civilian Tuesday:** odd jobs, gym, errands, friends asking him to fill roles because he is “good at everything,” late-night gaming/music, one plan for a business he has not started.  
**Money:** unstable; adaptable work history mirrors his personality.  
**Family:** loved but expected to figure himself out eventually.  
**Specialty:** rapid adaptation, role switching, social/physical flexibility.  
**Voice:** fast, high profanity, jokes constantly, changes vocabulary with audience.  
**Private wound:** praise has always been for how well he becomes what people need.  
**Desire:** identity plus usefulness.  
**Fear:** being replaceable despite being adaptable.  
**Moral boundary:** will not betray somebody who trusted him personally for a promotion.  
**Stress habit:** says yes to everything.  
**Secret:** does not know what he wants if nobody needs anything from him.  
**Key ties:** Benji = aspirational leader; Tone = mentor; Mina = professional authority.  
**Failure mode:** no stable self underneath adaptation.

## Mina “Halo” Brooks — 33 — cell lead

**Civilian Tuesday:** early workout, team check-ins disguised as casual messages, manages a household/family responsibility, tries to read or watch something unrelated to work and fails.  
**Money:** stable enough to support others, which increases dependency pressure.  
**Family:** one parent/younger sibling sees her as the responsible one.  
**Specialty:** small-team leadership, fear management, extraction decisions.  
**Voice:** clear, medium-short, moderate profanity only with peers, calm when everybody else is emotional.  
**Private wound:** survivor guilt from bringing most—but not all—of a crew home.  
**Desire:** leadership without worship.  
**Fear:** believing survival proves her choices were morally right.  
**Moral boundary:** will not order somebody to stay behind merely to protect reputation or property.  
**Stress habit:** revisits decisions privately and sleeps poorly.  
**Secret:** she sometimes wishes somebody else would take command and hates herself for the relief in that thought.  
**Key ties:** Benji = emerging authority; Royce = old structure; Dez = protected younger operator.  
**Failure mode:** certainty becomes performance she cannot stop.

## Sora “Gymnast” Okada — 26 — elite social-entry asset

**Civilian Tuesday:** disciplined training, polished academic/professional schedule, brand/event work, family check-in, careful meal, late-night reading about people whose rooms she may enter next week.  
**Money:** comfortable enough to access elite spaces but not wealthy enough to belong to them without work.  
**Family:** proud of her achievements and unaware of the hidden operational layer.  
**Specialty:** elite social access, athletic control, observational discipline, public presentation.  
**Voice:** polished, low profanity, complete sentences, becomes more direct when emotionally safe.  
**Private wound:** years of being praised for presentation while having to prove intelligence twice.  
**Desire:** use access rather than be used as access.  
**Fear:** her entire resume is camouflage somebody else selected.  
**Moral boundary:** will not knowingly exploit a target's child or uninvolved dependent.  
**Stress habit:** trains harder and becomes more perfect outwardly.  
**Secret:** keeps detailed private notes on assignments because she distrusts how little context she receives.  
**Key ties:** Priya = legitimate-facing handler/friend; Grant = target-world power; Rae = unknown operational twin.  
**Failure mode:** perfection masks loss of agency.

# Maintenance rule

If a Hitman character gains money, loses a person, changes rank, becomes publicly known, acquires a legitimate business, starts a relationship, or learns the Prim3 sibling truth, update this file and `CHARACTER-RELATIONSHIP-STATE.md`. Hitman cannot reset to street archetypes between episodes.
