# Season Reveal Map — PROPOSAL

The owner adopted the structure of Issue #19 on 2026-11-03: each season reveals the **people, culture and power** of one more Equity Uprise floor. It does not lock the physical floor. Every floor B1–L7 exists, is wayfound, and can be visited in any season where canon and role permissions allow. What arrives season by season is the audience's *working understanding* of who sits there, what they owe each other, and who can override whom.

This file cross-references that structure with the canon location map (`story/EPISODE-WORKPLACE-LOCATION-MAP.md`) episode by episode. Rule applied throughout:

> **Zero relocations.** No episode's mission moves. No canon footprint is removed. Where a season's spotlight floor is absent from an episode's canon footprint, this map proposes a workplace scene *around* the mission (before, between or after), never inside it.

Per-episode scene additions below are **pending owner sign-off**. The season→floor mapping itself is adopted as structure.

## 4. Rules this map adds (all PROPOSED)

1. **Spotlight is editorial, not a security gate.** No episode may use "that floor isn't revealed yet" as an in-world reason a character cannot go somewhere. Role-based access (`EQUITY-UPRISE-WORKPLACE-CANON.md`) is the only in-world gate.
2. **A gain scene is a workplace scene, not a mission scene.** It sits before, between or after the canon mission footprint; it uses the floor's named workstations (`world/equity-uprise-workplace-roster.v1.json`; mccluster `workplace-occupancy-v1.json` is the geometry side and is read-only from here); it obeys "if you appear, you move".
3. **Glimpses stay.** Earlier-season glimpses of later-spotlight floors (S1E1's hidden F6 observation, S2E3's F4 reinforcement) are canon and are not removed to "save" the reveal. The reveal is of the people, not the room.
4. **B1 is never the surprise.** Any draft that frames B1 as discovered, unlocked, or secret is wrong under this map and under `CANON.md` §2.
5. **Knowledge is tracked separately from presence.** A character standing on F6 in S1 does not learn F6's S7 truths. The implementation contract is `story/WORLD-MEMORY-CONTRACT.md`.

## 5. What this map does not do

- It does not change any death, ending, song, title or the open S6E3 slot.
- It does not write the gain scenes. Each is one line of intent here; each needs its episode owner's development write and the owner's sign-off.
- It does not touch geometry. Floor programs, workstations and access rules stay with the mccluster building authority.
- It does not assert PRIM2's body is at HQ. F6 "fully understood" means his *permitted interfaces* are understood (`CANON.md` §17).

> **Same building. Same people. One more floor's working life each year.**
