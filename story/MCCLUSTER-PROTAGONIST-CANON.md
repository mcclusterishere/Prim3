# McCluster — Protagonist / Succession Canon

## Canon reset

The canonical PRIM3 protagonist is **McCluster**.

The prior protagonist identity is retired from current canon. Historical treatments, episode plans, game rules and character references must resolve to McCluster unless an explicitly archived source is being discussed as obsolete development history.

McCluster does **not** enter the series by joining McCluster Corp.

He enters by joining **Equity Uprise**.

That distinction is foundational.

## Opening truth

At the beginning of PRIM3, McCluster understands Equity Uprise as a serious development/workforce institution with an unusually sophisticated technical campus.

He does not understand that Equity Uprise sits inside a much larger McCluster Corp ecosystem, that McCluster Corp is governed through the PRIM architecture, that PRIM2 is the presiding Mantle holder, or that his own path through Equity Uprise is being observed as a potential succession path.

The name relationship between **McCluster** and **McCluster Corp** is meaningful, but its explanation is reveal-gated.

## The inheritance

PRIM3 is ultimately an inheritance story.

McCluster is being prepared to inherit a system designed to be passed forward.

The inheritance is not a normal promotion, XP rank or ordinary executive appointment. It is validated transfer of the **Primary Root Identity Mantle** and the authority attached to it.

At opening, PRIM2 is the presiding holder.

At validated succession, McCluster becomes PRIM3.

After succession, McCluster becomes the active human root authority over the PRIM-governed McCluster Corp system, subject to the governance and constitutional limits established elsewhere in canon.

Equity Uprise remains meaningful after succession. It is not discarded as a fake front.

## Chosen-one structure without prophecy

McCluster should feel low-key "Neo-like" in structure without becoming a magical prophecy character.

Canon rule:

> **The system has been preparing a path for him before he understands that a path exists.**

That may appear through unusual assignments, conditional permissions, senior people knowing more about him than expected, cross-domain tests, temporary access, and PRIM2 observing him without explaining why.

PRIM never simply tells him he is chosen.

The succession must remain earned, contested and dangerous.

## Why McCluster

McCluster's defining advantage is not being the world's best specialist.

He is the Wildcard / Flex because he repeatedly crosses seams between technical work, physical systems, security, operations, logistics, media, research, business, institutional politics and human relationships.

His defining flaw is the inverse of the same strength:

> **Being able to connect everything can become believing he should control everything.**

The succession story therefore asks whether someone capable of holding enormous centralized authority can learn not to confuse capability with entitlement.

## McCluster / Benji / Ghost continuity

The reverse-reveal structure remains.

- Hitman / Benji — approximately age 19–26.
- Whip Equipped / Ghost — approximately age 26–33.
- PRIM3 / Equity Uprise — approximately age 33–40.

**Benji is McCluster.**

**Ghost is McCluster during the Whip Equipped period.**

They are life-stage identities, not separate contemporary characters.

## The name mystery

The shared name between the protagonist and McCluster Corp is intentional story pressure.

Do not resolve the exact origin too early.

The audience may wonder whether the relationship is lineage, prior institutional design, deliberate identity construction, Mantle history, or something McCluster himself does not yet understand.

Current canon does not reduce succession to blood inheritance.

## Equity Uprise is the first doorway

McCluster's normal story begins inside Equity Uprise.

The audience first learns the larger world through the lobby, cohorts, instructors, security, labs, media spaces, research rooms, infrastructure spaces, command floor, B1 technical operations and roof/mobility operations.

The institution becomes stranger gradually because it is too capable, too connected and too deeply integrated with McCluster Corp systems for an ordinary program.

## Spatial progression

McCluster's institutional progression is partly visible through where he can work.

Default arc:

F1 arrival and onboarding
-> F2 cohort/community integration
-> F3 recurring Wildcard / fellowship desk
-> cross-domain B1/F4/F5 access as earned
-> F6 command access
-> root truth outside Equity Uprise
-> Site 0 succession conflict

This is not a rigid level-lock formula. Access is a story language for trust and responsibility.

## Site 0 distinction

Equity Uprise HQ is the recurring workplace for the series.

It is **not** the hidden root headquarters assaulted in the final RAID.

Use these terms consistently:

- **Equity Uprise HQ / Equity Uprise building / Uprise HQ** = recurring day-to-day workplace.
- **Site 0 / Root HQ** = hidden McCluster Corp / PRIM root facility involved in the final assault and succession crisis.

Characters may casually say "HQ" about Equity Uprise because it is their working headquarters. Final-arc material should say Site 0 or Root HQ when ambiguity matters.

## Governing lines

> **McCluster joins Equity Uprise. He does not know he is entering his inheritance.**

> **The succession belongs to McCluster. The world belongs to everybody.**

> **Equity Uprise is where he learns the system. Site 0 is where he learns what the system was preparing him to become.**
