# Progression rules

## Goal
Protagonist passes through **all 13 phenotypes** across the series without rocket-promoting every episode.

## Four tracks (tick one per episode)
1. **Seat** — physical/political nearness to brief and board
2. **Clearance** — white-box crumbs → grey → black
3. **Role cycle** — support work rotates T / E / R / Lead shadow while title stays stable
4. **Agency** — support Prime → run op for Prime → run op at Prime

## Title changes
- At **season boundary** at most
- Never required every episode
- Same “Operator” label can cover many episodes of different *work*

## Phenotype pass
All 13 Prim3-side phenotypes appear as seats the protagonist occupies or shadows before the final turn. Order is curriculum-driven (support literacy before agency), not a checklist rush.

## Grades still apply
Binary mission fail banned. Partial work grades. Season continues.
