# Progression rules

## Core correction

PRIM3 progression is not one protagonist passing through every phenotype.

The roster is an ensemble of named people with different disciplines, careers, loyalties and rates of advancement.

Jordan's seven academic years remain an important chronological spine, but gameplay progression must deepen the whole cast.

---

# Character progression

A character may progress through some combination of:

1. **Qualification** — what functions they are trained/authorized to perform.
2. **Seat** — physical/political nearness to mission planning and institutional authority.
3. **Clearance** — which information and environments they may legitimately access.
4. **Mastery** — demonstrated technical/field competence.
5. **Leadership** — responsibility for other people and decisions.
6. **Relationship state** — trust, debt, rivalry, mentorship, attachment, grief.
7. **Psychological adaptation** — old priors strengthened, challenged or modified by lived consequences.

No character needs to pass through every role.

Specialists should remain meaningfully better than generalists at their deepest work.

---

# Jordan progression

Jordan is the **Wildcard / Flex**, not the universal class tree.

His growth is expressed through:
- increased cross-lane literacy;
- better judgment about when not to intervene;
- growing institutional trust/clearance;
- increasing cost of becoming everybody's emergency answer;
- changing relationships;
- deeper understanding of authority;
- eventual willingness to challenge PRIM2.

Ordinary PLAY still withholds Jordan from the normal selectable roster. His Wildcard call-up is governed by `game/WILDCARD-INTERVENTION-AUTHORITY.md`.

Jordan becoming **PRIM3** is not an XP unlock. It is the final Mantle succession event.

---

# Ensemble campaign progression

The player should feel the roster becoming more capable because specific people grow.

Examples:
- Aya deepens cyber-physical and validation capability;
- Chioma develops command judgment;
- Rafael/Oksana improve picture quality and warning;
- Jonas changes how the team interprets Pylon/system behavior;
- other named operators acquire qualifications based on their own histories and missions.

Progression should make losing a veteran mechanically and emotionally different from losing a generic unit.

---

# Grades still apply

Binary mission failure remains discouraged where useful partial outcomes exist.

Mission grades can affect:
- Standing;
- resources;
- injuries/fatigue;
- qualification opportunities;
- relationship state;
- Intel;
- future mission conditions;
- Wildcard/Apex pressure.

Learning Mastery remains separate from mission outcome.

---

# Governing law

> **The roster does not level up around Jordan. Jordan grows inside a roster that is leveling up around itself.**
