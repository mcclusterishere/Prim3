# Aya Shinoda / Hana Lee — Intelligence Research Grounding

## Purpose

This file records the real-world grounding behind `story/AYA-HANA-INTELLIGENCE-CANON.md`.

It is not evidence that any fictional PRIM3 event occurred in the real world.

Hana Lee, Prime, McCluster, Operation GLASSHOUSE, the Living Root investigation and every espionage event in the story are fictional.

---

# 1. Republic of Korea National Intelligence Service

South Korea's current national intelligence organization is the **National Intelligence Service (NIS / 국가정보원)**.

The NIS publicly describes national-security work spanning overseas intelligence, economic security / industrial security, counterintelligence, cyber security, AI and other emerging security domains.

Official sources:

- National Intelligence Service, Republic of Korea — https://www.nis.go.kr/
- NIS organizational / government-identity page listing associated security centers, including cyber security, industrial-secret protection, defense-industry protection and AI security — https://nis.go.kr/ID/1_8_1.do

This makes it plausible in fiction that a technically sophisticated South Korean intelligence officer could be tasked with assessing an opaque foreign organization whose activities combine advanced robotics, cyber-physical control, industrial technology and transnational command authority.

It does **not** imply that the real NIS conducts the specific fictional activity depicted in PRIM3.

---

# 2. Fictionalization boundary

The following are inventions for PRIM3:

- Hana Lee being an NIS officer;
- her research/professional cover;
- her assignment against McCluster;
- the Living Root hypothesis;
- Prime identifying her;
- Prime using her as an unwitting adversarial red team;
- Operation GLASSHOUSE being used as the opportunity for her death;
- Prime withholding decisive safety information;
- and Aya later entering McCluster to investigate.

No real intelligence officer, Korean government employee, university, company or facility is represented by Hana or the GLASSHOUSE operation.

---

# 3. Writing-safety boundary

Hana's intelligence work should remain at the level of:

- strategic objective;
- inference;
- institutional suspicion;
- character motivation;
- competing loyalties;
- evidence interpretation;
- and dramatic consequence.

The story does not require operational instructions for espionage, covert access, evasion, sabotage, credential theft, covert communications or exfiltration.

The intelligence plot exists to deepen character psychology and Prime's control architecture, not to function as a real-world espionage manual.
