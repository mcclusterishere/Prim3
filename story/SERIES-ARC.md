# PRIM3 Series Arc

PRIM3 is a **seven-season / 21-episode ensemble techno-thriller**.

- 3 episodes per season.
- 1 song = 1 episode = 1 mission.
- WATCH target ~9 minutes; PLAY expands the same event.
- Jordan Vale's seven academic years provide a major chronological/succession spine.
- Episode ownership rotates across the multinational cast; Jordan may be supporting, cameo, mentioned-only or absent.
- Most Episodes 02–18 should primarily deepen characters other than Jordan.
- Global Episode 01 `High Alert` is a future/endgame exception centered on **PRIM2 + Jordan + Royce**.
- Final trilogy: **RAID -> Evil Twin -> Ghost In The Wires**.
- Jordan becomes **PRIM3** only after succession.

- visible: **Program for Resilient Infrastructure Management**
- root: **Primary Root Identity Mantle**
