The owner's November 9 decision replaces the old seven-academic-year chronology. One season is one academic semester. Two semesters per academic year make seven semesters a 3.5-academic-year accelerated degree path. This is not a claim that Fall 2026 through Fall 2029 lasts exactly 42 calendar months.

Implementation/editorial framing: the season's opening and midterms episodes may frame or flash forward into a single continuous final operation. RAID → Evil Twin → Ghost remains continuous around the Fall 2029 finals convergence; PRIM2 is not left injured for months between these actions. S07E01 and S07E02 denote the opening/midterms *episode frames*, not a demand to separate the raid's physical action across those dates. S07E03 is the required finals frame and succession resolution. The pilot's endgame opening then returns with the card **FALL 2026**, avoiding an incorrect numeric rewind interval. This framing preserves the existing endgame causal order and ownership.

McCluster enters college at 33 and reaches the Fall 2029 finale at approximately 36–37, depending on his unspecified birthday. No exact birthday is invented. Benji remains McCluster; Site 0 is not his beginning; he becomes PRIM3 only after validated succession. The age-19–26 Hitman era, age-26–33 Ghost/Whip era, Royce's seven-year age difference and Hana's seven-year age difference are unchanged.
