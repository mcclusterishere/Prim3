# Ghost endgame — keep him alive in the cloud

If the player **spares** Prime after RAID / Evil Twin confession:

## What happens to him

He is uploaded. **Ghost in the Wires.**  
He is still alive — as the building’s and the entire organization’s **personal assistant** to the new commander.

**You** are the new Prime / commander.

## What the game becomes

Not little side missions anymore.

**World-domination conquest layer** — closer to **Civilization V (but sharper)** than to a single tactical raid:

| Arm | Function |
|-----|----------|
| **Hitman** | Your **army** — kinetic, deniable, street-to-black-ops |
| **Prim3 / McCluster side** | Your **government** — institutions, cover, policy, systems |
| **Ghost-Prime** | Cloud advisor — feeds, suggestions, dual-intel, the old god-view now in your ear |
| **Espionage** | You remain (or become) implanted as spy / intel bearer across **multiple world militaries** — different truth to different flags, same as he did |
| **Special operations** | Your small teams still exist — higher-level scalpel strikes inside the grand map |

## Fantasy in one line

You inherit the dual machine. Hitman is the fist. Prim3 is the state face. Ghost-Prime is the assistant who already ran the planet from a tank. The map opens. Conquest and espionage replace “one compound tonight.”

## Vs kill path

| Choice | Product |
|--------|---------|
| **Spare → Ghost** | Civ-scale command + assistant Prime + army/government split |
| **Kill → Whip Equipped** | Colder personal story — car, Bridgeport, no throne |

## Design note

Tactical XCOM-style and FPS Hitman layers can still fire as **ops inside** the Civ layer. The Civ layer is the post-spare **campaign scale**, not a replacement of all combat fantasy on day one of the series.
