# Ghost endgame — keep him alive in the cloud

If the player **spares** Prime after RAID / Evil Twin confession:

## What happens to him

He is uploaded. **Ghost in the Wires.**
He is still alive — as the building’s and the entire organization’s **personal assistant** to the new commander.

**You** are the new Prime / commander.

## What the game becomes

Not little side missions anymore.

**World-domination conquest layer** — closer to **Civilization V (but sharper)** than to a single tactical raid:

| Arm | Function |
|-----|----------|
| **Hitman** | Your **army** |
| **Prim3 / McCluster side** | Your **government** |
| **Ghost-Prime** | Cloud advisor — old god-view in your ear |
| **Espionage** | Implanted intel across multiple world militaries |
| **Special operations** | Higher-level scalpel strikes on the grand map |

## Vs kill path
