# Prim3 main-character dossiers

These dossiers implement `HUMAN-STORY-ENGINE.md` and `CHARACTER-PERSONALIZATION-TEMPLATE.md` for the 24 Prim3/McCluster mains. They are **proposed development**, not a silent rewrite of root canon.

## 01 — Jordan Vale — United States — 33

**Civilian baseline:** First-year college student, recently homeless, living as cheaply and close to campus/work as circumstances allow. A normal Tuesday before McCluster is class, library/computer-lab time, job hunting, fixing somebody else's tech for cash, checking account balance too often, and trying to look less financially unstable than he is.

**Money / family:** Money is immediate, not abstract. He thinks in bus fare, food, tuition, phone service, storage, and whether he can afford to say no to an opportunity. Family ties exist but he hates becoming another emergency somebody has to solve.

**Education / specialty:** Years of practical IT, infrastructure and data-center work; formally under-credentialed. Strongest at systems diagnosis, infrastructure intuition and recognizing when official descriptions do not match operational reality.

**Social presentation:** Older freshman energy: observant, capable, sometimes defensive when younger classmates assume age equals authority or failure. He often makes jokes right before admitting something serious.

**Voice:** Medium-short sentences, concrete examples, technical slang only when useful, moderate profanity, answers directly unless the question touches shame or dependency. Talks more when excited about a system; less when embarrassed about money.

**Private wound:** Years of being useful without being institutionally secure. Being treated as disposable hurts more than he admits.

**Desire:** legitimacy and control over his future.  
**Fear:** becoming powerful enough to never be disposable and then treating others as disposable.  
**Contradiction:** deeply distrusts institutions while craving recognition from one.

**Moral boundary:** believes he would never knowingly sacrifice a teammate for institutional efficiency.  
**Stress habit:** stops sleeping, over-researches, starts treating every problem like it can be solved if he just refuses to quit.  
**Secret:** he sometimes needs McCluster to be good because admitting it may be dangerous would mean the best opportunity of his life is another trap.

**Relationships:** trusts Aya; protects Valeria; is professionally dependent on Chioma and Arjun earlier than he likes; underestimates Grace's ability to read him; Prime sees through him from the beginning.

**Failure mode:** tenacity becomes fixation.  
**Longevity:** full seven-season spine.  
**Test:** whether he can become Prime without converting love, grief and friendship into resource accounting.
