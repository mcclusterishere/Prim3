# Episode progression — every song is an episode

**Source:** owner lyric files in hand (Prim3 *.docx / RAID pdf).  
**Rule:** one song = one episode. Order is **story progression**, not album random.  
**Characters:** Jordan Vale · **Aya Shinoda** · Benji · Prime · Maren Quill · hire chain.

High Alert is **2:47** (Prime’s pause). Other episodes ~6:00 = song + lived story + **tech in the room**.

---

## E01 — High Alert
**Song job:** Prime POV. Tank wake. Dual raid (Jordan + Benji). Hire-chain threat. Neuron/robot fire. Memory plants for everything below.  
**Story progress:** In medias res — main site already under contact. Raid does not end.  
**Tech:** House AI, robots, EMP/circuit kill, multi-site map.  
**Dev:** Audience meets the war before the education.

---

## E02 — White Grey Black Hat
**Lyrics:** White/grey/black hat + box; permission vs war; pen test vs trenches.  
**Story progress:** Jordan’s early seat — **authorization**. Aya teaches doors: white is permission, black is war, grey needs a signature.  
**Tech:** Access control, scoped engagement rules, reporting vs silent win.  
**Dev:** Jordan wants in; Aya sets the standard. First moral language of the house.

---

## E03 — OSINT
**Lyrics:** Open sources, social/forums, vulndb, sharing centers, indicators, quiet money for silence.  
**Story progress:** Jordan learns to **enter view** of a target without forcing the door. Ties to “scope of my work” seed.  
**Tech:** Public sources, logging, vulnerability databases, traffic indicators.  
**Dev:** Jordan sees how Prim3 packages people. Unease starts small.

---

## E04 — Anti Social Engineering
**Lyrics:** Antisocial unless social-engineering; shoulder surf; phishing/smishing; Tinder/IG; spoof router; DEFcon red team energy.  
**Story progress:** Human layer. Jordan fails a staged diversion (likes being chosen). Aya calls it. Shadow of **Sly**/Evil Twin method.  
**Tech:** Social platforms as attack surface, spoof, physical shoulder/drone surf.  
**Dev:** Jordan learns desire is a vector. Aya’s trust becomes the counter-example.

---

## E05 — Red Blue Purple White Team
**Lyrics:** Red offense, blue defense, purple mix, white rules; yellow/orange/green code colors.  
**Story progress:** Jordan is placed on a **crew**. Meets cohort as colors, not job titles. Paid-in-full vs greasy corporate.  
**Tech:** Team roles in live exercise; rules of engagement.  
**Dev:** Belonging. First taste of family that is also a machine.

---

## E06 — Patch Work
**Lyrics:** *Give me one night I can patch it up*; sandbox; trojans; UEFI/BIOS; validate; code signing; XSS/ex’s; static/dynamic analysis; blacklist IPs. **Relationship song under firmware.**  
**Story progress:** Jordan × **Aya Shinoda** go public in the work and private in the metaphor. Fight over trust/baggage/old scripts. Reconciliation as clean patch.  
**Tech:** Validation, signing, analysis, secure practices on real decks.  
**Dev:** Love as integrity check. Core emotional bond of the Prim3 lane.

---

## E07 — App Attacks
**Lyrics:** Priv, RBAC, XSS, CSP, injection, SQLMap-class energy (from catalog + app-layer war).  
**Story progress:** Harder ops. Jordan’s team lives in soft targets. Aya refuses dirty shortcuts. Dual-board smell starts.  
**Tech:** App-layer attacks/defenses in controlled then live contexts.  
**Dev:** Competence up; moral friction up.

---

## E08 — Installation Types
**Lyrics:** Multiboot / in-place / upgrade path language (catalog). Path found vs brothers searching (High Alert plant).  
**Story progress:** Jordan chooses a **path**—deeper install into Prim3, not a side gig. Aya is CapEx energy (live with the system); temptation of OpEx exits.  
**Tech:** Install/upgrade discipline as plot.  
**Dev:** Commitment. No more tourist seat.

---

## E09 — Dive In
**Lyrics:** Physical + RF OSINT energy; dumpster/drone (catalog). Angels on serpent plant.  
**Story progress:** Off the glass, into the world. Field-R adjacency. First real physical risk.  
**Tech:** SDR-class sense, physical collection.  
**Dev:** Jordan stops being only a bay operator.

---

## E10 — IoT
**Lyrics:** Defaults, smart devices, soft targets (catalog). Soft/software double.  
**Story progress:** Soft doors everywhere—including home and compound sensors. Hint that Prime’s house *is* the IoT thesis.  
**Tech:** Consumer/default device exploitation as soft entry.  
**Dev:** Paranoia becomes architectural.

---

## E11 — IaaS, SaaS, PaaS
**Lyrics:** Serving layers; hybrid; platform helps craft/test/report; “feels so right” sign-in.  
**Story progress:** Institutional stack revealed as **service models**—McCluster doesn’t only market; it serves control in layers. Maren’s floor connects to real backend.  
**Tech:** Cloud service models as lived infrastructure.  
**Dev:** Jordan sees the holding company under the smile.

---

## E12 — Cap Ex vs Op Ex
**Lyrics:** **Wifey vs sidepiece**—own vs rent; high maintenance vs ghostable; CapEx privilege vs OpEx exit.  
**Story progress:** Relationship economics. **Aya = CapEx** (live with her / the work). Side options = OpEx. Money and love use the same chart.  
**Tech/finance:** Capital vs operational spend on real kit and real bonds.  
**Dev:** Jordan must choose what he owns vs what he rents—people included.

---

## E13 — Trappin From The Cloud
**Lyrics:** Scalability, allocate, disaster recovery, elasticity, high availability; trap booming while at rest; sniff packets.  
**Story progress:** Ops at scale. Jordan runs cloud-side work that doesn’t sleep. Compare to Prime’s tank rest.  
**Tech:** Cloud scale, recovery, elastic pricing.  
**Dev:** He becomes the kind of operator the dual board notices.

---

## E14 — Per Diem
**Lyrics:** Hardware identity, hybrid, grind vs seasonal (catalog). Online vs in-person grind plant.  
**Story progress:** Human cost. Cohort rates, exhaustion, paid-in-full identity. Aya and Jordan under pressure; Benji’s lane intercuts hotter.  
**Tech/culture:** Identity gear, hybrid work, rate politics.  
**Dev:** Moral break accelerates—Jordan can feel the board is wrong.

---

## E15 — RAID (Hot Site)
**Lyrics/doctrine:** RAID levels, hot/warm/cold sites; multi-compound.  
**Story progress:** **Full assault.** Outer sites. Main site. Jordan + Benji. Completes what E01 opened. Prime can be **defeated**.  
**Tech:** Multi-site failover as geography and play.  
**Dev:** Climax of the climb.

---

## E16 — Evil Twin
**Lyrics:** Dual identity / social chain / SIM-class / diversion (owner mission notes + catalog).  
**Story progress:** **Confession after defeat.** Greater good. Dual board. Hire chain. Serpent and dame-dime seeds cash out. Aya hears *Prime’s people* explained.  
**Tech:** Evil twin / social-engineering chain as confessed method.  
**Dev:** Truth. Choice setup.

---

## Ending mode — Ghost In The Wires
**Lyrics:** Wire, infrastructure, cabling, connectors; forever in the system.  
**If Jordan spares Prime:** upload; mentor forever; he remains the wire he always was in the tank.  
**If kill:** Whip Equipped path (not this song’s mercy).
