# Jordan Vale — Hitman Origin Canon

## Authority

This file locks Jordan Vale's first adult seven-year arc and the identity relationship among **Jordan Vale**, **Benji**, **Bigga Lex the Jet**, and **Royce**.

Where older PRIM3 material treats Benji as a separate contemporary character, this file supersedes that interpretation.

**Benji is Jordan Vale's Hitman identity from age 19 through age 26.**

Where an older draft needs a separate present-day Hitman-side operator after Jordan has left Hitman, that structural role belongs to **Royce** unless later owner canon deliberately changes it.

---

# 1. Age 19 — first college attempt

Jordan enters college for the first time at **19 years old**.

He did **not** grow up as a drug dealer and did not arrive at college with an established drug operation. College is the first place where he begins selling drugs.

The operation grows quickly. Jordan becomes highly successful at selling on campus and uses the money in part to pay his tuition.

This is an important character distinction:

- Jordan is not following a childhood profession;
- he discovers an opportunity inside a system;
- he scales it rapidly;
- and he begins using an illegal solution to solve a legitimate financial problem.

Jordan nearly gets caught.

He understands the warning and nevertheless continues selling.

That decision matters. His eventual removal from school is not a single innocent mistake. He sees the risk, believes he can continue managing it, and keeps going.

---

# 2. The dean's private intervention

The dean of the college learns that Jordan is selling drugs on campus.

Instead of immediately turning the matter into a criminal case, the dean approaches Jordan privately and gives him an exit: **leave the college rather than have the situation escalated into a path that could put him in jail.**

Jordan accepts.

He does not create a public confrontation, force the school to humiliate him, or gamble on continuing after the dean has given him a way out.

He **gracefully bows out**.

The exact college, dean identity, mechanics of the near-catch, substance mix, money totals, and administrative/legal paperwork remain open until deliberately written.

---

# 3. The lie Jordan tells his parents

Jordan does not tell his parents the real reason he left school.

He tells them that he **flunked out**.

To Jordan, academic failure is the easier shame to carry home than admitting:

- he sold drugs at school;
- the school discovered it;
- and he was privately given an opportunity to leave before the situation became criminal.

This becomes one of the earliest durable secrets in Jordan's adult life.

Later, when Jordan returns to college at 33, the audience should understand that this is not simply an older student trying school for the first time. He is returning to a place in his life that ended fourteen years earlier under a lie his family still may not fully understand.

---

# 4. Why Jordan joins Hitman

Jordan does **not** leave college at 19 and immediately sign up to become a Hitman operator.

He enters Hitman through **culture, friendship, and music**.

Jordan already grew up with **Bigga Lex the Jet**, one of the Hitman operators who holds major authority inside the organization and Jordan's closest personal bridge into that world.

Jordan's initial reasons for being around Hitman are ordinary compared with what the organization later becomes for him:

- his best friend is there;
- his social circle overlaps with the crew;
- he wants to make music with people inside Hitman;
- the culture gives him belonging after leaving college;
- and Hitman initially feels like a crew/community before it feels like an operational institution.

Jordan does not join because he dreams of becoming an operator.

**Friendships turn him into one.**

The transition must be gradual enough that Jordan can plausibly look back and identify multiple points where social loyalty became operational obligation.

---

# 5. Benji

Inside Hitman, Jordan becomes known as **Benji**.

Benji is not a separate person, clone, brother, rival, or parallel protagonist.

**Benji = Jordan Vale during his Hitman years.**

The Hitman-era songs previously associated with Hitman Benji are therefore treated as Jordan's own earlier-life music/story material where their content remains compatible with later detailed canon.

The tonal difference between young Benji and 33-year-old Jordan is intentional. Jordan has lived another seven years after leaving Hitman before PRIM3 begins.

The Hitman arc covers approximately:

**AGE 19 -> AGE 26**

This is Jordan's first seven-year adult identity arc.

---

# 6. Bigga Lex the Jet

**Bigga Lex the Jet** is central to why Jordan stays in Hitman as long as he does.

Jordan grew up with him.

Jordan's loyalty to Hitman is therefore never purely loyalty to an organization. A major part of it is loyalty to his best friend.

Bigga Lex is already an operator and major authority figure within Hitman when Jordan enters the culture around it.

Over the seven-year period, Jordan/Benji becomes valuable enough inside the organization that Bigga Lex eventually views him as a legitimate successor.

---

# 7. Age 26 — succession refused

At approximately **26 years old**, Bigga Lex the Jet retires from Hitman.

Bigga Lex attempts to give **Benji/Jordan his title and position**.

This is the first major succession opportunity of Jordan's life.

Jordan refuses it.

He does not fight for the chair, negotiate for a larger share, or accept leadership out of obligation.

He **gracefully bows out of Hitman**.

The reason is fundamental:

> Jordan was primarily in Hitman because his best friend was there. Once Bigga Lex leaves, Jordan does not want the organization badly enough to inherit it.

This becomes a critical mirror for the later PRIM3 succession arc.

At 26, Jordan is offered power and walks away.

At 40, Jordan will confront a form of power he may not be able to walk away from so easily.

---

# 8. Royce succession

Jordan does not leave Bigga Lex's position empty.

Instead, Benji/Jordan gives the opportunity to **Royce**, one of his biggest Hitman rivals.

Royce therefore enters the long-form saga as more than a late-game enemy.

He is:

- a genuine rival from Jordan's Hitman period;
- the person who receives the position Jordan refused;
- the operator whose later trajectory can show what Jordan's life might have become if he had remained;
- and the natural present-day Hitman-side counterpart once the PRIM3 story reaches the dual-board conflict.

The exact nature of Jordan and Royce's rivalry, why Jordan believes Royce should receive the position despite that rivalry, whether Royce interprets the transfer as respect, abandonment, insult, opportunity, or some mixture, and how their relationship changes during the fourteen years after age 19 remain open for later character-by-character development.

---

# 9. Seven-year structural meaning

The first seven-year arc is now canonically:

```text
AGE 19
FIRST COLLEGE ATTEMPT
-> first drug dealing begins on campus
-> operation grows and helps pay tuition
-> near-catch
-> Jordan continues anyway
-> dean discovers it and offers private exit
-> Jordan leaves school
-> tells parents he flunked out
-> enters Hitman through Bigga Lex / friendship / music / culture
-> friendships gradually turn him into an operator
-> Jordan operates as BENJI
-> seven years of Hitman life
-> Bigga Lex retires
-> offers Benji the title
-> Benji refuses succession
-> Benji gives the position to rival ROYCE
-> Jordan leaves Hitman
AGE 26
```

This history should not be presented as a detached exposition dump inside PRIM3. It should surface through behavior, old music, recognition, records, relationships, contradictions, scars, operational instincts, and eventually explicit revelation.

---

# 10. Continuity migration rule

Effective with this canon:

1. **Benji is Jordan Vale's former Hitman identity.**
2. There is no separate contemporary Benji who independently climbs Hitman alongside Jordan.
3. **Royce inherits the structural role of the post-Benji Hitman-side rival/successor** in later dual-board material unless deliberately revised.
4. Older references to `Jordan + Benji` as two different men in RAID/Evil Twin are superseded at the canon level and must be rewritten as the finale is refined.
5. References to `Benji` that clearly describe Jordan's age-19-to-26 Hitman period remain valid and should not be renamed to Royce.
6. Prime's relationship to Jordan must eventually account for Jordan's hidden Hitman history, but **whether Prime knew about Benji from the beginning is not locked by this file**.
