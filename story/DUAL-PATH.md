# Dual path — Prim3 and Hitman

## The split
| Path | Identity | Play | Strength |
|------|----------|------|----------|
| **Prim3** | Systems, IT, institutional stealth | XCOM-style tactical | Intellect, control of information |
| **Hitman** | Brute force, kill-religion, street multinational | CoD-style / mobile FPS | Bodies, fear, deniable kinetic |

Both climb toward **Prime**. Sometimes parallel. Sometimes head-on.

## Why they fight each other
Prime (and the appearance of opposition) requires conflict:

1. **Cover** — clients and states see two enemies, not one hand
2. **Cull** — weak operators on either path die; only extreme intellect and extreme violence remain useful
3. **Masking** — blood between the houses hides quieter dual-board objectives

Casualties are not “accidents in the script.” They are the tax of the theory.

## Dual play (product)
- Player can run **Prim3 missions** (TB, phones later as light TB)
- Player can run **Hitman missions** (FPS, phone-first CoD energy, high death expected)
- Some calendar beats are **linked**: same objective, two orgs, possibly fighting over it because Prime pointed both at the same bone

## Story rule
Neither path is “the good guys.” Prim3 teaches and steers. Hitman ends. Prime sits above both until the upload.

## Music rule
- Prim3 catalog = systems literacy in the bars
- Hitman catalog (Hitshake, Crimewave, …) = street kill-gospel — separate show energy allowed
