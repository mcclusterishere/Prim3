# Hitman Benji — Benjamin Chase, McCluster's clone

## Continuity correction

**Owner decisions (2026-11-02, 2026-11-03): Benjamin "Benji" Chase is not McCluster; he is McCluster's clone.** The character formerly called Royce is **Benjamin Chase**: a government-program clone of McCluster (an engineered derivative born seven years after him), "low-key McCluster's evil twin". He is Benjamin Chase until he takes over the Hitman organisation at about 19; after that he is dubbed **"Hitman Benji"**. He is a separate person with his own life and choices, the Hitman sovereign and the Hitman-side RAID attacker. McCluster's own age-19-to-26 Hitman life (college, Bigga Lex, the studio, the retaliation, the refused chair) is McCluster's: "The retaliation he did was all him." ## Core character engine

McCluster in his Hitman years combines:
- hustler pattern recognition;
- cross-domain initiative;
- loyalty to people more than institutions;
- retaliatory escalation after perceived existential betrayal;
- discomfort with being owned by a system;
- and a recurring ability to recognize overlapping opportunity before other people categorize it.

His internal moral story is roughly:

> **I do not start with innocent people. But if somebody knowingly crosses me, what happens next is on them.**

The danger is that McCluster decides for himself who has forfeited mercy.

McCluster's refusal is partly protective: Lex has children and McCluster does not want accepting the chair to create a reason Lex must return to danger for him.

The refusal is also morally complicated because McCluster knows Benji wants power much more aggressively.
