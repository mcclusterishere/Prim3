## Core character engine

Jordan/Benji combines:
- hustler pattern recognition;
- cross-domain initiative;
- loyalty to people more than institutions;
- retaliatory escalation after perceived existential betrayal;
- discomfort with being owned by a system;
- and a recurring ability to recognize overlapping opportunity before other people categorize it.

His internal moral story is roughly:

> **I do not start with innocent people. But if somebody knowingly crosses me, what happens next is on them.**

The danger is that Benji decides for himself who has forfeited mercy.

Jordan's refusal is partly protective: Lex has children and Jordan does not want accepting the chair to create a reason Lex must return to danger for him.

The refusal is also morally complicated because Jordan knows Royce wants power much more aggressively.
