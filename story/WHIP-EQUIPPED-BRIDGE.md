# Whip Equipped — the prequel bridge (McCluster, age 26–33)

**Owner decision (2026-11-02): Whip Equipped is the prequel.** It covers McCluster's life from about 26 to 33, the years between leaving Hitman and entering Equity Uprise. It happens **before** PRIM3. It is not a sequel, not a branch ending, and not reached by killing anyone.

Version 1.1.0 of this file called Whip Equipped the bridge "from the kill-Prime path", with the entry condition "Kill Prime (either play side) → dimension → kill alt-self → Bridgeport lot", and said the economy was broken because "that world's Prime" had been killed. All of that is withdrawn. It contradicted `CANON.md` §8–§9 and `ENDING-BRANCHES.md`, which already made Whip Equipped the age-26–33 prequel.

Full game/app bible = later. Continuity + brand + first song locked here / in `whip-equipped/`.

## Where it sits

```text
AGE 19–26   HITMAN          McCluster refuses Bigga Lex's chair; Benji takes it
AGE 26–33   WHIP EQUIPPED   the Ghost Years: vehicles, mobility, infrastructure work, the woman, the plane
AGE 33–40   PRIM3           haircut to waves, college, Equity Uprise, succession
```

Release order runs backward (PRIM3, then Whip Equipped, then Hitman). Story order runs forward. PRIM3 may hint at Whip Equipped through habits and objects, never through exposition (`CANON.md` §8: "Background truth is not disclosure permission").

## Geographic lock
- **Bridgeport, Connecticut** — real geography for racing / gig map

## The parking lot
He **sleeps in the same parking lot** where he used to **sell** in the prior life: the Hitman years, when Bigga Lex found him selling parking at Vibes in Bridgeport (`MCCLUSTER-HITMAN-ORIGIN-CANON.md`). Same asphalt. The car is home through winter. This is the Ghost Years' vehicle living and mobile homelessness (`CANON.md` §9), not the aftermath of any killing.

## How it ends, and how PRIM3 begins
- The Ghost Years end with the private-aircraft event and McCluster's decision to live a fixed, conventional life.
- At 33 he cuts his long hair to waves and enrolls in college; Equity Uprise is the institution he joins (`CANON.md` §10, §19).
- PRIM2 has been watching since the Ghost Years; Site 0's identity update reacquires him (S1E1 `The Blip`).
- Whip Equipped music (Salvage Title, Tears Don't Fall and the rest) belongs to this era and is not reassigned to PRIM3 (`MCCLUSTER-PSYCHOLOGICAL-BACKGROUND.md` §20).
