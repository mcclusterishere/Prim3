Whip Equipped is McCluster's **age-26-to-33 prequel**, the Ghost Years. It is not entered by killing PRIM2, shifting dimensions, or killing an alternate self. That ending is retired.

See `ENDING-BRANCHES.md`, `../CANON.md`, and `MCCLUSTER-HITMAN-ORIGIN-CANON.md`.
