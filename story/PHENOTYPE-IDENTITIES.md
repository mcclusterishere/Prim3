# Phenotype identities

**Phenotype = role in the system. Character = person. Wear = clothing.**

This table gives every recurring Prim3 role at least two human faces where practical so the world does not feel like one person per job. Full personal histories for the Prim3/McCluster cast live in `CAST-NATIONS.md`; Hitman personal histories live in `HITMAN-CAST.md`.

Root `CANON.md` wins if any proposed identity conflicts with locked canon.

## Shared apex

| Role | Primary identity | Secondary / succession pressure | Personal tension |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prime** | **Presiding Prime / Prime-00** | **Jordan Vale / future Prime Negative One** | Savant seeking a worthy end versus survivor who does not yet know he is being trained to provide it. |

## Usage rule

When an episode needs a role, use an existing person before inventing a new one. New recurring characters should exist because a new human relationship or discipline is required, not because a writer forgot the table.
