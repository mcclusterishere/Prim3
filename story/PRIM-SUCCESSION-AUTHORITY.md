# PRIM — Succession & Numbering Authority

## Core definition

**PRIM** is an acronym for:

> **Primary Root Identity Mantle**

The PRIM is not merely a job title. It is the highest-trust human authority role in the hidden McCluster / Site 0 command architecture.

The word **Mantle** is deliberate: the authority is inherited. A holder does not simply receive a promotion; they receive the living identity, privileges, obligations and command burden of the office.

The number identifies the succession generation of the holder.

---

# 1. Canonical succession sequence

The sequence is:

- **PRIM0** — first holder / founder-generation leader
- **PRIM1** — second holder
- **PRIM2** — third holder and the presiding PRIM during almost the entirety of the PRIM3 show
- **PRIM3** — Jordan Vale after succession

Therefore the man previously called **Presiding Prime / Prime-00** in older drafts is canonically **PRIM2**.

Legacy references to `Prime-00`, `Prime-0`, `Prime`, `Presiding Prime` or similar language should be interpreted as references to **PRIM2** when they describe Jordan's predecessor during the age-33-to-40 PRIM3 story.

`Prime-00` is deprecated continuity language and does **not** mean the first PRIM holder.

---

# 2. Why the show is called PRIM3

The television/game/story franchise remains titled **PRIM3** even though Jordan does not hold the Mantle at the beginning.

The title names the destination of the story.

The seven-season PRIM3 narrative is the story of the succession path that ultimately produces **PRIM3: Jordan Vale**.

For most of the early story, the audience is not required to understand what the number means.

The title can initially read as:

- a brand;
- a program name;
- a stylized technology title;
- or an unexplained series designation.

Only later does the audience learn that the `3` is a **succession index**.

Canonical reveal principle:

> **The audience has been looking at Jordan's eventual title since Episode 1 without knowing it.**

# 4. Meaning of the acronym

## Primary

There may be many identities, credentials, operators, directors and service principals beneath the system, but one identity is ultimately treated as the **primary authority of last resort**.

## Root

The Mantle sits at the root of the trust hierarchy.

When lower-level authority conflicts cannot resolve, the architecture ultimately traces upward toward the PRIM identity.

This explains why Hana Lee's `Living Root` hypothesis is so dangerous: she is independently approaching the actual meaning of the PRIM role without knowing its formal name.

## Identity

The office is tied to a continuously validated **living human identity**, not merely to a password, account, token or committee vote.

That identity can authorize systems, personnel and cross-domain actions that ordinary administrators cannot.

## Mantle

The authority can pass to a successor.

The Mantle includes:

- recognition by the command architecture;
- highest-trust identity status;
- succession obligations;
- command privileges;
- institutional responsibility;
- and the burden of becoming the person onto whom otherwise distributed authority collapses.

The Mantle is therefore simultaneously:

- a technical trust object;
- a political office;
- an operational command role;
- and a psychological burden.

---

# 5. The number belongs to the holder, not the software version

The numeral after PRIM does **not** mean:

- software version;
- Site 0 revision;
- cohort number;
- mission generation;
- corporate restructuring;
- or season number.

It means only:

> **Which human succession holder is this?**

Numbering begins at zero because the original architecture was designed by technical people and treated the founding holder as generation/index `0`.

Thus:

```text
FOUNDING HOLDER       = PRIM0
FIRST SUCCESSOR       = PRIM1
SECOND SUCCESSOR      = PRIM2
THIRD SUCCESSOR       = PRIM3 / JORDAN VALE
```

---

# 6. PRIM0

PRIM0 is the first holder and founder-generation authority.

PRIM0 establishes the original concept that a distributed system still requires a final accountable human trust root.

PRIM0's civilian identity, exact era, nationality, death/retirement circumstances and relationship to later Site 0 architecture remain open until separately developed.

Important structural fact:

> **PRIM0 creates the problem every later PRIM inherits: how can one human remain accountable for a system too large for one human to personally understand?**

---

# 7. PRIM1

PRIM1 is the first true succession proof.

The transition from PRIM0 to PRIM1 demonstrates that the Mantle can move from one living identity to another while preserving institutional continuity.

PRIM1's identity and complete history remain deliberately open.

PRIM1 should eventually matter because the first succession establishes doctrines, rituals, technical assumptions and hidden precedent that PRIM2 later inherits.

PRIM1 must not become retroactively irrelevant merely because the current show focuses on PRIM2 and PRIM3.

---

# 8. PRIM2 — Jordan's predecessor

The current presiding leader during the main PRIM3 chronology is **PRIM2**.

PRIM2 is the person previously called Presiding Prime / Prime-00.

Existing locked traits transfer to PRIM2:

- dream-AI / distributed cognition architecture;
- Site 0 command anchoring;
- hidden coordination across McCluster / PRIM and Hitman;
- long-term observation of Jordan during the Ghost Years;
- exploitation of Hana Lee as an unwitting adversarial red team;
- manipulation of information boundaries rather than simplistic direct control;
- genuine resistance during RAID;
- inability to simply resign the Mantle;
- and eventual privilege reduction into Ghost after Jordan's succession.

PRIM2 is therefore the **third human to hold the Mantle**.

His exact civilian identity remains intentionally unresolved unless later owner canon changes that.

---

# 9. PRIM3 — Jordan Vale

Jordan Vale becomes **PRIM3** only after the final succession event.

He does not begin the series as PRIM3.

He earns, reaches, defeats, inherits and then redefines the Mantle.

The title of the show therefore has an additional meaning:

> **PRIM3 is not the organization Jordan joins. PRIM3 is the person Jordan becomes.**

This does not mean Jordan becomes psychologically identical to PRIM2.

Jordan's central post-succession challenge is whether he can preserve the useful parts of a unified trust architecture without reproducing PRIM2's belief that capability justifies unilateral control.

---

# 10. Succession mechanics at the story level

The exact cryptographic / biometric implementation can remain abstract unless a mission needs it.

At story level, succession requires:

1. a worthy challenger reaches the presiding PRIM;
2. the presiding PRIM genuinely resists rather than ceremonially resigning;
3. the challenger proves sufficient operational and psychological capacity to assume the burden;
4. the existing living root remains valid long enough for the architecture to recognize and transfer authority;
5. the outgoing PRIM's autonomous command privileges are revoked or reduced;
6. the incoming holder becomes the new highest-trust identity.

This preserves the existing RAID / Evil Twin / Ghost structure.

The post-RAID transition is therefore:

```text
PRIM2 — CURRENT ROOT
        ↓
DEFEATED BUT STILL BIOMETRICALLY / SYSTEMICALLY VALID
        ↓
SUCCESSION WINDOW
        ↓
JORDAN RECOGNIZED
        ↓
PRIM3 — NEW ROOT
        ↓
PRIM2 WRITE / COMMAND PRIVILEGES REVOKED
        ↓
FORMER PRIM2 PERSISTS AS GHOST-CLASS ADVISORY INTELLIGENCE
```

---

# 11. Hana Lee continuity

Hana's hidden investigation becomes even more coherent under this terminology.

She does not know the acronym PRIM.

She independently infers a **Living Root** because she observes that supposedly independent McCluster systems behave as though a continuously authenticated human identity sits above them.

Writer truth:

> **The Living Root Hana is searching for is PRIM2.**

If she had survived long enough to complete the investigation, she could have discovered not just a mysterious leader but the existence of the **Primary Root Identity Mantle** itself.

This is why her work threatens PRIM2 at the architectural level.

---

# 12. Jordan / PRIM2 thematic contrast

PRIM2 and Jordan are not merely old leader and new leader.

They represent competing answers to the same systems problem.

PRIM2's answer increasingly becomes:

> **If one person can see enough of the system, that person should be allowed to control enough of the system to keep it coherent.**

Jordan's growth must ultimately challenge that premise:

> **Seeing the whole system does not make every human inside the system yours to decide for.**

The succession is successful only if PRIM3 is not simply a younger PRIM2.

Preferred formal styling:

- **PRIM3**

No space is required in brand/title styling.

For highly technical internal records, `PRIM-2` / `PRIM-3` may appear as machine-readable identifiers, but consumer-facing story branding should prefer `PRIM2` and `PRIM3`.

The show remains styled:

> **PRIM3**

not `PRIME 3` and not `PRIM 3`.

# 14. Spoiler law

Do not explain the succession numbering too early.

Early-series characters can use `Prime` conversationally without revealing that the holder is formally PRIM2.

Early UI can obscure, truncate, compartmentalize or contextualize formal designation where narratively justified.

The reveal should progressively establish:

```text
"PRIME" SOUNDS LIKE A TITLE
        ↓
PRIM IS ACTUALLY AN ACRONYM
        ↓
THERE HAVE BEEN MULTIPLE HOLDERS
        ↓
THE CURRENT HOLDER IS PRIM2
        ↓
THE NUMBER IS A SUCCESSION INDEX
        ↓
PRIM3 HAS ALWAYS MEANT JORDAN
```

The final realization should cause the series title itself to be reinterpreted.
