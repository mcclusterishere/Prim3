# Ending branches

## Path A — Ghost (default myth)
Protagonist does **not** kill Prime.  
Uploads Prime into the system → **Ghost in the Wires**.  
Forever mentor. Shared rule of the machine. Prim3 continues under that tension.

## Path B — Kill Prime (player choice)
Protagonist kills Prime, believing that is how **Prim3 stays alive** and the world gets better.

### Immediate consequence
- Dimension shift
- Protagonist confronts and **kills an alternate version of himself** (same age)
- Alone on the other side

### Sequel product — **Whip Equipped**
| Layer | What it is |
|-------|------------|
| **World** | Worse future: in that universe Prime was killed years earlier; economy in freefall |
| **Protagonist life** | Delivery / gig driver living in his car; sleeps in the whip through winter to survive |
| **Game** | Mobile car game — street racing, shady dealings, Need-for-Speed energy |
| **Story** | Gig worker → own dealership through grit and dirt |
| **Real product** | Gig delivery + rental platform brand **Whip Equipped** (owner project) |
| **Watch** | Car movie / series lane under the same name |

### Who sees Path B
Players who choose **kill Prime / kill Prim3 leadership** on **either** playable side (Prim3 lane or Hitman lane) can be routed into this Hitman-season / series end sting that opens Whip Equipped.

### Rule
Path A and Path B are both real. Ghost is the “they rule the wires” myth. Kill is the “you inherit a colder America and a car” myth. Do not soft-lock only one without player agency where the product offers the choice.

Whip Equipped full bible = **later**. This file only locks the bridge.
