# Narrative Development Layers

Think of PRIM3 as a body under construction.

## Bone — already present
Canon, organizations, Prime succession, Site 0, technology curriculum, major ending branches, song concepts.

## Tendons — now required
Cause-and-effect links between canon events. Why one scene creates the next. Why a relationship changes. Why an episode cannot simply reset.

## Muscle — next build
Full recurring cast with individual voices, backstories, desires, fears, contradictions, friendships, rivalries, romances, money problems, family obligations, technical specialties, and meaningful flaws.

## Skin — episode expression
Actual scenes: classrooms, group chats, commutes, meals, work shifts, flirtation, arguments, mission prep, debriefs, grief, jokes, celebrations, and ordinary downtime.

## Nervous system — continuity
A living relationship state that carries information across the whole series: who knows what, who trusts whom, who owes whom, who has lied, who has been injured, who is grieving, who has been promoted, and what private information can detonate later.

## Heart — theme
A man returns to school seeking legitimacy and stability, enters an institution that truly recognizes his ability, and slowly discovers that being seen by powerful systems can become another form of captivity.

Every future story addition should strengthen at least one of these layers.
