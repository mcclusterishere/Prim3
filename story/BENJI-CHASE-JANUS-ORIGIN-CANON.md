# Benjamin "Benji" Chase — the JANUS origin

> **Fiction.** This is fictional thriller narrative. It is not a claim about any real government, genetic programme, school, family or person. "JANUS" is a working in-world codename. The real platform account "Hitman Benji / Benji Chase" belongs to the owner's fiction; nothing here describes a real individual.

## Owner decision (2026-11-03)

Owner: "Hitman Benji is actually… Royce. Rename Royce Benjamin Chase … Benji is low-key McCluster's evil twin … Royce's origin is a government program. He is a clone of McCluster … He gets dubbed Hitman Benji after he takes over the organization."

Locked by that decision:
- The character called **Royce** in canon from September to November 2026 is **Benjamin Chase**.
- He is a **clone of McCluster** produced by a **government programme**: McCluster's evil twin.
- He is **Benjamin Chase** until he takes over the Hitman organisation (at about 19, when McCluster refuses Bigga Lex's chair). After the takeover he is dubbed **"Hitman Benji"**. Short form in canon prose: **Benji**.
- He is a **separate person**. He is not McCluster, not McCluster's alias, and not a programmable device. His choices are his.
- Everything canon already said about Royce stays true of him: seven years younger than McCluster; the chair McCluster refused; the Hitman sovereign through PRIM3; the Hitman-side RAID element; the near-kill, alliance and joint defeat of PRIM2; survival as an autonomous counterweight after Ghost.
- McCluster's own Hitman-era deeds, including the New Mexico retaliation, are McCluster's: "The retaliation he did was all him."

**Machine keys.** Game data, schemas and mission YAMLs keep their `royce_*` keys (`royce_incursion`, `royce_incursions_max_per_season`, `royce:` blocks, `royceIntervention`). They refer to Benji. They were not renamed so that no reference breaks.

## One-sentence premise

The man McCluster knows as his dangerously ambitious younger Hitman rival is an engineered biological **derivative of McCluster**, created from an archived early-life sample by a covert, government-linked continuity programme. Benji knows he was shaped for something; he does **not** know his source is the man who refused the chair he took.

## Origin: the file existed before the man

Adapted from the branch. Point 1 to 5 are CANON with the owner decision; point 6 is PROPOSED.

1. McCluster's birth creates a match in a hidden historical lineage registry that claims to identify people with unusual compatibility for an experimental human-machine command/identity interface. The registry includes other family lines; its scientific validity is contested.
2. The programme archives a biological sample when McCluster is an infant.
3. When McCluster is about seven, the programme begins a gestation from that source and edits the derivative's visible phenotype, including pigmentation, so that he presents as white and will not be recognised as a duplicate. "Clone" is the characters' word; strictly he is an **engineered derivative**, which is why the two men do not look alike.
4. **Benjamin Chase is born seven years after McCluster** and ages normally. No accelerated ageing, no time contradiction.
5. He is raised through proxies, contractors and ordinary-seeming institutions. He gets a real upbringing: attachment, music, neighbourhoods, fear and ambition. The engineering does not decide his personality or morality.
6. *(PROPOSED)* The programme wanted an **alternate human root candidate** it could influence if the original refused enrolment or control. Whether Benji could ever be validated as a Mantle holder is unproven, and is one of Evil Twin's questions.

**Who ordered it** (PROPOSED, open): a government-linked legacy procurement/continuity directorate under the working codename JANUS. The specific person, agency and PRIM0/PRIM1-era sponsor remain open. Do not claim PRIM2 commissioned Benji's birth; PRIM2 later learns and exploits fragments of the archive.

## The Hitman collision

- When McCluster is 19, Benjamin is 12 and nowhere near the dorm story. Do not put him in it.
- By McCluster's mid-twenties, Benjamin Chase has arrived in Hitman's orbit: very young, charismatic, volatile and unnervingly disciplined. Some habits (clean recall, precise debrief language, access he cannot explain) do not fit anyone around him.
- *(PROPOSED)* **Brandon**, a historical Hitman-era figure, tells McCluster the scene around Benjamin "is feeling 'federal'". He sees irregularities, not lab evidence. Not a real-person allegation; surname and role unassigned.
- When Lex retires, **McCluster refuses** the chair. He does not appoint Benjamin; by refusing and not stopping the succession, he **allows** Benjamin's ascent. Benjamin takes a chair he believes he earned, is dubbed **Hitman Benji**, and mistakes McCluster's refusal for contempt.

## A person, not a caricature

Benji's contrast with McCluster is built on **institutional conditioning versus personal choice**, never on a claim that one race is naturally more criminal or more civilised. He is an assertive, white-presenting Hitman figure with dangerous humour and real stage presence, counterweighted by intelligence, genuine loyalty, fear of disposability and competence at running a real organisation. He has ordinary Tuesdays, relationships and failures, and episodes McCluster is not in. He can love someone without faking it, disobey his makers, and choose restraint when it costs him.

He already finds being McCluster's "leftovers" intolerable. Learning that his body was derived from McCluster threatens something deeper: **was any accomplishment ever his own?** The truthful answer must be yes.
