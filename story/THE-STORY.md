# THE STORY — how it’s told

This is the spine. Episodes teach; this file is the **plot of the world**.

---

## World in one breath

**McCluster Corp** looks like a marketing company. It is a **holding company**: owns the **building and the land**, holds **equity** across a stack of companies. People work side by side while sitting on different subsidiaries. The mark is the **M** (white geometric M + red Dual Sight bars).

**Prim3** is how the serious work runs — systems, cohorts, field kits, curriculum in the songs.

**Prime** sits at the center. He does not simply sleep. In a **suspension tank** under the main compound, his dreams are wired into the house AI: while the body floats, **he is the machines**. He only wakes for a true emergency. The main site sits behind **several outer compounds**. To reach him you should have to burn through a hot-site stack. That almost never happens.

Above the street, two mountains climb toward him:

| Path | Nature | Play |
|------|--------|------|
| **Prim3** | Intellect, IT, institutional stealth | XCOM-style tactical |
| **Hitman** | Brute force, kill as religion, street multinational | FPS / mobile CoD energy |

Prime needs them both. Sometimes he points them at the same bone so the weak die and the cover stays loud. **Casualties are policy.**

---

## How the show is structured

**Form:** ~6-minute episodes · 3 per season · one song ≈ one episode · watch or play.

**Trick:** The series **opens at the end of the board.**

### OPEN — S1E1 High Alert (tip of RAID)

Prime is in the tank, running the world as dream-AI.  
**HIGH ALERT.** Outer sites are already wrong. **Main site** is under dual raid:

- **Protagonist** (Prim3 path)  
- **Hitman Benji** (brute path)  

He wakes. He knows who hired the road. **Some of those names are his** — contracts, equity, dual-board pieces that grew teeth. The song is **threat talk** at them while robots fire on his neuron clock (nerves = neurons; fire first = machines before flesh).

Every bar is a **double meaning** and a **memory plant**: flash of first hire, marketing tech-packs vs Hitman’s street “techs and packs,” gov/police dialing him for what they can’t do, EMP frying a kit, diversion ops, wire-nervous because he *is* the wire. The raiders climb while he talks shit; the audience is watching **clips of the whole war** inside one emergency.

Then the series does something strange: it **backs up**.

## MIDDLE — the seasons (how we got to the alarm)

After the open, episodes walk the **curriculum and the climb** — the same events High Alert already flashed.

**What Prime was doing in those memories (the seeds):**

| Memory | He was… |
|--------|---------|
| Scope of the work | Hiring / showing the real McCluster floor to someone who thought it was only marketing |
| Dial-first / no 9 | Sitting on **gov and police contracts** — the call for jobs states can’t touch |
| Tech packs / merch | Running the **marketing face** and real technology packs — dissing Hitman’s street promote |
| Dame / diversion / serpent | Running **social and dual-board tools** (the path that becomes Evil Twin’s confession) |
| Fry circuits | Proving software and EMP are weapons, not metaphors |
| Wire nervous | Living as the network in the tank |
| Paid in full | Building a cohort that is licensed; everyone else is trial |

**What the protagonist was doing:**  
Coming up inside Prim3 / McCluster — learning hats, OSINT, patch, apps, cloud, per diem — believing in the work until the board feels wrong. Missions for “the government” and the company. Cohort as family across subsidiaries.

**What Benji was doing (parallel Hitman lane):**  
Connecticut, young, nerd-savage, recruited out of school energy — drugs through college, initiation (**Hitshake**), street climb, outsmarts the pure street side and takes the corp in a short, violent arc. Hitman’s gospel is kill, giveaways, gang product. His path is the other half of the dual raid.

**Why they eventually aim at the same main site:**  
Prime’s dual-board theory, the hire chain, the cull, and the protagonist’s moral break (Prime as villain) collide with Benji’s takeover hunger. High Alert is that collision **already in progress**.

Episode songs are not random lessons. They are **chapters of the same machine** the pilot already showed on fire.

## LATE — the finale cluster

Order is locked:

### 1. RAID (Hot Site)
The full doctrine and the full assault. Multi-site theory made playable and visible. The emergency the pilot opened on is completed as structure: outer sites, hot site, main. Prime can be **defeated** here.

### 2. Evil Twin
**After** he is beaten — the **confession**.  
He explains the dual board: McCluster and Hitman, tools against each other and for each other, social engineering chains (SIM, diversion, “jewelry on the wrist”), why he embedded in both, why the greater good was the alibi. The hire chain that High Alert threatened is named in the dark.

### 3. Choice

**Spare him (greater good accepted) → Ghost in the Wires**  
He is not killed. Consciousness goes into the system. Forever mentor. Prim3 and the protagonist remain tangled with a ghost who already was the wire in the tank. “Prime and the protagonist rule the wires” myth.

**Kill him → Whip Equipped**  
Protagonist kills Prime to “save” Prim3 / the world. Dimension shift. Kills an alternate self. Wakes into a **worse America** where Prime died years earlier — economy broken. Gig driver living in a car, **same Bridgeport parking lot** energy, racing and shady climb toward a dealership. Brand/game/story: **Whip Equipped**.
