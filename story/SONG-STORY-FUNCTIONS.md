## Integration rule

For every track used in narrative development, define four separate fields:

- **Technical payload:** what real concept the song contains.
- **Human payload:** what emotion, attitude, temptation or conflict it naturally supports.
- **Story pressure:** what event could force characters to confront that concept.
- **Character ownership:** whose perspective or arc makes the song emotionally meaningful.

A track should never be assigned only because its technical topic matches a lesson.

The authoritative order is `EPISODE-PROGRESSION.md`. Individual expandable
chapters are indexed in `../episodes/README.md`.

## Examples of useful dramatic translation

- **High Alert:** vigilance is useful technically, but dramatically it belongs to a person who cannot turn vigilance off.
- **Patch Work:** patching systems can become a story about trying to repair trust after betrayal.
- **Installation Types:** technical replacement versus in-place upgrade can mirror whether a person reinvents themselves or carries old damage forward.
- **Cap Ex Vs. Op Ex:** ownership versus rental becomes a conflict about commitment, institutional control, and who bears long-term cost.
- **Per Diem:** hardware language supports an episode about temporary workers, disposable labor, pride, and who is considered permanent.
- **Data Breach:** the breach is not just a technical event; it can expose a private truth that permanently changes relationships.
- **RAID:** redundancy and disaster recovery become the architecture of the final assault and the question of whether PRIM2 designed his own defeat to be survivable.
- **Evil Twin:** duplicated identities, deceptive access, and parallel systems naturally support the revelation that apparently separate organizations have been manipulated through one hidden command structure.
- **Ghost In The Wires:** infrastructure becomes memory and presence; a person can be gone physically while remaining embedded in systems and habits.

## Continuity goal

By the end of development, a listener should be able to hear a track and remember not only the lesson but the character, relationship, loss, victory or betrayal associated with it.
