# McCluster Corp

McCluster Corp is the larger holding/operating ecosystem behind the visible Equity Uprise institution.

## Visible doorway

**McCluster does not join McCluster Corp at the start of PRIM3.**

He joins **Equity Uprise**.

Equity Uprise is the recurring workplace the audience knows: the building, cohorts, instructors, field teams, security, media, policy/research, executive command and technical operations.

## McCluster Corp

McCluster Corp sits behind and around that visible institution.

It may hold or coordinate:

- buildings and land;
- equity/brands/companies;
- infrastructure;
- logistics;
- technical operations;
- institutional partnerships;
- other assets revealed as story requires.

The exact subsidiary map remains reveal-gated.

## PRIM relationship

McCluster Corp is governed at its hidden highest-trust layer through the PRIM architecture.

During most of the series:

- PRIM2 is the presiding Mantle holder;
- Equity Uprise is one visible institutional node;
- McCluster is an Equity Uprise participant/operator whose path is being evaluated without full disclosure;
- validated succession eventually makes McCluster PRIM3.

The company name's relationship to McCluster is deliberate story pressure and should not be fully explained early.

## HQ distinction

- **Equity Uprise HQ** — recurring workplace/home of the ensemble.
- **Site 0 / Root HQ** — hidden PRIM/McCluster Corp root facility attacked in the final RAID.

They are not the same building.

## Production rule

Do not use “McCluster Corp office” as a generic substitute for the Equity Uprise workplace.

If a scene happens in the recurring building, name the Equity Uprise floor/room when practical.
