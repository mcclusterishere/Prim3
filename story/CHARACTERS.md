## Evil Twin operation — cast from the song + doctrine

Prime is speaking the op. Centralized command means **both Hitgirls and the kiosk kid serve one design** while each corp still thinks the objective is “theirs.”

### Beat (two-level escalation)

1. **Lower Hitman exec** is marked.  
2. **Hitman-side Hitgirl** (street/social) runs the bump: Insta/finsta, walk-past, phone screen drop because she’s hot, he lets it go.  
3. **Lil bro at the kiosk** — looks broke, is not — “fix phone cheap,” **SIM swap** (pre–eSIM era energy so the story carries).  
4. Access from lower exec → path to **higher sponsor / political money**.  
5. **McCluster-side Hitgirl — the Harvard gymnast** — is the face for the higher board (lunch with politicians, necklace cut, “ten I see”). Same centralized hand; she believes McCluster objective; Hitman pieces believe Hitman objective.  
6. They **worked as a team without knowing** — clandestine because command is Prime-only at the true center.
