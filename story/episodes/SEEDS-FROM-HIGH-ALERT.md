# Seeds from High Alert → every later episode

S1E1 is a **montage of the future and the past at once**.  
Each row is a **seed** the later episode must pay off or deepen.  
When that episode airs, the audience should feel *we already saw a flash of this when Prime was talking shit on the main site.*

**Progressive rule:** early seasons pay small; late seasons reveal why that jab was lethal.

---

## 01 — White Grey Black Hat
**Seed (from High Alert):** *“May not let you in if you decline searches.”*  
**Memory image:** Door / access denial — authorization is life or death on his ground.  
**Build toward:** Hats and boxes are not theory; they are who gets through the glass when Prime is awake.

## 02 — Anti Social Engineering
**Seed:** *“Another dame, another dime, another diversion.”*  
**Memory image:** A social diversion op in flash — pretty, cheap, effective.  
**Build toward:** Full human-layer curriculum; same energy Evil Twin will confess at scale.

## 03 — OSINT
**Seed:** *“Rest you may when you enter view of the scope of my work.”*  
**Memory image:** First time the real scope is *seen* — hire / interview / enter view.  
**Build toward:** OSINT as the discipline of entering view without permission; scope is a target package.

## 04 — Patch Work
**Seed:** *“I conduct myself so shockingly I fry circuits” + formula.*  
**Memory image:** NOW EMP / circuit kill on a raider kit; trust and validation inverted.  
**Build toward:** Patch, signing, validation — he already showed he can un-patch a life mid-fight.
