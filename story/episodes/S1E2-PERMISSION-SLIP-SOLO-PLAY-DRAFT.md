## 1. The episode in one paragraph

**A woman who does everything inside the rules finds the one question the rules have not answered, and has to earn the answer without pretending she already has it.** Aya is on camera explaining hot aisles for an onboarding film while a lawful task-force operation she is part of goes live around the shoot. Her permission is Nusrat's word over comms ("Scope is Zone C. Only C."). Her problem is a legitimate target that may hold up somebody else's service. Nusrat's growth (WATCH) is the confidence to make a bounded decision from a desk and stay answerable for it. Miguel's is keeping people safe at a door without lying to them about why it is closed. White-hat purpose, black-box knowledge: discovery changes what Aya knows; a signed extension changes what she may do; neither changes the other.

## 3. PLAY: Aya's solo

### 3.1 Shape

The Cover meter and its thresholds are proposal §3.4 values and are not restated. Separate layers on the board: **physical access**, **legal scope**, **observed knowledge**, **tactical control**. A movement tile means Aya can move there, not that she may inspect or change what is in the room. Scope status sits on the inspected object or action.

### 3.4 Four routes

- **Extension.** Needs the identified target and the observed warning. Aya names one question, one D action and the cost of refusal; "let us into everything" is invalid. Approval is deterministic against the evidence and the authored scope, never a roll. Nusrat's HOLD THE LINE (off-board) protects the decision window from one authored pressure event, not from physical danger. Grants one named observation, nothing more.
- **Inference.** Needs observations in authorized space that actually discriminate. Repeating one indicator is not corroboration. Slower, more protected work; Aya states the residual uncertainty. Inconclusive results may turn into a request or a partial withdrawal. The strongest route for "knowledge rises, authorization stays still".
- **Emergency.** Needs an authored, independently observed life-safety danger (Miguel's report on comms plus Aya's own second observation), necessity, proportionality and a logged exception. Clock pressure, annoyance or wanting to see D never qualify; a danger the player caused never earns the same integrity treatment. Grants only the F action.
- **Violation.** Needs the boundary shown and an explicit acknowledgement. A mis-tap, colour confusion or camera angle never triggers it. The knowledge is real and its provenance stays unauthorized. Review and compliance debt persist through success; Nusrat carries the review because the scope was hers to hold.

### 3.6 Outcomes

Objective, Team, Exposure, Integrity and Time stay separate; learning evidence is its own record. Resolve in order **collapse → partial → cowboy win → emergency save → lawful but costly → clean**; the first that applies wins. An unauthorized, unverified extraction is never a cowboy win, and a real emergency never scrubs an unrelated violation. Cover at extraction gives a separate aftermath variant (clean, noticed, burned). A "noticed" crew member is one of the F4 crew slots in the expanded-ensemble plan (§9) and stays unnamed until the creation gate.

**Collapse** ends the operation through exposure, lost evidence, restricted authority, injury or withdrawal. It never rolls a death. If the owner chooses a Season 1 death at E02 (§3.7 candidates: Miguel, Nusrat), it is authored into WATCH, not produced by a failure route.

### 3.7 Teaching inside the operation

The loop: see the problem → form a claim → act → observe → verify → explain what changed. Practice cards are dismissible; ordinary commands stay available.

- **A. Two axes.** Knowing a system is not permission to change it; "authorized, poorly understood" is not a contradiction.
- **B. Coordinates and route.** On a local tutorial grid (unitless; the game grid's 3.54 m per tile does not enter), from (2,2) to (5,4): +3 and +2, an unobstructed lower bound of 5 steps; two blocked squares force a legal route of 7. Both are right answers to different questions. Tile steps, straight-line distance and AP cost are never conflated.
- **C. Slope, optional.** Rise 2 over run 3 is a relation between changes, not an order to move diagonally through cover. The y-axis direction is always labelled.
- **D. Dependency reasoning.** Pick the observation that tells two explanations apart; a repeated dashboard is not independent. Prepares E03's corroboration work.
- **E. A bounded request.** Actor, action, object/zone, purpose, duration, constraints; which part is broader than the evidence. A fictional in-game lesson, not legal advice.
- **F. Validation after change.** "The action completed" and "the intended result is true" are two claims. Mastery comes from demonstrated reasoning, not from a clean tactical result.
