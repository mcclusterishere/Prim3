## Story (watch mode)

### Cold open
A resume is not a document — it’s a **view**. Someone is about to enter the scope of the work. Sleep on it and you miss the window. Cut to an operator who does not sleep on windows.

### Hook 1 — High Alert
Title meaning lands in body language: shoulders set, eyes scanning, voiceover or diegetic earpiece carrying the hook. This is not “first day nervous.” This is **operational nervous** — the good kind. Nerves that work.

### Verse — scope of the work
We learn who this person is without a Wikipedia scroll:

- **Dial-first** — when the building calls, it’s this number  
- Doesn’t need a “9”; is the **1 twice over**  
- Moves **tech packs**, designs **merch** — McCluster surface and Prim3 substance in the same hands

- Online noise is background; **grind is in person**

Visuals stay institutional: M on glass, Dual Sight only as a flash, no Hitman halo.
