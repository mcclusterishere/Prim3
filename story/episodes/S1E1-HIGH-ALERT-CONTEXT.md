# S1E1 — High Alert

## Episode clock

| Block | Time | Job |
|-------|------|-----|
| **A — Mission end (the song)** | **0:00–2:47** | High Alert. Prime’s pause. Dual raid on main site. Threat talk. Neuron/robot fire. |
| **B — Context (how we got here)** | **~2:47–6:00** (~3:13) | The path that made the impossible stack fail. Not a full season — the **immediate chain** that put Jordan and Benji on the same glass tonight. |

**Rule for the series:** song = **mission end-state**. Second half = **context that led up to that mission.**  
High Alert is the template: you see the emergency first, then why the door was already open.

You already locked this:

## Block B — Context (~3:13) — path to tonight

This is not “Jordan’s whole childhood.” It is the **short fuse** that turned two climbs into one address on the same night.

### Beat 1 — Two contracts, one bone (≈40s)

Split screen or hard cuts:

- **Prim3 paper:** Authority-03 / mission language that sends a cohort cell at a “hostile institutional node.” Jordan is on that paper. Chioma or Helen signed something that looks righteous.  
- **Hitman paper:** Sable / Royce-era or Benji’s new order — same geography described as a **score**, not a lesson. Benji takes it as coronation fuel.  

Audience feels it: **same bone, two logos.** Prime’s dual-board fingerprint without naming him yet.

### Beat 2 — Outer sites go soft (≈45s)

- Defense floor (Volkov / Bondarenko energy): alerts that should stay yellow.  
- One outer compound fails “training” pattern — Marcus or Emre line stressed.  
- Pylon reroutes; something in the stack accepts a lie.  

This is **why** main-site contact is even possible. Multi-site religion cracks in miniature.

### Beat 3 — Jordan’s last clean hour (≈40s)

- Bay with **Aya Shinoda**: seal holds, one millimeter of trust.  
- Jordan gets the go. She does not fully know the target is the tank’s house.  
- Optional: freelancer friend warns *faces on an old list* — he goes anyway.  

Human cost seed: he is not a stranger to the building he is about to raid.

### Beat 4 — Benji’s last clean hour (≈40s)

- Crew (Dez, Rae Twin, Kiosk peripheral): Hitman language — giveaway, pack, kill religion.  
- Benji frames the compound as the mountain top, not a classroom.  
- No handshake with Jordan. They are not allies on the ground. Same address only.  

### Beat 5 — The fuse meets (≈30s)

- Both teams cross the line where outer sites should have stopped them.  
- Map tiles flip.  
- Cut back toward the tank — **we now understand why Block A had to happen.**  

Sting: not resolution. **Permission to start Season 1’s climb episodes** knowing the house will burn.

## Explain it plain

**High Alert the song** is Prime, already underwater in the emergency — the **mission at its loudest.**  

**The rest of the episode** rewinds just far enough to show:

1. Both orgs got pointed at the same place  
2. Outer sites failed  
3. Jordan left a clean bay with Aya  
4. Benji left a dirty crew with hunger  
5. They arrived  

So when Season 1 continues into hats, OSINT, anti-social, you are not watching random lessons. You are watching **the kind of machine that can manufacture a night like High Alert** — and the people who still think they are only doing their job.
