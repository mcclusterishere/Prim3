# Prim3 secondary character dossiers

These are the **48 attached characters** from `CAST-NATIONS.md`: one lost legacy operator and one living outside/freelance connection for each of the 24 Prim3 mains. Their purpose is to make every main character arrive with history and remain connected to a world beyond McCluster.

Legacy characters are deceased before or around the main chronology unless later canon says otherwise. Their deaths are not spectacle; the point is the behavioral residue they leave behind.

## Attached to Jordan Vale

### Kwame Asante — Ghana — legacy
Former infrastructure mentor who treated documentation as respect for the next person touching the system. Patient, dryly funny, allergic to technical ego. **Want:** build apprentices who no longer need him. **Flaw:** stayed useful to organizations longer than they deserved. **Residue:** Jordan hears Kwame's voice whenever he is tempted to skip the record and “just fix it.”

### Sofia Berg — Sweden — living freelancer
Independent digital archivist with a minimalist life and maximal distrust of unverifiable claims. **Want:** records ordinary people can own, not merely request. **Flaw:** assumes institutions always act strategically when sometimes they are simply chaotic. **Connection:** she helps Jordan preserve evidence of his own history and refuses to be impressed by McCluster access.

## Attached to Aya Shinoda

### Hana Lee — South Korea — legacy
Robotics engineer who taught Aya that graceful failure is a design requirement, not a nice-to-have. **Want:** machines that reveal their uncertainty. **Flaw:** perfectionism made her slow to ask for help. **Residue:** Aya repeats Hana's design phrases under stress without acknowledging where they came from.

### Tariq Al-Farsi — Saudi Arabia — living freelancer
Aviation-systems contractor and Aya's old research friend. Sociable where Aya is reserved, practical where she can become obsessive. **Want:** keep engineering connected to ordinary life. **Flaw:** dismisses institutional politics until they matter. **Connection:** reminds Aya she once expected a career where the hardest problem was technical.

## Attached to Arjun Mehta

### Priyan Perera — Sri Lanka — legacy

### Fatima Zahra — Morocco — living freelancer
Structural engineer who prefers ugly workable solutions to impressive fragile ones. **Want:** independence from prestige-driven clients. **Flaw:** leaves collaborations too quickly when she senses politics. **Connection:** she is the person Arjun calls when he needs an answer from somebody who owes McCluster nothing.

# Secondary-cast rule

A secondary character enters a scene because they create **pressure, history, consequence or an outside life** for a main character. Do not use the global roster as decorative diversity. If a secondary appears, the audience should understand what changes in the main character because that specific person exists.
