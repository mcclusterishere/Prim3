## Writing law

Every episode blueprint must identify:

- workplace floor(s);
- named recurring workspaces naturally active there;
- ordinary work interrupted by the episode;
- which background archetypes are present;
- what visible workplace state is written forward afterward.

The workplace is not a mission-select menu. Characters have routines before the plot arrives.

## Song law

A technical song should be associated with the place where the concept is experienced, diagnosed, debated, repaired, governed or communicated.

F4 can be used for literal recording/media production without forcing every song's story event to occur on F4.

## Site 0 law

S7 endgame material may move to Site 0 because Site 0 is the hidden root HQ.

Do not retroactively redefine Equity Uprise as Site 0.

> **Equity Uprise is home. Site 0 is the inheritance conflict.**
