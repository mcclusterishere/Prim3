# PRIM3

**PRIM3** is a seven-season adult coming-of-age techno-thriller and infotainment universe.

At 33, a broke first-year college student—fresh out of homelessness, carrying years of real IT and data-center experience but no degree—is recruited into an apparently innocent corporation. Across seven academic years, he advances toward Ph.D.-level mastery, survives covert missions, watches new cohorts arrive and disappear, and moves closer to the most powerful and unwanted position on Earth: **Prime**.

The story opens at the end: Agent Prime Negative One seals his helmet while a recruit he selected raids his facility to execute him and inherit the role. Then the story returns to his first day of college.

## Core promise

- Seven seasons; one academic year per season.
- Adult coming-of-age: from an adult in his prime to an adult in his ultimate prime.
- Character-driven serialized drama where meaningful characters can die.
- Real information technology and biotechnology concepts taught through story problems.
- AI-assisted visual production: character design, photographic scenes, animation, music and episodic assembly.
- Canon stored openly and persistently in this repository.

## Canon anchors

- The protagonist enters college at 33 after homelessness.
- He has extensive practical IT/data-center knowledge without the degree that validates it.
- The presiding Prime was recruited at 33 and seized the role in under nine months, overriding the system's age-40 rule.
- Prime cannot resign. Succession requires a worthy candidate to execute the resisting operator.
- The presiding Prime secretly selects new talent every year and accompanies cohorts on extremely low-survival missions.
- Candidate attrition occurs primarily through mission failure—not routine internal assassination.
- Site 0 is the eventual destination, burden and prison; it is not the protagonist's starting point.
- The exact M-shaped helmet architecture, visible forehead camera, smoked visor, red `//` bars and rear Mission Core belong to Prime's later visual canon.
