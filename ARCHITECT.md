## 3. PRIM3 narrative architecture

**7 academic years = 7 seasons.**  
**3 episodes per season = 21 episodes.**  
**1 song = 1 episode = 1 mission.**

Each episode is approximately **9 minutes**:

1. **Music film (~3 min)** — technical/emotional thesis
2. **Human story (~3 min)** — character pressure and continuity
3. **Mission (~3 min)** — WATCH authored action or PLAY gamified version

Jordan leaves between academic years for recurring **summer policy fellowships**. Those summers change his worldview but are not added to PRIM3's 21-episode count. Equity Uprise may later become the separate show/game lane that tells those summers.

Current final trilogy:

**RAID (HOT SITE) → Evil Twin → Ghost In The Wires**

Episode 18 / Season 6 Episode 3 remains open for owner Song #21.

## 6. Game rule

Prim3 missions use graded outcomes rather than false binary failure.

PLAY may modify:
- objective grade
- team condition
- exposure
- integrity/compliance
- time
- relationship/debrief state

WATCH and PLAY still resolve the same major story event unless a branch is explicitly canonized.

## 7. Non-negotiables

- All owner context belongs on **main**
- One song = one episode = one mission
- 7 seasons × 3 episodes = 21
- Summers are policy-fellowship continuity, not extra Prim3 episodes
- Graded outcomes, not false binary fail
- Human story must matter independently of the technical lesson
- Hitman ≠ IT firm
- Ghost ≠ kill path
