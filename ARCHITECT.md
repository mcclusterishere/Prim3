## 3. PRIM3 narrative architecture

**7 academic years = 7 seasons.**  
**3 episodes per season = 21 episodes.**  
**1 song = 1 episode = 1 canonical mission.**

WATCH is longform and flexible. The default planning band is about **20–35 minutes**. It is not capped at nine minutes, and it is not three equal three-minute blocks.

Causal grammar:

1. **Human trigger / cold open**
2. **Music film / operational brief**
3. **Human escalation / mission threshold**
4. **WATCH mission** — the authored canonical action path

PLAY expands the same event. The player does not become McCluster in ordinary pre-succession missions.

McCluster leaves between academic years for recurring **summer policy fellowships**. Those summers change his worldview but are not added to PRIM3's 21-episode count. They may later support a separate Equity Uprise lane.

Current final trilogy:

**RAID (HOT SITE) → Evil Twin → Ghost In The Wires**

Episode 18 / Season 6 Episode 3 remains open for owner Song #21. Its story function is locked in `CANON.md`.

See `story/EPISODE-PROGRESSION.md`, `story/EPISODE-MISSION-BIBLE.md`, `story/THE-STORY.md`.

## 6. Game rule

Prim3 missions use graded outcomes rather than false binary failure.

Ordinary PLAY launches without McCluster. He is a limited Wildcard call, not the default pawn.

PLAY may modify:
- objective grade
- team condition
- exposure
- integrity/compliance
- time
- relationship/debrief state

WATCH and PLAY still resolve the same major story event unless a branch is explicitly canonized.

## 7. Non-negotiables

- All owner context belongs on **main**
- One song = one episode = one canonical mission
- 7 seasons × 3 episodes = 21
- Summers are policy-fellowship continuity, not extra Prim3 episodes
- WATCH is longform. Do not revive the nine-minute cap
- Graded outcomes, not false binary fail
