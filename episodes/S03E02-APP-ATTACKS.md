# S3E2 - APP ATTACKS

## Merge contract

This chapter preserves the locked result: Jordan disobeys Chioma, the workaround
saves the operation, and that success damages their relationship. Gameplay may
grade the method and fallout but cannot rewrite the central conflict into easy
agreement.

## Chapter promise

A dispatch/logistics application begins failing during an active operation.
Jordan sees a fast workaround outside Chioma's conservative plan. The player
must keep essential work moving while deciding how much authority a correct
technical instinct earns.

## Song payload

- **Technical:** privilege boundaries, role-based access, input validation,
  content security, injection families as risk categories, code review,
  debugging, and error handling.
- **Human:** compulsion, ego, divided allegiance, and the thrill of proving a
  system is weaker than its owners admit.
- **Visual:** hostile inputs becoming physical pressure on doors, queues, and
  dispatch lanes; errors appearing as fractures in the operational picture.
- **Safety:** the player isolates components, validates data, and moves work to
  a safe path. Named offensive tools from the lyrics remain references, not
  usable mechanics.

## Nine-minute episode

### 0:00-3:00 - Music film

Application layers appear as rooms stacked behind one screen. Every malformed
input changes a physical room until privileged space is exposed. Jordan moves
through the result while Chioma's voice repeats the boundary he is about to cross.

### 3:00-6:00 - Human story

The breach aftermath has made Chioma more conservative and Jordan less willing
to trust institutional delay. They agree on the technical danger and disagree
on who owns the decision. Aya sees both of them converting fear into certainty.

### 6:00-9:00 - Watch / Play mission

WATCH has Jordan route essential dispatch work through a safer reduced-function
path after Chioma orders containment. It works, but he acts before obtaining
her approval. PLAY requires component isolation, privilege protection, input
classification, safe-mode routing, and an explicit command decision.

## Playable level contract

- **Player role:** Jordan operating under Chioma's incident command.
- **Environment:** dispatch floor, loading routes, and a layered application map.
- **Primary objective:** preserve critical dispatch while isolating the affected
  application component.
- **Optional objectives:** maintain evidence; avoid granting broad privileges;
  communicate deviation before it becomes irreversible.
- **Complication:** waiting for the safest path harms the active operation;
  acting quickly can violate command and expand exposure.
- **Exit state:** the service works; the chain of command does not feel repaired.

## Player verbs

Inspect, isolate, validate, assign role, route, request approval, deviate, verify.

## Graded outcomes

| Channel | Strong result | Weak but continuing result |

| Time | Safe mode activates before backlog peaks | Delay causes operational harm |

## Persistent state written by the level

Jordan earns capability credit while Chioma trust falls according to how the
deviation is handled. Aya's mediation burden rises. The workaround becomes a
future precedent Prime can cite to justify selecting Jordan.

## Prime background trace

- **Visible trace:** one second before Jordan's reduced-function workaround
  succeeds, a hidden authorization layer changes from `BLOCK` to `TEMPORARY
  EXCEPTION - P-00`.
- **First-watch meaning:** automatic emergency failover or an unseen senior
  operator cleared the path.
- **Ghost payoff:** Jordan's celebrated disobedience occurred inside a boundary
  Prime silently opened; Chioma was never shown the exception.

## Technology accuracy gate

- **Sensor / input:** application errors, audit events, user reports, queue and
  dispatch health.
- **Compute / reasoning:** isolate failing boundaries, compare safe-mode output,
  and validate assumptions with test cases.
- **Energy / infrastructure:** application service, identity layer, database,
  operator terminals, and field communications.
- **Access / authorization:** role-scoped responder permissions; command
  deviation is a human governance choice, not a hidden superpower.
- **Human decision owner:** Chioma; Jordan owns any deliberate deviation and its
  documentation.
- **Failure mode:** unsafe inputs reach privileged functions, broad access is
  granted, or the operation stops entirely.
- **Real concept taught:** robust applications validate inputs, constrain
  privileges, and retain a safe operating path.
- **Fictional extrapolation:** interface visualization only.
