# S3E1 - DATA BREACH

## Merge contract

This chapter implements the locked Episode 07 placement and preserves the
revelation that Jordan finds a pre-recruitment file about himself. The source
package already includes the project's only complete MP3/SRT/TTML/distribution
set and should become the first end-to-end music integration test.

## Chapter promise

A compromise of McCluster's personnel/training system turns incident response
into a room full of people watching their own private lives become categories
on a screen. Jordan must contain the service while discovering the institution
collected him before he joined it.

## Song payload

- **Technical:** breach indicators, ransomware behavior, physical access,
  password attacks, MFA, supply-chain risk, abnormal transfers, access logs,
  and proactive response.
- **Human:** exposure, shame, institutional secrecy, and the fear that a record
  can control how others see you.
- **Visual:** inaccessible files, duplicated profiles, redacted biographies,
  and personal memories rendered as rows in an incident queue.
- **Safety:** response centers on containment, evidence, notification, and
  defensive controls; no attack path is made executable.

## Nine-minute episode

### 0:00-3:00 - Music film

The completed track becomes the first production-standard sequence: timed
lyrics appear as incident indicators while the same human face is fragmented
across badge data, messages, training scores, and surveillance stills.

### 3:00-6:00 - Human story

The cohort learns McCluster retained information far beyond ordinary employment
records. People argue about whether privacy matters during an emergency. Jordan
opens a file dated before his application and understands recognition may have
been surveillance.

### 6:00-9:00 - Watch / Play mission

WATCH contains the affected environment while Jordan preserves the file that
changes his understanding of recruitment. PLAY requires the player to classify
affected data, isolate services, preserve evidence, and prioritize people who
need warning without destroying the trail.

## Playable level contract

- **Player role:** Jordan inside a small incident-response team.
- **Environment:** operations center plus abstracted personnel-system service
  map and a secure evidence room.
- **Primary objective:** contain the breach and establish a defensible impact
  scope.
- **Optional objectives:** protect notification data; keep essential training
  operations available; preserve Jordan's pre-recruitment record lawfully.
- **Complication:** containment actions can erase volatile evidence or expose
  more personal information to responders.
- **Exit state:** immediate spread is stopped; trust inside the cohort is not.

## Player verbs

Inspect, classify, isolate, preserve, prioritize, authorize, notify, debrief.

## Graded outcomes

| Channel | Strong result | Weak but continuing result |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Scope established and spread contained | Services isolated with uncertain scope |
| Team | Affected people are protected and informed | Rumors reach them before official notice |
| Exposure | Need-to-know access is maintained | Response itself spreads private details |
| Integrity | Evidence and notification duties preserved | Hasty wiping destroys part of the trail |
| Time | High-risk systems contained first | Low-value systems consume the response |

## Persistent state written by the level

Jordan gains the pre-recruitment evidence flag. Cohort trust in McCluster falls.
Notification quality changes later willingness to report mistakes in `Patient
Zero`. The complete song package establishes the media-manifest pattern later
tracks should copy.

## Prime background trace

- **Visible trace:** Jordan's pre-recruitment dossier contains a tiny `P-00`
  authorization mark in its metadata pane.
- **First-watch meaning:** an unfamiliar legacy approval or records code.
- **Ghost payoff:** Presiding Prime personally signed Jordan into the candidate
  architecture before Jordan knew Prime existed.

## Technology accuracy gate

- **Sensor / input:** service alerts, file-access behavior, transfer telemetry,
  identity logs, staff reports.
- **Compute / reasoning:** correlate indicators, establish time windows, and
  classify impact with explicit uncertainty.
- **Energy / infrastructure:** personnel services, identity provider, network,
  backups, and secure evidence storage.
- **Access / authorization:** incident-response role; personal records are
  accessed only to determine impact and notification.
- **Human decision owner:** incident commander with privacy/legal owner input.
- **Failure mode:** uncontrolled spread, premature restoration, evidence loss,
  or response-driven privacy harm.
- **Real concept taught:** containment and evidence preservation are distinct
  from immediately restoring every service.
- **Fictional extrapolation:** none required.
