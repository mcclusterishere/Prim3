# PRIM3 — Living-Cast Episode Migration Audit

## Purpose

This audit records the September 10, 2026 review of **every canonical episode file** against the current ensemble, PRIM, Wildcard, character-lifecycle and reverse-reveal authorities.

It does **not** rewrite Episodes 01–18. It tells the next rewrite pass what material can be preserved and what character/story assumptions are stale.

- `story/ENSEMBLE-POV-AUTHORITY.md`

Canonical rewrite law:

> **Preserve strong incidents. Replace stale character architecture.**

---

# Classification key

### CURRENT
Core chronology/ownership is consistent with present canon. Add scene-level living-cast development during production.

### PARTIAL MIGRATION
Technical incident and substantial character material remain usable, but ownership, recurring cast, micro-development or intervention fields need deliberate revision.

### MAJOR MIGRATION
Technical premise may survive, but current owner/gameplay/relationship architecture materially conflicts with higher canon.

### SEVERE STALE CONTINUITY
Contains a premise directly invalidated by newer canon such as separate contemporary Benji, Prime-00 formal identity, first-discovery chronology, or Jordan-default gameplay.

# Repo-wide conclusions

## Strong material to preserve

The local drafts contain substantial useful technical/inciting-event work. The migration should **not** erase:
- access authorization in E02;
- evidence/provenance in E03;
- social trust/access in E04;
- role/command exercise in E05;
- communications + physical dependency in E06;
- breach/identity in E07;
- app safe-failure in E08;
- malware classification/shame in E09;
- migration/rollback in E10;
- physical evidence/decommissioning in E11;
- cyber-physical building state in E12;
- shared service responsibility in E13;
- CapEx/OpEx dependency economics in E14;
- cloud elasticity in E15;
- road-kit/human fatigue in E16;
- patch/rollback/trust in E17.

## Stale patterns to remove everywhere

- episode owner = only person who develops;
- named support character appearing without a persistent write;
- Jordan as default player/incident coordinator;
- separate present-day Benji;
- `Prime-00`/`P-00` as current formal identity;
- PRIM2 first discovering Jordan during college;
- PRIM2 physically lurking in every episode;
- automatic historical/outside guest quota;
- unnamed disposable casualty when a recurring character should carry the loss;
- clean one-for-one replacement after death;
- emotional reset after injury/death/promotion;
- faceless RAID assault-team casualties.

## Current writing law

> **Episode ownership determines depth, not exclusivity.**

> **If you appear, you move.**

> **If you die, the building changes.**

> **Preserve strong incidents. Replace stale character architecture.**
