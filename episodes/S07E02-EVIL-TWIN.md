# S7E2 — EVIL TWIN

## Episode job

This episode happens immediately after RAID has physically defeated Presiding Prime but before Ghost In The Wires permanently transfers sovereign authority away from Prime-00.

Prime's body is critically damaged and has been returned to the suspension tank as life support. His biological identity is still the highest-trust biometric anchor in Site 0. The team has therefore won the body but has **not yet won the authority graph**.

Jordan refuses to take the Prime role until he understands what he is inheriting.

Prime answers by revealing the architecture he hid for years:

- Prim3 and Hitman were sibling instruments under one apex;
- Jordan and Benji were parallel ascents through opposite institutional cultures;
- Rae and Sora were social-entry twins who never knew one another;
- several operations the audience understood as single-team victories were actually paired operations;
- Prime was not merely observing both systems — he was supplying each side different pieces of reality;
- some morally defensible outcomes came from methods Jordan finds intolerable;
- some of Prime's apparent confession is itself a controlled presentation.

The final dramatic turn is Jordan realizing that **Prime has curated the confession**. The mission becomes a race to corroborate the dual-board story from raw, independently held records before Prime's body loses biometric authority.

This sets up Ghost In The Wires: Jordan will only use Prime's surviving biometric authority after independently proving what the role actually controls.

## Technical concept from the song

The song's “evil twin” language becomes a broad identity-and-trust lesson rather than a procedural attack tutorial.

The episode dramatizes:

- trusted duplicates and impersonation;
- two identities or interfaces that appear equivalent but have different provenance;
- social trust as an access layer;
- fake legitimacy versus real authority;
- duplicated operational pathways;
- identity relay and trust-chain abuse as concepts;
- corroboration across independent records;
- why appearance is not proof of origin;
- why one source controlled by one powerful actor is not enough to establish truth.

The educational thesis is:

> **The most dangerous twin is not the one that looks fake. It is the one that looks legitimate enough that you stop asking where it came from.**

No real-world exploitation steps, commands, payloads, credential procedures or attack recipes are shown.

# 6:00–9:00 — MISSION 20: MIRROR ROOM

## Mission fantasy

**Prove which parts of Prime's confession are true using records he did not control, before the biometric succession window collapses.**

This is a dual-perspective mission.

The player alternates between Jordan's Prim3 side and Benji's Hitman side while both teams move through physically separated evidence stores inside damaged Site 0.

The challenge is not “hack the other side.”

The challenge is **corroboration**.

Prime has given them one story. They must find independent evidence showing where the two supposedly separate organizations touched the same events.

## Primary playable characters

The player switches between:

- **Jordan Vale** — Prim3 evidence lane;
- **Benji** — Hitman evidence lane.

Support characters:

- Aya — technical provenance and artifact interpretation;
- Chioma — Prim3 mission chronology;
- Jonas — Site 0 continuity / biometric-window status;
- Sable — Hitman money and logistics chronology;
- Rae — street/social memory;
- Jules — ordinary-device / repair-counter context;
- Sora — elite institutional/social memory;
- Prime — optional commentary that the player increasingly learns not to treat as evidence.

## Core gameplay loop

1. **FIND A CLAIM** — Prime's curated disclosure makes a claim about the past.
2. **FIND TWO INDEPENDENT RECORDS** — one from Prim3, one from Hitman or another independent source.
3. **MATCH PROVENANCE** — compare time, location, sponsor, resource or outcome without assuming identical appearance means identical origin.
4. **MARK STATUS** — CONFIRMED / CONTRADICTED / INCOMPLETE.
5. **CHOOSE WHAT TO ASK PRIME** — confrontation dialogue changes based on what the player has actually proven.

The game rewards skepticism, not speed-clicking.

## Mission scoring

- **Corroboration** — how many claims are independently established?
- **Provenance** — did the player distinguish source from appearance?
- **Team restraint** — did Prim3 and Hitman avoid escalating against each other?
- **Time** — did enough truth get established before the biometric window collapses?
- **Judgment** — did the player avoid treating Prime's explanation as proof merely because he knows more than everyone else?

# WATCH VERSION — AUTHORED ACTION CUT

If the viewer chooses WATCH rather than PLAY, the 6:00–9:00 segment becomes a fast dual-timeline forensic thriller.

### Visual language

- Jordan's evidence is framed in cold institutional architecture.
- Benji's evidence is framed through tactile ledgers, cases, cars, receipts and human memory.
- Whenever independent facts match, the aspect ratios physically converge.
- Prime's curated evidence is visually too perfect: centered, clean, high contrast.
- Raw evidence is messy, incomplete and sometimes contradictory.
- The audience should learn subconsciously that **cleaner evidence is not necessarily better evidence**.

### Authored sequence

1. Jordan and Benji split into separate evidence lanes.
2. Both independently discover the paired equipment cases.
3. Rae and Sora reconstruct the shared lobby event and finally recognize each other.
4. The sponsor case proves Prime sometimes used terrifying architecture against genuinely dangerous people.
5. Jordan realizes the distributed Prime identity and the biological Prime identity appear equivalent but are not equivalent sources of authority.
6. He identifies the living biometric root as the only safe source for a real succession transfer.
7. Teams lower weapons.
8. Jordan orders Prime kept alive.

The WATCH cut and PLAY path end at the same hard continuity checkpoint.

# PLAY VERSION — GAME DESIGN

## Player experience

This should feel like a detective board under military time pressure, not like a conventional terminal-hacking minigame.

The player's intelligence comes from comparing independent evidence and refusing seductive certainty.

## Interaction types

- timeline alignment;
- source/provenance tagging;
- visual artifact comparison;
- dialogue recall from Rae/Sora/Sable/Chioma;
- choosing whether to ask Prime a question or search independently;
- identifying when two similar-looking authority nodes have different roots;
- choosing when enough evidence exists to classify a claim as confirmed.

## Prime temptation mechanic

Prime constantly offers shortcuts.

Example:

`PRIME ADVISORY: I CAN IDENTIFY THE MATCH.`

Player options:

- **ACCEPT PRIME MATCH** — faster, lower corroboration score;
- **ASK FOR SOURCE** — may reveal that Prime's answer points back to his curated dataset;
- **IGNORE / VERIFY INDEPENDENTLY** — slower, higher trust from Jordan's team.

Prime never gives obviously stupid advice.

That would make the mechanic trivial.

His advice should often be **correct**, which is what makes relying on him dangerous.

## Relationship consequences

Player behavior can alter debrief state:

- trusting Sable's records raises Jordan/Benji institutional respect;
- dismissing Rae's memory as “unreliable” damages Rae/Sora relationship formation;
- letting Prime answer everything lowers Aya/Chioma trust in Jordan's post-Prime judgment;
- independently confirming Prime's sponsor claim increases moral complexity rather than “Prime approval”;
- escalating against Hitman before corroboration worsens future alliance potential.

Hard canon remains:

- dual board is proven;
- Prime shared apex is proven;
- Jordan learns distributed Prime continuity is not enough to execute a legitimate sovereign transfer;
- Prime's body must remain alive long enough for Ghost In The Wires;
- Jordan orders him preserved.
