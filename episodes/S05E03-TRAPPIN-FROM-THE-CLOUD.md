# S5E3 - TRAPPIN FROM THE CLOUD

## Merge contract

This chapter preserves Jordan's first indirect glimpse of the Hitman side and
the possibility of Benji appearing without either man understanding the other.
It must not create a prior Jordan/Benji relationship or conversation.

## Chapter promise

A sudden international event drives demand beyond every forecast. The player
must scale services without mixing sensitive workloads, then investigate a
pattern that belongs to no known Prim3 team.

## Song payload

- **Technical:** cloud compute, storage, networking, analytics, agility,
  scalability, disaster recovery, elasticity, high availability, allocation,
  and supply/demand.
- **Human:** ambition, access to opportunity, reacting faster than danger, and
  the unease of discovering someone else profits or acts inside the same crisis.
- **Visual:** a street-scale operation expanding into a world-scale map;
  workloads move like packages while one unexplained route travels against the
  flow.
- **Safety:** player actions manage authorized services and defensive anomaly
  correlation; traffic “sniffing” is represented as owned telemetry.

## Nine-minute episode

### 0:00-3:00 - Music film

The four basic cloud services become a fast visual lesson, then the image scales
from one block to multiple regions. Elastic resources breathe with demand while
one dark convoy of data/people never appears on the official ledger.

### 3:00-6:00 - Human story

Jordan believes he finally understands Prim3's global reach. Architecture and
budget access have made him confident. Telemetry from the unfolding event
reveals actions no Prim3 owner can explain, forcing him to consider that the
organization is only one board in a larger game.

### 6:00-9:00 - Watch / Play mission

WATCH scales the service, preserves continuity, and ends on a frame of another
team leaving with an unknown objective. PLAY requires capacity allocation,
health monitoring, workload separation, failover, and correlation of the
unexplained operational trace without abandoning the live service.

## Playable level contract

- **Player role:** Jordan as global service coordinator.
- **Environment:** multi-region operations map connected to one grounded field
  location where consequences are visible.
- **Primary objective:** maintain the critical service through the demand spike.
- **Optional objectives:** control cost; keep sensitive workloads separated;
  preserve the unknown trace; identify the frame containing the other team.
- **Complication:** every resource assigned to investigation is unavailable for
  response, and vice versa.
- **Exit state:** Prim3 succeeds publicly; Jordan privately knows another actor
  completed a different objective.

## Player verbs

Allocate, scale, segment, monitor, fail over, correlate, preserve, review.

## Graded outcomes

| Channel | Strong result | Weak but continuing result |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | Service remains available across the spike | Service degrades but critical users persist |
| Team | Operators sustain a manageable load | Staff fatigue and handoff errors accumulate |
| Exposure | Sensitive workloads remain separated | Emergency scaling blurs boundaries |
| Integrity | Unknown trace is preserved without spying outside scope | Player destroys or over-collects evidence |
| Time | Scaling leads demand | Response chases failures after they occur |

## Persistent state written by the level

Jordan gains the first Hitman-trace evidence flag. Owned/rented capacity and
cost states from Episode 14 are consumed. Benji may be visually recorded but
is not identified to Jordan. Prime's dual-board control remains background truth.

## Prime background trace

- **Visible trace:** two identical hard cases leave one logistics system through
  separated procurement lanes; one reaches Jordan's team and the other reaches
  Benji's people in another city.
- **First-watch meaning:** global logistics duplication during a large response.
- **Ghost payoff:** Prime packed and observed both lanes; the supposedly unknown
  second actor was another instrument under his hidden authority.

## Technology accuracy gate

- **Sensor / input:** demand metrics, service health, resource allocation,
  workload labels, owned telemetry, and field reports.
- **Compute / reasoning:** forecast near-term load, allocate capacity, verify
  separation, and correlate unexplained events with uncertainty.
- **Energy / infrastructure:** regions/zones, compute, storage, networking,
  analytics, identity, and field connectivity.
- **Access / authorization:** Prim3-owned accounts and telemetry only.
- **Human decision owner:** Jordan under global incident command.
- **Failure mode:** overload, uncontrolled cost, mixed workloads, failed
  failover, or investigation distracting from service.
- **Real concept taught:** elasticity changes allocated capacity; resilience
  still requires architecture, monitoring, and tested recovery.
- **Fictional extrapolation:** global physical/technical events share one
  stylized command map.
