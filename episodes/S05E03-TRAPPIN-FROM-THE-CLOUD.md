# Human problem

A sudden international demand spike pushes a critical service beyond forecasted capacity.

Sari has dreamed of operating at this scale. The reality is uglier: everything expands at once—capacity, cost, workload, blast radius, visibility and moral consequences.

On the Hitman side, Sable manages a different organization facing the same fundamental truth: scale is not glamour; scale is responsibility multiplied.

The episode creates parallel operations without claiming both organizations are secretly the same team.

---

# Technical thesis

Teach:
- elasticity vs resilience;
- compute/storage/network scaling;
- workload separation;
- failover;
- high availability;
- resource allocation;
- demand forecasting;
- cost/operational debt;
- regional dependency;
- authorized telemetry and anomaly correlation.

Core line:

> **Scaling the service also scales the consequences of being wrong.**
