# Human problem

Jordan arrives for onboarding with one device/item in his issued kit that does not match the manifest.

Urgency pushes toward waving him through because he is expected, useful and already sponsored.

Nusrat's belief:

> **A rule that changes depending on who is standing at the door is theater.**

The episode explores authorization, intent and incomplete system knowledge without treating ambiguity as guilt.

---

# Technical thesis

Two axes stay distinct:

```text
AUTHORIZATION / INTENT
vs.
SYSTEM KNOWLEDGE / VISIBILITY
```

Teach:
- written scope;
- asset identity;
- least privilege;
- rules of engagement;
- documented exceptions;
- evidence/provenance;
- escalation without bypass.

# WATCH spine

1. Jordan's kit fails manifest verification.
2. Priya/Maren feel the scheduling/public consequence while Nusrat/Miguel own the boundary.
3. Helen is asked for an exception and refuses to turn executive preference into undocumented access.
4. Jordan declares the discrepancy instead of minimizing it.
5. Aya helps establish safe technical custody without pretending technical familiarity equals authority.
6. The team resolves provenance and onboarding through a legitimate alternate path.
7. Chioma's brief debrief observation plants that Nusrat is someone worth trusting under pressure.

PRIM2's hidden approval may arrive **after** ordinary process produces a defensible result. It is institutional gravity, not a first-discovery event.

# PLAY

The player controls the people deciding what Jordan may touch.

Primary verbs:
`inspect / compare / verify / hold / document / escalate / authorize / deny / preserve`.

Strong play preserves both schedule and integrity through alternatives. Weak play either grants unjustified access or treats uncertainty as wrongdoing.
