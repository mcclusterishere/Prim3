# ADV-01 — State Actor Operator

## Function
Hostile government or intelligence service operators encountered during missions (especially later-season government-directed operations). Must feel like a real opposing force with its own coherent visual language — not generic “bad guys in black.”

## Visual direction (to be locked)
- Distinct national or service palette (not McCluster black/ivory/red)
- Functional military or intelligence cut with clear differences in helmet, armor layout, and markings
- Their own equivalent of Dual Sight / state indicators if desired
- Higher or lower tech emphasis depending on the specific adversary

## Social signal
“We are the other system. We also believe we are the legitimate ones.”

## Notes
Keep deliberately under-designed until a specific adversary is chosen for an episode. The goal is coherent opposition, not mirror-image McCluster.
