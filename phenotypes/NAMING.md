# Phenotype naming (hybrid system)

## Rule
Every phenotype is spoken and titled as:

**Class word + number + role words**

Not a bare acronym. Not a code-only label.

**Good:** `Cohort-02 Trainee`  
**Bad:** `CO-02` alone  
**Bad:** `CFI-01` alone when you mean clothes

Codes may still exist in file paths. **Human language is what we use to think.**

---

## Two kinds of things

| Kind | Meaning | Example |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Role phenotype** | Job / place in the system | Cohort-02 Trainee, Field-E Entry Operator |
| **Wear skin** | Clothes / visual kit | Wear-01 Company Black |

Roles are who someone **is**.  
Wear is what they **put on**.

A Cohort-02 Trainee’s normal clothes are Wear-01 Company Black or Wear-02 Company Bone.

## How to say the confusing relationships

| Don’t say | Say |
|-----------|-----|
| CO-02 default skin is CFI-01 | **Cohort-02 Trainee normally wears Wear-01 Company Black** |
| FO-T under CO-03 | **Cohort-03 Lead runs a team that includes Field-T Technical Operator** |
| EI-03 signs FO mission | **Authority-03 Mission Sponsor puts their name on the field operation** |
| SOI-02 vs BS-01 | **Defense-02 Access Officer controls who enters; System-01 Building Platform runs the building’s automated systems** |

---

## Progression in plain language

**Cohort path:**  
Cohort-02 Trainee → Cohort-01 Operator → Cohort-03 Lead → Prime-N1 Prime

**Clothes along the path:**  
Trainee mostly in Company Wear.  
Operator / Lead use Company Wear off-kit, Field kits on external ops.  
Prime uses the full Prime operator identity.

**Authority path (separate from operator succession):**  
Authority-03 Mission Sponsor → Authority-02 Director → Authority-01 Executive

## File paths
Old folder names may still use short codes (CO-02.md, FO-T.md).  
Inside each file, the title should use the **hybrid name**.  
When talking, writing story, or designing the game — use hybrid names only.
