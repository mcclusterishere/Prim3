# FO-E — Entry Field Operator

## Function
The physical access and close-security specialist. Responsible for getting the team onto the objective, controlling immediate space, and protecting the Technical operator while the technical work is performed. This is the kinetic and spatial problem-solver of the field triad.

## Visual & gear emphasis
- Highest soft-to-mid armor load among the three basic field phenotypes
- Entry tools (mechanical, limited energetic, bypass hardware)
- Sidearm + mission-selected primary more commonly carried
- Strong Dual Sight / `//` visibility for rapid recognition in confined or low-light spaces
- Gloves and boots optimized for forced movement, climbing, and close work
- Minimal bulk beyond what entry and protection require

## Core responsibilities
- Route selection and physical entry
- Immediate area security once inside
- Close protection of FO-T during the technical window
- Controlling doors, corridors, and chokepoints
- Leading movement on contact or emergency exfil

## Behavioral expectations
- Highest baseline physical readiness on the team
- Decisions are spatial and temporal: where to be, when to move, what to block
- Low tolerance for technical debate once the team is exposed
- Treats the Technical operator as a protected asset during the critical phase
- After contact, prioritizes team integrity and exit over continued objective work unless ordered otherwise

## Relationship to other field phenotypes
- Enables FO-T by creating and holding the working space
- Receives early warning and external picture from FO-R
- Often the first through the door and the last to break contact on exit

## Narrative utility
This is the phenotype for forced entry, corridor fights, and the physical tension that surrounds technical work. It keeps the story kinetic without turning the team into generic special forces.
