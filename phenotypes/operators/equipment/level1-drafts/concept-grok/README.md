# Level-one equipment: Grok concept sheet

`sheet-original.jpg` is a concept render of the 18 level-one items, supplied by the owner on 2026-11-02 (made with Grok). The owner judged it more realistic than the blockouts.

**Concept art only; never geometry authority.**

`E01.png` … `T06.png` are per-item crops for image-to-3D:
- each is cut from the sheet with its label excluded, then upscaled to 1024 px;
- the T row's purple is shifted to lime, under the owner's rule (2026-11-02): role colours yes, never purple.

**Limits:**
- Each item is only about 240 px wide in the sheet, so detail is limited. Full-size single-item renders would give better 3D.
- T04's energy swirl is an effect, not an object. It belongs in the engine as the Sync Pulse area ring and should be removed before 3D generation.
