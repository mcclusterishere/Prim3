# Cohort-02 Trainee

*(File id: CO-02 — use hybrid name when talking or designing.)*

## What this is
**Role:** Early member of the cohort path.  
Training, intake, internal exercises, low-visibility institutional work.  
**Not** a field operator. Not a building guard. Not an executive.

## What they wear
**Normal clothes = Company Wear**

| Wear name | Old code | Use |
|-----------|----------|-----|
| **Wear-01 Company Black** | CFI-01 | Primary daily clothes |
| **Wear-02 Company Bone** | CFI-02 | Alternate daily clothes |

### Rule in one line
**Cohort-02 Trainee is the job. Company Wear is the outfit.**

| Situation | What they wear |
|-----------|----------------|
| Office / campus / daily life | Company Wear only |
| Internal training | Company Wear + light training layer |
| Real external mission | Not this role’s job — move up to Cohort-01 Operator + Field kit |

## Job expectations
- Learning procedures under evaluation
- More formal and careful than Cohort-01 Operator
- Does not run Field-T / Field-E / Field-R jobs as their identity
- Main product: development under observation

## Path
**Cohort-02 Trainee** → **Cohort-01 Operator** → **Cohort-03 Lead** → **Prime-N1 Prime**

Clothes stay Company Wear for daily life even after promotion.  
Field kits only when the role and mission require them.

“I have been brought inside. I have tools. I am being watched.”
