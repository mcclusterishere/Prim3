# CO-02 — Cohort Operator (Light / Training)

## Function
Transitional loadout used during early training, internal exercises, and low-visibility corporate technical work. Bridges Corporate Fits and full CO-01.

## Visual direction
- Corporate Fits base (Authority Black or Bone) with added technical layers
- Light soft protection or utility vest that still looks institutional
- Wrist module and restrained Dual Sight accents only
- No heavy armor, no full helmet, no Mission Core

## Social signal
“I have been given tools. I am being watched.”

## Behavioral expectation
Still learning procedures, more formal than later operators, high awareness of evaluation.
