# CO-02 — Cohort Operator (Light / Training)

## Function
Early cohort identity. Used during intake, training, internal exercises, and low-visibility institutional work. **Not field ops.** This is the stage where someone belongs to the cohort path but has not yet become a full mission-capable CO-01 operator.

CO-02 is a **role / progression phenotype**.  
**CFI-01 / CFI-02 are its default skins** when not in a light training overlay.

## CFI linkage (locked)

| Layer | What it is |
|-------|------------|
| **CO-02** | Who they are in the system (cohort light / training) |
| **CFI-01 / CFI-02** | What they wear in normal institutional life |

### Rule
- **Default skin for CO-02 = Corporate Fits**
  - **CFI-01** Authority Black — primary institutional default
  - **CFI-02** Bone Herringbone — approved alternate colorway
- CFI is wardrobe, not a separate career class
- CO-02 does **not** get FO-T / FO-E / FO-R kits as standard identity
- A light training overlay (thin utility layer, wrist module, restrained Dual Sight) may sit **on top of** CFI during exercises — it does not replace CFI as the base

### When CO-02 wears what
| Context | Skin |
|---------|------|
| Campus / office / daily institutional life | **CFI-01 or CFI-02** pure |
| Internal training / low-visibility technical work | **CFI base + light CO-02 overlay** |
| External field deployment | Not standard — promote/path into CO-01 + FO kits |
| Formal institutional presence | **CFI** (never EI executive system) |

### What this is not
- CFI is **not** merged into the CO-02 phenotype ID
- CO-02 is **not** “just clothes” — status and evaluation still apply while wearing CFI
- Wearing CFI does **not** make someone EI-01/02/03
- CO-02 in CFI is still being watched and scored as cohort

## Visual direction
- **Base:** Corporate Fits (Authority Black or Bone Herringbone) — locked assets under `assets/canon/brands/mccluster-corp/corporate-fits-identity/`
- **Optional overlay:** light soft protection or utility layer that still reads institutional
- Wrist module and restrained Dual Sight accents only
- No heavy armor, no full helmet, no Mission Core, no FO entry bulk

## Social signal
“I have been brought inside. I have tools. I am being watched.”

## Behavioral expectation
- Still learning procedures
- More formal and evaluation-aware than CO-01
- High awareness of hierarchy; low freelancing
- Not expected to hold FO-E space or FO-T technical windows
- Primary product is development under observation, not external mission results

## Place in cohort progression
| ID | Name | Default non-tactical skin | Ops posture |
|----|------|---------------------------|-------------|
| **CO-02** | Light / Training | **CFI-01 / CFI-02** | Training / internal |
| **CO-01** | Standard | CFI between missions; ops kit when active | Mission-capable |
| **CO-03** | Lead | CFI / restrained institutional when off-kit | Runs the element |
| **PRIME-N1** | Succession end-state | Full operator identity | End-state |

**Path:** CO-02 (CFI life) → CO-01 (ops identity) → CO-03 → PRIME-N1

## Trifecta utility
- **Game:** Starter cohort class; civilian-looking skin with hidden evaluation pressure; unlock path into CO-01/FO
- **Story:** “Ordinary” institutional body that is already inside the machine
- **Real:** Trainee / junior institutional operator wardrobe and status — professional clothes, escalating access, continuous assessment

## Transition
- From RS-01 / AC-01 / open intake into CO-02
- From CO-02 → CO-01 after real operational exposure and clearance
- Skin stays CFI until ops kit is issued; CFI remains the off-kit default even after promotion
