# FO-R — Recon / Overwatch Field Operator

## Function
The external awareness and early-warning specialist. Responsible for surveillance of the approach, perimeter, and surrounding environment while the rest of the team is on the objective. This phenotype owns the question: “What is about to happen to us from outside?”

FO-R exists so that FO-E and FO-T can stay focused inward. Their primary product is a continuous, accurate external picture and the authority to force a change of plan when that picture collapses.

## Visual Direction
- Lightest armor of the three basic field phenotypes for maximum mobility and low visual/thermal signature
- Darker, more subdued weathering; prioritizes blending over presence
- Dual Sight / red `//` is present but controllable — can be muted or covered when signature must stay low
- Silhouette optimized for static or semi-static observation positions and rapid displacement
- Optics and sensor packages are the visual signature more than heavy armor or weapons
- Face protection minimal and low-profile (headset, soft cover, or partial shell only)
- Overall look: the person who is hard to see and harder to surprise

## Gear Specifications

### Design philosophy
Gear exists to see farther, stay quieter, and move faster than the threat.  
Observation quality and signature management outrank armor and pure kinetic load.  
Every item must justify itself against the cost of being detected.

### Equipment Floor
| Item | Specification |
|------|---------------|
| Base layer | Dark technical field suit, low-signature materials |
| Armor | Lightest soft torso protection of the triad |
| Sidearm | One compact service sidearm, low-profile holster |
| Optics | Mission-appropriate observation optics |
| Sensors | Basic environmental / approach detection capability |
| Comms | Secure team radio + reliable low-profile earpiece |
| Recording | Observation and mission recording suite |
| Identification | Dual Sight or red `//` that can be subdued |
| Handwear | Low-bulk gloves compatible with optics and device use |
| Footwear | Quiet-soled field boots optimized for movement and static positions |

### Equipment Ceiling (mission-authorized)
**Observation & Sensors**
- Enhanced day/night optics
- Extended-duration observation tools
- Compact sensor packages (motion, acoustic, or simple RF as authorized)
- Recording systems optimized for long takes and evidence-quality capture

**Protection & Mobility**
- Light soft armor only
- Minimal extremity protection
- No heavy plates that increase signature or reduce displacement speed
- No full helmet

**Kinetic**
- Sidearm mandatory
- Longer weapon only when the specific overwatch role requires it
- No heavy infantry-style loadouts

**Electronics**
- Extended secure comms and relay capability when acting as the external node
- Limited personal navigation and timing tools
- No full technical exploitation stack (FO-T domain)
- No primary entry/breaching tools (FO-E domain)

### Hard Exclusions
- Full Prime M-helmet and Mission Core
- Heavy armor or plate carriers
- Primary technical exploitation kits
- Primary entry/breaching loads
- High-visibility Dual Sight configurations when the mission requires low signature
- Clean Corporate Fits garments
- Non-McCluster marks

## Core Responsibilities
- Pre-mission surveillance of approaches and patterns of life
- Live perimeter and external threat monitoring during the technical window
- Early warning of approaching forces, cameras, vehicles, or environmental changes
- Guiding team movement on approach and exfiltration
- Maintaining the continuous external communications and observation picture
- Forcing an abort or displacement recommendation when the external threat state becomes unrecoverable

## Behavioral Expectations

### Core posture
A Recon / Overwatch Field Operator treats information as the first line of protection. They are comfortable being alone at the edge of the team’s bubble for long periods. Boredom is not a failure state; it is part of the work. The only unacceptable state is being surprised.

### During the observation window
- Highest patience and observational discipline on the team
- Speaks only when the external picture changes or when coordination requires it
- Maintains continuous mental models of approach routes, timing, and likely threat behavior
- Comfortable operating semi-independently
- Resists the urge to collapse inward toward the objective unless the picture demands it

### Decision logic
- Prefers early, clear warning over late, dramatic intervention
- Will force a mission abort recommendation if the external threat state deteriorates beyond recovery
- Does not require perfect certainty to raise the alarm; acts on the best available picture and updates it in real time
- Treats “I think something is wrong” as actionable when the cost of being wrong is lower than the cost of silence

### Speech patterns
- Extremely economical while the situation is stable
- When the picture changes, language becomes precise, timed, and directive
- Avoids narrative or emotional content during live observation
- After-action speech focuses on what was seen, when it was seen, and what it implied for the team’s options

### Relationship to pressure
- External threat increases reporting tempo and clarity, not volume for its own sake
- Visible stress appears as intensified stillness and sharper discrimination of signal from noise
- The operator is expected to remain the most patient mind on the team even when the interior of the objective is becoming chaotic

## Psychological Load (FO-R specific)
- Prolonged isolation at the edge of the team while still carrying responsibility for everyone’s survival
- The weight of being the first to know that the situation is collapsing and the one who must say it
- Chronic low-grade hyper-vigilance that can be difficult to shut off after return
- Guilt or second-guessing when a threat is detected late or when an abort recommendation is overridden
- The particular strain of watching danger approach people you cannot immediately reach

## Relationship to the Field Triad
| Phenotype | Relationship to FO-R |
|-----------|----------------------|
| FO-T Technical | Receives the external picture so they can stay focused on systems |
| FO-E Entry | Receives early warning that determines whether the space is still worth holding |
| FO-R Recon | Owns the outside world; often first to detect trouble and last to be seen |

## Failure Modes
- Tunnel vision on a single approach while missing a secondary vector
- Delaying a warning in search of higher certainty until the warning is too late
- Collapsing inward toward the team too early and losing the external picture
- Over-warning to the point that the team loses the ability to distinguish signal from noise
- Post-mission inability to stand down from continuous environmental scanning

## Narrative Utility
FO-R is the phenotype for tension that comes from what is *approaching* rather than what is already in the room. It creates dread through information, timing, and the gap between knowledge and the ability to act. When used well, the audience feels the external noose tightening while FO-T is still deep in the technical work and FO-E is still holding the door.

## Transition
- From CO-01 or FO-01 when assigned the recon / overwatch role on an external mission
- Back to CO-01 or Corporate Fits when returning to controlled environments
- Repeated FO-R exposure deepens the hyper-vigilance and anticipatory components of the broader operator trauma profile, with particular emphasis on isolation, responsibility for distant teammates, and the burden of being the first to see the end of the safe window
