# FO-R — Recon / Overwatch Field Operator

## Function
The external awareness and early-warning specialist. Responsible for surveillance of the approach, perimeter, and surrounding environment while the rest of the team is on the objective. This phenotype owns the question: “What is about to happen to us from outside?”

## Visual & gear emphasis
- Lightest armor of the three for maximum mobility and low signature
- Enhanced optics, sensors, and recording
- Long-range or extended-duration observation tools
- Strong but controllable Dual Sight / `//` (can be muted when signature must stay low)
- Minimal kinetic load relative to FO-E; sidearm primary, longer weapon only when overwatch role requires it
- Gear optimized for static or semi-static positions and rapid displacement

## Core responsibilities
- Pre-mission and live surveillance of the target approaches
- Perimeter and external threat monitoring during the technical window
- Early warning of approaching forces, cameras, or environmental changes
- Guiding team movement on approach and exfil
- Maintaining the external communications and observation picture

## Behavioral expectations
- Highest patience and observational discipline on the team
- Speaks only when the external picture changes or when required for coordination
- Comfortable operating semi-independently at the edge of the team’s bubble
- Treats boredom and long static periods as part of the work
- Will force a mission abort recommendation if the external threat state deteriorates beyond recovery

## Relationship to other field phenotypes
- Provides the external picture that lets FO-E and FO-T stay focused inward
- Often the first to detect trouble and the last to be seen by the adversary
- Critical to clean exfiltration

## Narrative utility
This is the phenotype for tension that comes from what is *approaching* rather than what is already in the room. It creates dread through information and timing instead of pure kinetic action.
