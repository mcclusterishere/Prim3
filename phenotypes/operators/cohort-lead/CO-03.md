# CO-03 — Cohort Lead

## Function
The senior active cohort identity. This is the operator who runs a small element — typically a field triad (FO-T / FO-E / FO-R) or an internal institutional team — under direction from higher authority (Prime or institutional command). CO-03 is still clearly cohort, not yet Prime. They carry more responsibility, more evaluation weight, and more visibility than CO-01.

This is the visible step between “competent operator” and “succession candidate.”

## Distinction
| Phenotype | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **CO-02** Light / Training | Early exposure, low-visibility work |
| **CO-01** Standard | Active mission-capable cohort member |
| **CO-03** Lead | Runs the element; accountable for the team’s outcome |
| **PRIME-N1** | End-state succession identity |

## Visual direction
- Same institutional operator language as CO-01, with slight elevation
- Cleaner, more deliberate kit presentation — less “lived-in scramble,” more controlled authority
- Dual Sight / `//` still present and readable; may carry slightly stronger mark hierarchy
- No full Prime M-helmet or Mission Core
- Armor and modules match the mission (can wear FO kits when leading in the field)
- Silhouette should read as “the one the others orient to” without becoming executive or pure military command

## Equipment ceiling
- Full CO-01 / FO mission kit as required by the operation
- Additional comms and coordination tools appropriate to leading a small element
- Still below PRIME-N1 gear ceiling
- No exclusive “command only” super-kit that breaks the institutional operator look

## Core responsibilities
- Directing the small element on mission
- Translating higher intent into concrete team action
- Holding the team to standard under pressure
- Making the call when FO-R warns, FO-E wants to collapse, or FO-T needs more time
- Accounting for the team’s result — success, failure, and attrition — upward
- Protecting the evaluation integrity of the element (no freelancing that poisons the record)

## Behavioral expectations

### Core posture
A Cohort Lead moves like someone who is already being measured for something larger. They are still one of the operators, but they are no longer only responsible for their own lane. The team’s survival and the mission’s outcome both sit on them.

### Decision logic
- Integrates FO-R’s external picture, FO-E’s space assessment, and FO-T’s technical status into a single call
- Will end a technical window early to preserve the team when the picture demands it
- Will hold a window open when the take is worth the risk and the space is still tenable
- Does not hide uncertainty from the team; does not perform panic upward

### Relationship to the team
- Still one of them — eats the same risk, wears related kit
- Expected to be the calmest decision-maker when the situation turns
- Genuine care for specific operators coexists with the institutional requirement to treat the element as a unit that can take losses
- After attrition, leads the re-stabilization before private processing

### Relationship to hierarchy
- Receives intent from Prime / institutional command and turns it into action
- Owns the after-action truth of what happened
- Quietly evaluated on whether the element stayed coherent under pressure

### Speech
- Clearer and more directive than CO-01 under live conditions
- Short when the situation is live; precise when reporting upward
- Does not narrate for effect

## Psychological load (CO-03 specific)
- Responsibility for people who are still peers, not subordinates in a traditional military sense
- The knowledge that every call is also an evaluation event
- Carrying both the mission result and the human cost upward
- Standing between the team’s loyalty to each other and the institution’s demand for results
- Accelerated exposure to the succession-path pressure without yet holding Prime authority

## Failure modes
- Over-identifying with the team and refusing necessary hard calls
- Over-identifying with the institution and treating the team as pure expendable resource
- Freezing between FO-T’s need for time and FO-R/FO-E’s need to move
- Performing certainty upward while the picture is still broken
- Burning out the element to look decisive

## Narrative & game utility
- **Story:** The human hinge between cohort and Prime. Where loyalty, competence, and institutional pressure collide most visibly.
- **Game:** Squad-lead role. Player (or AI director) makes the integrated calls across the field triad.
- **Real:** Small-team lead in a high-accountability institutional environment — responsible for execution, safety, and honest reporting.

## Transition
- From CO-01 after demonstrated ability to hold an element together under real pressure
- From CO-03 → PRIME-N1 only through the succession path (not automatic promotion)
- Can still deploy in FO-T / FO-E / FO-R configurations when the lead must also fill a lane
