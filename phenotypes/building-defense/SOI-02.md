# SOI-02 — Access Control Officer

## Class
**Building Defense**

## Function
Controls who enters and exits McCluster facilities. Owns lobbies, vehicle gates, badge points, visitor processing, and identity verification. This is the human face of the building’s perimeter — polite, precise, and absolute about credentials.

## Visual direction
- Same Authority Black institutional foundation as SOI-01
- Even cleaner silhouette; less “patrol,” more “post”
- Body camera standard
- Cyberdeck or fixed terminal access for identity systems
- Sidearm present but visually secondary
- Dual Sight / M marks restrained and professional
- May include visible access-control interface (badge reader interaction, tablet, or post terminal)

## Equipment emphasis
| Item | Role |
|------|------|
| Corporate foundation | Institutional authority |
| Ultra-thin vest | Minimal protection without tactical bulk |
| Body camera | Continuous accountability |
| Identity systems access | Badge, biometric, visitor log, watchlist |
| Sidearm | Last resort only |
| Comms | Direct to SOI-01 patrol and SOI-03 technical posts |

## Core responsibilities
- Identity verification and badge enforcement
- Visitor processing and escort handoff
- Vehicle and pedestrian gate control
- Watchlist and denial enforcement
- Immediate lockdown initiation at the access layer
- Clean logging of every entry, exit, and exception

## Behavioral expectations
- Highest procedural consistency of the Building Defense class
- Calm, repetitive, exact — the job is to never get bored of checking
- Polite until credentials fail; absolute when they do
- Does not chase; holds the line and calls mobile response (SOI-01)
- Treats every exception as both a people problem and a systems problem

## Social signal
“You do not enter because you want to. You enter because the system allows it.”

## Distinction from SOI-01
SOI-01 moves and responds.  
SOI-02 holds the threshold and decides who crosses it.

## Hard exclusions
Same as SOI-01, plus: no patrol-primary kit, no entry-team bulk, no field operator modules.
