# SOI-01 — Security Officer (Standard)

## Class
**Building Defense**

## Function
General corporate facility security. Protects McCluster personnel, facilities, information systems, and continuity of operations inside and around controlled buildings. This is the baseline building guard phenotype — cyber-first, professional, minimal defensive load.

Not a cohort operator. Not police. Not SWAT.

## Visual direction
- Authority-black corporate foundation
- Ultra-thin protective vest
- High-chest body camera (lens may echo Dual Sight)
- McCluster cyberdeck
- Exactly one compact sidearm in low-profile holster
- Small exact M; restrained Dual Sight language
- No external pouch field, no tactical overload

## Equipment floor
1. Corporate clothing foundation
2. Ultra-thin protective vest
3. Body camera
4. McCluster cyberdeck
5. One sidearm + retention holster

## Hard exclusions
Watches as identity gear, earpieces as default, cable wallets, visible drives, loose cables, gloves as standard, mystery modules, extra pouches, spare magazines, rifles, long guns, helmets, masks, camouflage, MOLLE, knee pads, riot equipment.

## Behavioral expectations
- Procedural, professional, quietly intimidating
- Loyalty to the institution and the facility
- Records and reports; does not freelance kinetic solutions
- Can lock doors, control access systems, and escalate
- Treats anomalies as both physical and digital events

## Social signal
“I protect the building and the data. I am not here to fight wars.”
