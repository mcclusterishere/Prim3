# RS-01 — Recruit Student

## Function
The visual identity of the protagonist and new intake *before* full corporate issue. This is the “ordinary but slightly off” look that lets the audience feel the gap between civilian life and the system that is about to claim them.

## Visual direction (to be locked)
- Practical, worn civilian clothing consistent with a broke 33-year-old first-year student
- Functional backpack, laptop sleeve, minimal tech
- No McCluster marks yet (or only the first subtle issued piece)
- Clothing that shows real use: frayed edges, repaired items, thrift or work-wear pieces
- Height and build still match the final Prime proportions so the later transformation feels continuous

## Equipment ceiling
- Personal laptop / phone
- Basic tools from prior IT / data-center life
- Nothing issued by the corporation yet

## Exclusions
- Any full Corporate Fits pieces
- Tactical or operator gear
- Luxury branding

## Social signal
“I am still outside. I am trying to get in.”

## Behavioral expectation
Hyper-observant, resourceful, slightly out of place among younger classmates, carrying invisible competence and visible material stress.

## Transition
RS-01 → Corporate Fits (CFI) on official issue / internship acceptance.  
This transition is one of the major visual and emotional beats of Season 1.
