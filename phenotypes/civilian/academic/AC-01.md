# AC-01 — Academic / Campus

## Function
Professors, administrators, and ordinary students who populate the college environment of Season 1. Provides contrast and grounding so the corporate and operator phenotypes feel alien and elevated when they appear.

## Visual direction
- Realistic contemporary academic and student clothing
- No McCluster marks
- Age and status readable through fit, quality, and wear

## Social signal
“This is the normal world the protagonist is trying to re-enter.”
