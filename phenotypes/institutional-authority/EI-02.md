# EI-02 — Program / Operations Director

## Class
**Institutional Authority** (suit triad)

## Function
Turns executive intent into runnable programs. This is the suit who moves resources, approves operational lines, and keeps the machinery of McCluster aligned with EI-01’s direction. Not the final authority. Not the person the operators usually see on the ground. The institutional middle power that makes missions possible or impossible.

## Distinction
| Phenotype | Role |
|-----------|------|
| **EI-01** Executive Authority | Strategic control, final institutional weight |
| **EI-02** Program / Operations Director | Converts intent into programs, resources, and constraints |
| **EI-03** Mission Sponsor | Owns a specific line of effort; accountable for its results |

## Visual direction
- Full corporate authority language (Authority Black or equivalent executive tailoring)
- Cleaner and more operational than pure boardroom EI-01; still unmistakably suit, not field
- Restrained Dual Sight / M marks — institutional, not operator
- No tactical armor, no FO kits, no SOI vest as primary identity
- May appear in secure ops spaces, briefings, and controlled facility zones
- Silhouette reads: “I control the pipeline, not the door.”

## Core responsibilities
- Translate EI-01 intent into funded, scheduled, constrained programs
- Allocate (and withhold) resources across cohort, field, and building defense
- Set operational boundaries the CO-03 layer must operate inside
- Absorb and filter upward reporting before it reaches EI-01
- Protect institutional continuity when individual missions fail

## Behavioral expectations
- Speaks in programs, risk, and continuity — not individual heroics
- Calm under political and operational pressure
- Will sacrifice a mission to protect a larger line of effort
- Maintains distance from the cohort’s internal bonds while depending on their output
- Evaluates CO-03 leads as much as missions

## Trifecta utility
- **Game:** Meta-layer control; unlocks/locks mission types and resources
- **Story:** Institutional politics, pressure from above, the face of “the system decided”
- **Real:** Program director / senior ops executive function in a high-accountability org

## Transition
- From senior EI-03 or exceptional CO-03 / institutional track
- Toward EI-01 only through rare succession of institutional power (distinct from operator succession to PRIME-N1)
