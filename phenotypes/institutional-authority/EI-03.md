# EI-03 — Mission Sponsor / Accountable Officer

## Function
Owns a specific line of effort. This is the suit the cohort actually feels. When a field team is sent out, an EI-03 has usually signed for the risk, the objective, and the political consequence. Closer to the operators than EI-01 or EI-02; still clearly institutional authority, not cohort.

## Place in Institutional Authority triad
| ID | Name | Focus |
|----|------|--------|
| **EI-01** | Executive Authority | Strategic control |
| **EI-02** | Program / Operations Director | Portfolio machinery |
| **EI-03** | Mission Sponsor | One line of effort — accountable for results |

## Visual direction
- Institutional suit language; may be slightly less formal than EI-01 in high-tempo periods
- Still no operator kit as identity
- Dual Sight / M restrained
- May appear in secure briefing spaces, TOC-adjacent rooms, or post-mission accountability sessions
- Reads as “the person whose name is on this operation”

## Core responsibilities
- Own the objective and the accepted risk for a mission or campaign line
- Receive CO-03 after-action truth and turn it into institutional reporting
- Protect or expose the team upward depending on standards and politics
- Absorb political and career consequence when operations succeed or fail
- Stay close enough to understand the work without becoming an operator

## Behavioral expectations
- More available to CO-03 than EI-02 or EI-01
- Under pressure, may push for results harder than the team can safely deliver — this is a known failure mode
- Can become either the team’s shield or the team’s problem
- Speech mixes operational detail with institutional framing

## Psychological / narrative load
- Career and identity tied to outcomes they do not personally execute
- Temptation to manage the story of the mission rather than the reality
- Tension between loyalty to operators and loyalty to the institution that judges them

## Trifecta utility
- **Game:** Quest-giver / mission authority with real political weight
- **Story:** Human face of institutional pressure on the cohort
- **Real:** Accountable officer model — one named person owns the line of effort
