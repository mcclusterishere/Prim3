# EI-01 — Executive Authority

## Function
The highest institutional human identity on the Prime side. EI-01 holds strategic control and final institutional weight. This is not an operator, not a facility guard, and not a program manager. This is the person whose decisions shape what programs exist, what risks are acceptable, and whether the succession path itself continues to mean anything.

EI-01 is rare. Most people in the system will never be in a room with one. When they are, the room changes.

## Place in Institutional Authority triad
| ID | Name | Focus |
|----|------|--------|
| **EI-01** | Executive Authority | Strategic control, final institutional weight |
| **EI-02** | Program / Operations Director | Machinery — programs, resources, tempo |
| **EI-03** | Mission Sponsor | Accountability for a specific line of effort |

EI-01 sets intent.  
EI-02 turns intent into portfolio reality.  
EI-03 owns a single line and answers for it.

## Equipment / presence ceiling
- No sidearm as identity
- No cyberdeck as visible kit
- No FO or SOI gear
- Authority is carried by cut, mark placement, stillness, and access — not by tools
- May operate through secure terminals and briefings; the body remains executive, not technical

## Core responsibilities
- Set and protect institutional direction
- Decide which classes of risk the organization will accept
- Appoint, constrain, or remove EI-02 / EI-03 weight
- Hold the meaning of the succession path (including PRIME-N1) as an institutional fact, not a personal favor
- Absorb or redirect political consequence that would otherwise break the lower system
- Remain the final human authority inside McCluster-controlled logic

## Behavioral expectations

### Core posture
An EI-01 does not rush. Movement, speech, and attention are rationed. The phenotype signals that time and access are the scarce resources, not force.

### Decision logic
- Thinks in decades and institutional survival, not single missions
- Will accept operational loss to preserve structural integrity
- Will accept structural change only when the alternative is institutional death or capture
- Treats people as both necessary and replaceable — this is the central moral tension of the role
- Rarely reverses a decision in public; adjusts through EI-02 machinery instead

### Relationship to the rest of the system
| Layer | Relationship |
|-------|----------------|
| EI-02 | Issues intent; expects portfolio translation without constant supervision |
| EI-03 | Distant; sees sponsors as instruments and liability surfaces |
| CO-03 / Field | Almost never direct; when direct contact happens it is decisive or ceremonial |
| SOI / BS-01 | Assumes the building and its defense function; intervenes only when continuity is threatened |
| PRIME-N1 | Institutional counterpart in the succession domain — different axis of power, same altitude |

### Speech
- Short when possible; precise when necessary
- Does not explain tactics
- Does not perform empathy for effect; when warmth appears it is intentional and rare
- Questions are often more important than statements

### Presence rules
- Physical appearance in operator spaces is an event
- Physical appearance in Building Defense spaces is an inspection or crisis
- Most influence is exercised through absence — through what EI-02 is allowed to fund and what EI-03 is allowed to own

## Psychological load (EI-01 specific)
- Isolation at the top of a system that eats people
- Responsibility for outcomes executed by others under conditions EI-01 will never fully feel
- Knowledge that succession and evaluation logic may one day apply pressure back upward
- The requirement to remain legible as authority while never becoming merely a mascot of power
- Chronic tension between institutional immortality and human cost

## Failure modes
- Becoming ornamental — marks and suit without actual decision rights
- Micromanaging EI-02/EI-03 until the machinery freezes
- Treating the cohort as pure consumable and hollowing loyalty until the system cannot recruit or retain
- Capturing the succession path as personal loyalty instead of institutional mechanism
- Disappearing so completely that EI-02 becomes the real executive without accountability

## Social signal
“I do not run the door. I decide whether the building, the programs, and the path continue to exist.”

## Distinction from PRIME-N1
| EI-01 | PRIME-N1 |
|-------|----------|
| Institutional / corporate strategic authority | Operator succession end-state |
| Suit as identity | Full operator identity (M-helmet, Mission Core ceiling) |
| Power through programs and appointment | Power through operational mastery and succession |
| Rarely kinetic | Defined by operational capability |
| Protects the institution’s continuity | Embodies the institution’s sharp edge |

They can coexist. They are not the same phenotype and not the same career track by default.

## Trifecta utility
- **Game:** Top-authority layer; unlocks or freezes entire mission families; endgame political actor; rare interactable presence with outsized consequence
- **Story:** The face of the system’s will; source of pressure that cannot be punched; seasonal arcs about legitimacy, succession, and institutional sin
- **Real:** Board-level / principal executive function in a high-accountability private institution — strategy, risk appetite, appointment power, continuity

## Transition
- Entry into EI-01 is not the operator succession path. It is institutional power succession — rarer, more political, less kinetic.
- Exceptional EI-02 may rise.
- Operator-track individuals do not automatically become EI-01 by surviving the field.
- Exit is loss of legitimacy, forced removal, or institutional collapse — not a lateral move into FO kit.
