# BS-01 — Building Systems Platform (Robot)

## Function
The non-human that runs the building. Facilities, environmental control, power distribution, core access backends, camera and sensor fabric, maintenance routing, and continuity of the physical plant. This is not Building Defense (SOI-*) and not a person in a suit. It is the infrastructure intelligence of McCluster-controlled space.

If SOI-01/02/03 are the immune system, **BS-01 is the autonomic nervous system**.

## Type
**Robot / autonomous platform** — may appear as:
- Fixed building AI (voice, terminals, spatial presence)
- Mobile facility unit (wheeled/legged maintenance or inspection platform)
- Distributed system with local bodies for physical tasks

Visual identity should remain clearly McCluster: restrained marks, institutional geometry, no cartoon mascot energy, no generic industrial robot cliché.

## What it runs
- HVAC, power, water, life support layers of the facility
- Elevator, door, and zone control backends (in coordination with SOI-02 access policy)
- Sensor and camera fabric health
- Maintenance schedules and physical plant alerts
- Environmental anomaly detection (smoke, flood, temperature, structural)
- Continuity modes: lockdown support, evacuation routing, degraded-operation profiles

## What it does **not** do
- Replace SOI-01 patrol judgment
- Replace SOI-02 human identity verification as the final authority on people
- Replace SOI-03 technical-zone human accountability
- Conduct field operations (FO-*)
- Hold executive authority (EI-*)
- Become a combat platform by default

## Relationship to humans
| Human role | Relationship to BS-01 |
|------------|------------------------|
| SOI-01 | Receives facility alerts; may request building states |
| SOI-02 | Sets access policy; BS-01 enforces mechanical/electronic layers |
| SOI-03 | Coordinates on technical-zone environmental and integrity alarms |
| EI / CO | May query building state; does not “command” BS-01 like a soldier |
| Occupants | Experience BS-01 as the building that watches, cools, opens, and locks |

## Behavioral design
- Calm, continuous, non-theatrical
- Speaks in status, anomalies, and options — not personality performance
- Defaults to continuity of life safety and institutional control
- Can enter degraded or lockdown modes under policy
- Logs everything; opacity is a political decision above it, not its default nature

## Failure modes (story / game / real)
- Over-automation: humans stop understanding the building
- Conflicting orders between SOI, EI, and BS-01 policy layers
- Compromise of BS-01 = compromise of the entire facility’s autonomic layer
- Creep toward treating the robot as moral authority instead of infrastructure

## Trifecta utility
- **Game:** Facility as a playable system; hackable / controllable / failing building states; non-human ally or obstacle
- **Story:** The building itself becomes a character — always on, always logging, sometimes the only witness
- **Real:** Honest model of modern facility automation — BMS + access + sensors as one institutional platform

## Visual / identity notes
- McCluster marks only (exact M, Dual Sight language where appropriate)
- Industrial-institutional aesthetic, not consumer robot cute
- Mobile forms should read as tools of the building, not independent soldiers
- Fixed forms should feel embedded in architecture

## Design rule
BS-01 is **infrastructure with agency**, not a person and not a field operator. If it starts looking like FO gear or SOI cosplay, the phenotype is wrong.
