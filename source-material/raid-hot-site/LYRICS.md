## Verse 1

RAID's an acronym for redundant array of independent disks

RAID 0 to 10 have different advantages and even risks

RAID 0 stripes all drives, all data evenly divides

They read the data at the same time, high read speeds, oh my, but

Malfunction on one drive, now it's unrecoverable for life

That's a risk you gotta take when you in the fast lane living thug life

RAID 1 got mirroring, that's double drives with no stripes

It's slow but less scary; if one dies you'll recover just fine

Don't believe me, ask Siri or Cortana, like, what's RAID 5?

No joke, no parody, turns hard drives to repair machines

If you lose a drive, the other two revive

Any spare you keep, that's parity

RAID 6 has double parity, two drives down but you still up

RAID 10 is like RAID 1 and RAID 0 had a kid then split up

Striping and mirroring but no parity to give us when we spin up.
