# McCluster Corp

## Story function

McCluster Corp is the apparently legitimate corporation that hires the protagonist during his first year of college. It is the audience's public entrance into the hidden Prime system.

McCluster Corp is a front owned and controlled by Apex Tactical Corp.

## Public face

McCluster Corp presents as a prestigious, culturally sophisticated technology corporation. Its recruiting materials, facilities, employees and clothing communicate opportunity, intelligence, taste and institutional power.

The company can perform real work and employ people who never discover its full purpose. A front is more durable when its public layer genuinely functions.

## Hidden function

McCluster Corp provides Apex Tactical Corp with:

- A legitimate recruiting pipeline
- Employment and academic-internship cover
- Access to technical talent
- Contract administration
- Data and candidate evaluation
- A clean corporate identity between governments and classified Apex operations
- A place to observe candidates before exposing them to the real organization

## Season One

The protagonist is formally hired by McCluster Corp, not Apex Tactical Corp.

During onboarding, he receives high-level designer corporate swag built from the exact McCluster Dual Sight monogram. Everyone inside the corporation wears branded clothing or accessories.

Because he enters from homelessness and severe financial instability, the clothing carries emotional weight. It is one of the first visible signs that an institution considers him valuable.

The brand is part of the seduction.

## Knowledge boundaries

- Ordinary employees may believe McCluster Corp is independent.
- Selected staff may know that a parent or government-contract layer exists without knowing Apex's scale.
- Cohort candidates gradually discover that McCluster Corp's hierarchy, permissions and mission exposure cannot be explained by its public business.
- The public does not automatically know that Apex Tactical Corp owns or controls McCluster Corp.
