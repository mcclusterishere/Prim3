# McCluster Corp (world entry)

> **Fiction.** This file describes McCluster Corp inside the PRIM3 story. The real McCluster Corp, its Equity Uprise brand and Matthew McCluster are different things and are never collapsed with this fiction (control repo `docs/control-plane/DOMAINS-AND-ENTITIES.md`).

## Story function

McCluster Corp is the larger holding/operating ecosystem behind **Equity Uprise**: property, brands, companies, infrastructure, logistics, technical operations and institutional partnerships (`CANON.md` §19; `story/MCCLUSTER-CORP.md`).

- McCluster does **not** begin PRIM3 by joining McCluster Corp. He joins **Equity Uprise**, the recurring workplace and training campus.
- Equity Uprise is real, not a disposable front. Its educational and community mission matters.
- McCluster Corp is governed at its hidden highest-trust layer through the PRIM architecture. PRIM2 is the presiding Mantle holder for almost all of the series.
- The exact subsidiary map is reveal-gated.

## Public face

McCluster Corp presents as a prestigious, culturally sophisticated organization. Its facilities, staff and clothing communicate opportunity, intelligence, taste and institutional power. The public layer genuinely functions: most people inside it do real work and never learn the root architecture.

## Onboarding and the brand

When McCluster joins Equity Uprise, his onboarding kit includes high-level designer pieces built from the exact McCluster Dual Sight monogram (see S1E2, where an unmanifested device in that kit is the human trigger).

Because he arrives from homelessness and severe financial instability, the clothing carries emotional weight: it is one of the first visible signs that an institution considers him valuable. Later it reads as the uniform of an architecture he cannot easily leave. The wardrobe arc is a feeling, not a recruitment scheme.

## Knowledge boundaries

- Ordinary staff know McCluster Corp as the parent ecosystem and PRIM as the **Program for Resilient Infrastructure Management** (`story/PRIM-DUAL-MEANING-AUTHORITY.md`).
- Selected staff know more of the structure without knowing the root.
- Hana Lee (before GLASSHOUSE) and later Aya suspect that the visible hierarchy does not explain the system's behaviour.
