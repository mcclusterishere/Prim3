# PRIM3 Canon Register

**Status:** Foundation canon  
**Continuity version:** 0.1.0  
**Authority:** Matthew McCluster  
**Rule:** If material conflicts with this register, this register wins until deliberately revised.

## Format

PRIM3 is a seven-season adult coming-of-age techno-thriller and infotainment series. Each season covers one academic year in the protagonist's seven-year development from first-year college student to Prime.

The series begins with a future event: Agent Prime Negative One puts on the helmet as a recruit he selected raids his facility. The story then flashes back to the protagonist's first day of college at 33.

## The protagonist

- Begins college at 33.
- Is freshly out of homelessness and at the lowest financial point of his life.
- Has years of practical IT, infrastructure and data-center experience.
- Possesses substantial knowledge but lacks the degree that institutionally validates it.
- Is recruited into an apparently innocent corporation during his first academic year.
- Advances across approximately seven years toward Ph.D.-level mastery.
- His defining advantage is tenacity: he continues after every rational reason to stop.
- He reaches the succession confrontation at approximately 40.
- He ultimately becomes **Agent Prime Negative One**.

His civilian name is not yet canon.

## The presiding Prime

- Was recruited at 33.
- Located, defeated and executed the prior Prime in less than nine months, before his 34th birthday.
- Overrode an architecture that ordinarily prohibited anyone under 40 from becoming Prime.
- Is a technological savant and the greatest living operator of the corporation.
- Is revered and feared throughout the organization.
- Has occupied the position for seven years when the protagonist is recruited and is approximately 40.
- Will be approximately 47 at the end of the protagonist's seven-year ascent.
- Secretly wants a worthy successor to end his life and relieve him of the role.
- Cannot surrender the role to an inferior operator.
- Genuinely resists every succession attempt.
- His civilian name is not yet canon.

## Succession law

1. Prime cannot resign.
2. Prime cannot appoint a successor directly.
3. The successor must locate, reach and execute the presiding Prime.
4. The presiding Prime must genuinely resist.
5. A candidate incapable of defeating Prime is incapable of safely inheriting Prime's authority.
6. The age floor is ordinarily 40, but the presiding Prime proved the architecture can be overridden through extraordinary capability.
7. Prime is the most powerful position on Earth and the last position a sane person should want.

## Recruitment and cohorts

- The presiding Prime selects exceptional talent every academic year.
- Candidates are recruited through education, internships and apparently legitimate corporate pathways.
- Cohorts generally contain approximately 10–15 people.
- Not every recruit initially knows that Prime or the succession process exists.
- New cohorts overlap with surviving members of previous cohorts.
- Candidates train, study, work and undertake missions together.
- Attrition happens primarily on legitimate missions with extremely low survival probabilities.
- Prime accompanies teams on these missions.
- Prime does not casually murder recruits; he survives conditions other exceptional operators cannot.
- A mission may return one, two or three people from a team of 10–15.
- Characters may survive across multiple seasons before dying.

## Dramatic contrast

The presiding Prime is a savant who mastered the system in nine months.

Prime Negative One is a survivor who takes seven years and cannot be permanently removed from the path.

The final succession is genius versus tenacity, speed versus endurance, and immediate mastery versus relentless growth.

## Site 0

Site 0 is the end-state of the journey—not the opening status quo. It eventually becomes Prime Negative One's command environment, responsibility and prison.

The canonical nine-level stack remains:

- L4 — Flight Control / Penthouse
- L3 — Flight Deck
- L2 — Administration / Aviation Security
- L1 — Ground Operations / OPERATE
- B1 — Open Technology Research Vault / LEARN
- B2 — Fabrication / BUILD
- B3 — Prime Command & Sustainment / COMMAND–LIVE–PRESERVE
- B4 — Transit Nexus / MOVE
- B5 — Supreme Habitat & Coordination Node / SUPREME COMMAND

## Prime visual anchors

- Exact M-shaped face architecture; never a pasted badge
- Matte-black helmet shell
- Warm ivory/chrome M edging
- Dark smoked visor
- Red `//` Dual Sight bars
- Visible forehead camera with real optical glass
- Rear Jetson-class Mission Core compute bay
- Camera/peace and weapon/war dual interpretation

## Music continuity

- The connected music project is **Run_Prim3**.
- **High Alert** is positioned as a final activation or epilogue.
- The fluid-filled holding/recovery-tank imagery belongs near the far end of the chronology.
- Music communicates the internal experience while the series dramatizes external events.
