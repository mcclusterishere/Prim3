# Prime Negative One — Before Prime

## Starting position

At 33, he enters college as a first-year student.

He is freshly out of homelessness, broke and at the lowest material point of his life. He has years of practical IT, infrastructure and data-center work behind him, but no completed degree to certify what he knows.

His classmates see an older freshman.

The audience has already seen the future Prime.

## Core dramatic need

He wants the world to officially recognize knowledge he has already earned through labor and survival.

He believes a degree will produce stability, legitimacy and control over his future. The corporation offers all three—and slowly transforms them into a trap.

## Defining advantage

Tenacity.

He is not necessarily the fastest learner or most immediately gifted candidate. He survives embarrassment, poverty, institutional rejection, physical danger and repeated failure without permanently leaving the path.

## Seven-year transformation

- Practical technician → rigorous scholar
- Older freshman → cohort leader
- Employee → investigator
- Candidate → operator
- Survivor → executioner
- Man seeking recognition → Prime Negative One

## Central danger

The system may teach him to interpret friends as resources, risks or possible successors. His struggle is to gain the capacity required to become Prime without surrendering the human relationships that made survival meaningful.

## Season One state

He does not know:

- That Prime exists
- That the corporation recruits annual cohorts
- That missions form a succession filter
- That the presiding Prime selected him personally
- That his homelessness was interpreted as evidence of resilience
- That his academic journey is already part of an operational evaluation

## Later visual canon

Prime Negative One is exactly six feet tall. His approved full-body Prime form uses:

- Tall, athletic adult proportions
- The exact M as face architecture
- Matte-black segmented composite armor over a flexible technical under-suit
- Warm ivory/chrome helmet and upper-torso M structure
- Smoked visor
- Red `//` Dual Sight bars
- Visible forehead camera
- Rear Jetson-class Mission Core
- Modular forearm interfaces
- Restrained red system illumination
- Rugged serviceable joints, gloves and boots
