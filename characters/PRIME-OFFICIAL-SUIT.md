# Official Prim3 Suit — Character Sheet

**This is the official suit for the position of Prime.**

The multi-view character sheet (front 3/4, front orthographic, side, rear, helmet close-up, Mission Core close-up) is locked as visual canon for the completed Prime identity / Agent Prime Negative One's operational suit.

## Locked visual details (from official character sheet)

- **Helmet**: Matte black shell with exact M-shaped face architecture (never a pasted badge). Warm ivory/chrome M edging. Dark smoked/black visor. Prominent red `//` Dual Sight bars integrated into the lower M. Visible forehead optical camera with real glass lens. Side ear/helmet modules with red accent lights.
- **Torso**: Matte black armored plates over textured under-suit. Large white/ivory V-shaped or M-derived chest plate. Red `//` Dual Sight vertical bars on right chest. Articulated segmented armor on shoulders, arms, and abdomen.
- **Arms & Hands**: Fully armored with black plates, red accent indicators, forearm computer/display module on left arm, black tactical gloves.
- **Legs**: Heavy segmented black knee and shin armor, reinforced boots.
- **Back / Mission Core**: Jetson-class Mission Core compute bay mounted on upper back. Rectangular black unit with dual vertical red light bars (`//`), cabling, and "P.I.O" labeling. Shoulder armor with white accents.
- **Overall**: Tactical operator aesthetic — matte black primary, ivory/chrome secondary accents, red functional lighting for Dual Sight and status. Full-body coverage, practical for operations, high-detail hard-surface armor over flexible undersuit.
