# McCluster Corp Security Officer style sheet

## SOI-01 — Authority Black

![McCluster Corp Security Officer Identity SOI-01](../../assets/canon/brands/mccluster-corp/security-officer-identity/MCC-SOI-01-authority-black-v01.jpg)

## Role distinction

This is a McCluster Corp security officer—not a cohort operator, police officer, soldier, or SWAT unit. The officer operates inside and around corporate facilities with a cyber-first mission and a minimal defensive load.

## Five-part system

| Component | Function | Identity behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate foundation | Maintains professional institutional appearance | Authority-black tailoring |
| Ultra-thin protective vest | Essential torso protection without restricting movement | Small exact M; no external pouch field |
| Body camera | Records officer activity and facility incidents | High-chest clip mount; camera lens may echo Dual Sight |
| McCluster cyberdeck | Secure field computing and systems access | Exact M lid inlay; controlled Dual Sight interface |
| Sidearm and holster | Last-resort defensive capability | Exactly one secured sidearm; restrained branded holster |

## Absolute exclusions

No accessory may be added merely to make the officer appear more tactical. The standard kit excludes watches, wrist computers, earpieces, cable wallets, visible drives, loose cables, gloves, mystery modules, extra pouches, spare magazines, rifles, long guns, helmets, masks, camouflage, MOLLE, knee pads, and riot equipment.

## Identity source rule

The render establishes fit, role, equipment ceiling, and placement logic. Production artwork must use the locked masters in `assets/canon/brands/mccluster-corp/marks/`. Omit a mark rather than approximate it.
