# McCluster Corp brand canon

## Brand position

McCluster Corp must look like an original global luxury house fused with an elite technology corporation. The wardrobe is not disposable promotional merchandise. It is tailored, expensive, desirable and immediately recognizable.

**North star: wealth, not riches.** McCluster is the private-client collection the public never sees. The reference to Gucci-type presence describes cultural confidence, construction quality, and house-level consistency—not permission to copy another fashion house.

The controlling clothing document is [`PRIVATE-CLIENT-FASHION-GUIDE.md`](PRIVATE-CLIENT-FASHION-GUIDE.md). The active Season One visual direction is [`CORPORATE-WEAR-STYLE-SHEET.md`](CORPORATE-WEAR-STYLE-SHEET.md).

## Locked symbol system

### M emblem — the House

The exact M emblem is the primary McCluster house mark. It must not be redrawn, simplified into a generic letter, traced from a render, or generated by an image model.

### Dual Sight

Dual Sight carries two readings:

- Peace: camera focus, observation and recording light
- War: weapon optic, target acquisition and red dot

### Two States

The red `//` glyph represents peace and wartime states. It is a secondary identity mark and system-state signal.

### Fidelity rule

Only the master assets in `assets/canon/brands/mccluster-corp/marks/` may supply individual marks. The 8-inch seamless SVG controls repeat geometry. **If an exact master cannot be placed, leave the location blank.** Approximation is prohibited.

## Corporate wardrobe rules

- Every McCluster Corp employee wears at least one branded garment or accessory while operating in corporate environments; the identifier may be private, tonal, or internal.
- Clothing must read as private-issue luxury: excellent materials, controlled tailoring, deliberate hardware, and disciplined identity placement.
- Avoid generic logo merchandise, random tactical camouflage, unrelated flower motifs, streetwear/drop language, and decorative filler.
- Do not imitate or remix another real fashion house's protected monogram.
- Negative space is part of the brand. The default office garment contains zero to three identity events total.
- Season One office trousers and tailored shorts remain visually solid black. They do not use visible all-over monogram.
- Trouser branding is limited to near-invisible black-on-black embroidery and one tiny red `//` bartack at a pocket or belt loop.
- Office shirts use one small exact chest M, an optional tonal-black exact Dual Sight on a sleeve or cuff, and a tiny exact red `//` accent.
- Full monogram repeat belongs on controlled statement outerwear, linings, accessories, and special pieces—not across an entire matching shirt-and-trouser uniform.
- A complete employee outfit must never read as pajamas, a tracksuit, a costume, a clown suit, or public promotional merch.
- The exact first-season onboarding package remains to be designed one item at a time.
- Apex Tactical Corp branding is not interchangeable with McCluster Corp branding.

## Narrative function

Season One uses the wardrobe as a visual and emotional progression:

1. **Recognition** — the protagonist receives clothing he could not presently afford.
2. **Belonging** — the pattern identifies him as part of an elite institution.
3. **Access** — employees read garment tier, issue, and placement as internal culture.
4. **Control** — the same system eventually becomes inseparable from the organization recruiting and evaluating him.

## Production rules

The 8-inch seamless master is authoritative for repeat geometry.

- Repeat: 8 x 8 inches / 203.2 x 203.2 mm
- Three visual colors; black may be substrate
- Print master may retain ivory hatching
- Vinyl/laser/small-format production requires cut-safe solid paths, not the hatched master
- Embroidery uses red fill first, ivory linework second; black remains substrate
- Test sew-outs, print strike-offs, and physical swatch approvals are mandatory
- Do not resize embroidery machine files beyond plus or minus 10 percent
- AI renders may establish garment concept only. Official identity layers must come from the exact vector masters.

See the supplied production guide and Private Client Fashion Guide for application-specific requirements.
