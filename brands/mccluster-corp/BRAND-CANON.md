# McCluster Corp brand canon

## Brand position

McCluster Corp must look like an original global luxury house fused with an elite technology corporation. The wardrobe is not disposable promotional merchandise. It is tailored, expensive, desirable and immediately recognizable.

The reference to Gucci-type presence describes the level of cultural saturation and construction quality—not permission to copy another fashion house.

## Locked symbol system

### M emblem — the House

The exact M emblem is the primary McCluster house mark. It must not be redrawn, simplified into a generic letter or used as a pasted substitute for unrelated geometry.

### Dual Sight

Dual Sight carries two readings:

- Peace: camera focus, observation and recording light
- War: weapon optic, target acquisition and red dot

### Two States

The red `//` glyph represents peace and wartime states. It is a secondary identity mark and system-state signal.

## Locked colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Story use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | `#080A0C` | 8 / 10 / 12 | Substrate, authority, concealment |
| Ivory | `#ECE6D8` | 236 / 230 / 216 | House linework, precision, prestige |
| Red | `#BC1B2A` | 188 / 27 / 42 | Two States, recording/target state, danger |

Physical swatches control production. Screen values are starting points.

## Corporate wardrobe rules

- Every McCluster Corp employee wears at least one visible branded garment or accessory while operating in corporate environments.
- The monogram may be used as an all-over pattern, engineered panel, lining, trim or singular house mark.
- Clothing must read as luxury-house-grade: excellent materials, controlled tailoring, deliberate hardware and disciplined pattern registration.
- Avoid generic logo-print merchandise, random tactical camouflage and unrelated flower motifs.
- Do not imitate or remix another real fashion house's protected monogram.
- Dense monogram should be balanced by black negative space.
- The exact first-season onboarding package remains to be designed one item at a time.
- Apex Tactical Corp branding is not interchangeable with McCluster Corp branding.

## Narrative function

Season One uses the swag as a visual and emotional progression:

1. **Recognition** — the protagonist receives clothing he could not presently afford.
2. **Belonging** — the pattern identifies him as part of an elite institution.
3. **Access** — employees read garment tier, issue or placement as internal culture.
4. **Control** — the same pattern eventually becomes inseparable from the system recruiting and evaluating him.

## Production rules

The 8-inch seamless master is authoritative for repeat geometry.

- Repeat: 8 x 8 inches / 203.2 x 203.2 mm
- Three visual colors; black may be substrate
- Print master may retain ivory hatching
- Vinyl/laser/small-format production requires cut-safe solid paths, not the hatched master
- Embroidery uses red fill first, ivory linework second; black remains substrate
- Test sew-outs, print strike-offs and physical swatch approvals are mandatory
- Do not resize embroidery machine files beyond plus or minus 10 percent

See the supplied production guide for application-specific requirements.
